# Source adjudication: T1E triple-oracle anchor

## Decision

T1E is qualified. It provides duplicate evidence for accepted `C-SG-002`, one new exact threshold-deficit consequence promoted as `C-SG-005`, and a guard consistent with `C-SG-004`. It does not establish a physical identity among the named programs or derive the cross-program lift.

## Oracle audit

O1 defines `E_breather=16*sqrt(1-omega^2)` and `Delta=16*(1-sqrt(1-omega^2))`, then proves `E_breather+Delta=16` by ring algebra. The partition is exact and now belongs to `C-SG-005`; Lean's encoding does not independently derive either the Hamiltonian energy or interaction physics. The threshold-deficit name avoids treating the defined complement as a separately derived binding potential.

O2.1–O2.3 reproduce the `t=0` Hamiltonian integral. P001 already derives the same energy from the full accepted breather and independently reconstructs it; T1E is duplicate evidence for `C-SG-002`, not a third accepted claim. The coefficient and square-root power mutations are useful regression guards but do not multiply the number of independent derivations.

O2.4 sets `E_adim=E0`, defines `E_shadow=E_adim*N^-1/2`, and compares it with `E_vantasner=E0/sqrt(N)`. Once those premises are inserted, equality is algebraic identity. No checked equation derives either program's lift, identifies its degrees of freedom, or shows that the two physical objects are the same.

O3 evaluates the accepted formula at `omega=1/sqrt(2)`. The exact result `8*sqrt(2)` and its high-precision decimal are fixed by substitution. This is regression coverage, not an independent numerical oracle for the functional claim.

The distinct-object guard correctly separates `C-SG-002` energy from the formula accepted in `C-SG-004`. T1E calls the latter a gradient-only action, whereas the canonical framework precisely records it as the period-averaged squared-gradient integral; the source name is not promoted.

## Exact qualification

Accepted mappings are `C-SG-002`, `C-SG-004`, and `C-SG-005`. The triple-oracle consistency narrative, program-level object identity, lift derivation, and “binding” interaction interpretation remain unaccepted. Their absence does not negate the exact threshold deficit; it limits the source unit to what its checks actually establish.
