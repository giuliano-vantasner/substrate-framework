# WN3 Source Adjudication

WN3 should be qualified rather than accepted literally. C-SG-019 already owns
the exact activity and coordinate-scale dependence of the zero-background
H-linear cosine coefficient. C-CMB-001 owns its inverse-square factorial
suppression context. C-OSC-001 newly owns the exact algebraic one-mode Fock
ladder, repeated-creation norm, finite truncation defect, low-coordinate power
element, and declared factorial-one mass families.

WN3.1 survives as exact classical scale dependence with C-SG-019's physical
ceiling. Rescaling a formal coordinate while leaving its kinetic and canonical
normalization unspecified is enough to show the source has no rate, but it is
not a physical comparison between otherwise fixed systems.

WN3.2's finite D=12 construction is correct below the top edge, and its norms
through order eight are correct. The canonical theorem strengthens it: on the
algebraic infinite Fock domain `[a,a_dagger]=I` and
`(a_dagger)^n|0>=sqrt(n!)|n>`, while the finite commutator is exactly
`I-12|11><11|`. The trace obstruction forbids a full identity commutator for
any finite matrices. The factorial is a ladder normalization or contraction
factor, not n! distinct final states or a density of states by itself.

WN3.3 survives only as a conditional low-sector element. With
`Q=low_scale*(a+a_dagger)`, the exact element
`<n|Q^n|0>=low_scale^n*sqrt(n!)` gives the H-linear coefficient square
proportional to `S^n/n!` on positive odd n. A high-sector operator/state
element, interaction normalization, energy rule, final-state measure, spectral
density, units, and parameter provenance remain missing. Calling the square a
Fermi-Golden-Rule rate is rejected.

The source's parity transition is also rejected. Its composition loop contains
only odd n because the cosine coefficient vanishes at even n. Multiplication
by `sqrt(n!)` cannot turn zero into a positive value. WN3.5 silently replaces
that family by `S^n/n!` on every positive integer and then declares even
orders open. This is a different declared sample space or process, not a
derived consequence.

The distinction changes normalization and modes. Positive odd support has
total `sinh(S)`; positive support has `exp(S)-1`; adding the vacuum gives
`exp(S)`. At source sample S=25, the all-positive family has tied modes 24 and
25, while the positive-odd composition has unique mode 25. The source's first-
argmax grid hides the ordinary tie. WN2's positivity-and-mode guard remains an
unaccepted universal physical criterion.

All five dependencies and ten unique reverse consumers replay in a sixteen-
node, 637-check graph with no compatibility stops. WN4 through WN7 and MD1
through MD6 remain individually pending. WN3 should map C-SG-019, C-CMB-001,
and C-OSC-001; it promotes no density of states, Fermi-Golden-Rule rate,
reopened even channel, admissibility law, physical Poisson process, medium
parameter, or material prediction.
