# E2 source adjudication

E2 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Its displayed generalized radial
functional and Euler–Lagrange equation survive independent exact derivation,
and its three selected stationary branches retain a decreasing conditional
energy per declared degree after replacing the angular inputs, finite walls,
and numerical oracle. The surviving result is C-RPROF-001/002.

The source execution itself is noncanonical evidence. It recomputes E1 with
endpoint-excluding midpoint arrays reduced by trapezoids, consumes biased
`I(1)`, `I(2)`, and `I(4)`, imposes exact vacuum values at finite walls,
returns a `solve_bvp` result without checking success or residuals, omits
endpoint energy estimates, and changes domain, mesh, and tolerance together.
Its current `np.trapezoid` branch executes successfully, so no version-only
compatibility failure is counted as scientific evidence or debt.

P105 independently varies the functional, derives the endpoint powers and
scale identity, uses accepted angular values, evolves the small regular-origin
signal through the vacuum-complement variable, checks asymptotic Robin data,
and isolates quadrature, cutoff, domain, tolerance, and maximum-step changes.
A fresh collocation implementation with a different initial construction and
Simpson quadrature agrees. Failed single-shooting and quantity-mismatch oracles
remain in attempts 0002–0004.

The literal checks are reviewed individually. The B=1 compatibility, corrected
stationary branches, and selected conditional ordering survive. Literature
bands are not selection or accuracy oracles. The combined refinement is
replaced. `I=B` and `I=B²` are distinct mutations: both change the energies
materially, yet both preserve the selected ordering. Thus the source guard
shows input sensitivity only and contradicts its own prose that the hierarchy
would be destroyed.

Nothing in E2 proves existence or uniqueness on the half-line, a local or
global minimum, a full three-dimensional field solution, or a variational
upper-bound theorem. Rational degree is not thereby a physical baryon number;
the words nucleon, deuteron, alpha, nucleus, binding, reaction, release, and
yield require separate accepted action, state, scale, and observation maps.
E3, E4, E5, KI5, TX1–TX5, MK5, MR5, and narrative consumers remain pending and
may use only the explicit conditional surfaces.

Decision: qualify E2 through C-RPROF-001 (`symbolic_verified`) and
C-RPROF-002 (`numeric_evidence`); reject all broader physical readings.
