# P072 Source Adjudication: MH3

MH3 executes successfully, but its exact reduction contradicts the stated
width-driven mechanism.

## Retained Mathematical Content

A declared real odd dressing of an otherwise even sech multiplier yields a
real symmetric two-mode compression with a cross-parity entry proportional to
the dressing amplitude. Identical nondegenerate real symmetric matrices admit
the same eigenbasis, and two declared eigenbases have an automatically unitary
relative product in the C-MIX-001 column convention.

## Rejected Interpretations

The source matrix simplifies to `pi*A/16` times
`[[4,sqrt(3)*b],[sqrt(3)*b,3]]`; every `kappa` cancels. Its two matrices differ
because it independently changes `b` from `1/3` to `1/4`, not because widths
are `1/2` and `2/3`. That odd dressing is a new declared profile and is absent
from C-QBL-001's accepted even background. The source also replaces
C-QBL-003's actual even `sech^2` mode by `sech`.

The imported diagonalizer returns row transforms, while the relative-product
formula is written as though they were column bases; C-MIX-001 already records
this convention defect. Regardless of orientation, unitarity is automatic,
and nonidentity depends on ordering, phases, degeneracies, and a common-space
identification. No accepted field, interaction, sector, current, generation,
or mass map makes that matrix CKM or its angle Cabibbo. Unequal singular values
are an order-one mathematical splitting, not MH2's hierarchy.

## Continuation Decision

Candidate A is retained only for the declared asymmetric compression and
equal-matrix guard. P072 continues with Candidates B-F. The accepted framework
is not retrofitted with physical flavor structure.
