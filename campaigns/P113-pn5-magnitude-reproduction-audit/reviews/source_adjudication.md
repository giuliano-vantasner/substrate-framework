# PN5 source adjudication

PN5 reproduces eleven runtime predicates from eleven static check sites at the
pinned source hash. It imports NumPy for a finite uniform argmax grid but uses
no sampled integration, so no `np.trapz` compatibility event occurs.

The seven declared input divisions close exactly. Their counts are
24,000,000,000; 8,000,000,000; 2,400,000,000; 800,000,000; 240,000,000;
80,000,000; and 24,000,000. Thus the exact selected range is 24 million through
24 billion, not the loose 10 million through 100 billion envelope. Thirty meV
does give exactly 800 million for the supplied rounded 24 MeV total.

All energies are external inputs. Common rescaling preserves every quotient,
and choosing `unit=total/n` manufactures any positive target integer `n`.
Exact inverse plateaus have a strict lower and closed upper endpoint. Binary
floating floor can fail there: exact decimal 0.3/0.1 gives three while the
binary-float expression floors to two. Selected-input containment in a named
decade is therefore bookkeeping, not prediction or mechanism.

The pair magnitude is exactly C-RES-001 with coupling product `g^2`. Its unique
positive optimum is `Gamma=2*abs(Delta)`, its peak is
`g^2/abs(Delta)`, and both loss limits vanish. PN5's numeric grid is regression
coverage only; its 0.002 optimum tolerance admits nearby wrong values, and its
endpoint checks use two finite samples rather than limits. The expression has
matrix-element dimensions under physical energy conventions, not inverse-time
rate dimensions.

The literature audit finds neither an explicit retraction nor PN5's bare floor
subdivision formula in arXiv:2501.08338v2. Its more precise transition-energy
surface is an external input, and its mode values occur inside a much richer
Hamiltonian, state, density, and rate model that P113 does not import.

PN5 is therefore qualified with no new claim, release, or package API. P110's
immutable adjudication already governs the exact quotient evidence, while
C-RES-001 already governs the finite pair theorem. Neither establishes a
quantum subdivision process, predicted magnitude, bath, physical channel,
probability, rate, yield, material scale, or observation. Qualified PN4 and
pending PN6 are direct source consumers; twenty-two additional consumers are
pinned transitively. The PN5-PN4 candidate cycle grants no authority.
