# P092 source adjudication

LB2 is qualified. Its exact abstract damped-harmonic-oscillator algebra
survives, but its nonlinear-breather, coherence-survival, and discharge
conclusions do not follow from that algebra.

For the declared equation

`q_tt + Gamma*q_t + omega_0^2*q = 0`,

the characteristic roots are
`-Gamma/2 +/- sqrt(Gamma^2-4*omega_0^2)/2`. The motion is underdamped,
critical, or overdamped according as `Gamma` is below, equal to, or above
`2*omega_0`. In the underdamped branch its actual angular frequency is
`omega_d=sqrt(omega_0^2-Gamma^2/4)`.

LB2 does not derive that equation with a finite-amplitude breather frequency as
its natural frequency. The accepted normalized small-amplitude sine-Gordon
equation instead gives each real spatial Fourier mode
`omega_0(k)=sqrt(1+k^2)>=1`. A breather frequency satisfies `0<omega_b<1` and
is not a real linear-mode natural frequency. The exact values `k=0`,
`Gamma=6/5`, and `omega_b=1/2` reverse the source classification: the actual
field mode is underdamped, while substitution of `omega_b` says overdamped.
Only the limiting band-edge statement `omega_b -> 1` can approach the `k=0`
mode, and it does not globalize to arbitrary finite amplitude.

The cycle headline also conflates observables. An underdamped coordinate has
amplitude envelope `exp(-Gamma*t/2)` and quadratic envelope
`exp(-Gamma*t)`, while its instantaneous mechanical energy obeys
`dE/dt=-Gamma*q_t^2`, not `-Gamma*E`. In a declared `1/Gamma` quadratic-envelope
window, actual cycles number `omega_d/(2*pi*Gamma)`. LB2's
`omega_0/(2*pi*Gamma)` is a nominal natural-frequency convention, accurate
only in weak damping. At critical damping the actual count tends to zero, but
the source count remains `1/(4*pi)`.

There are two incompatible meanings of “coherent breather,” neither supporting
LB2's headline. If it means an exactly time-periodic finite-energy field, the
accepted energy balance proves that positive uniform damping and vanishing
boundary flux exclude every nontrivial such solution for any `Gamma>0`, not
only after a `2*omega_b` boundary. If it means finite-time transient ringing,
LB2 supplies no observation window, nonlinear classifier, phase observable,
survival probability, or numerical oracle. Underdamped response, exact
periodicity, phase coherence, survival fraction, and population are different
predicates.

The source's decreasing positive number and rejection of one arbitrary
constant floor do not derive a probability or selector. No accepted map
connects normalized `Gamma`, `omega_b`, or `k` to material collision, density,
spark, DBD, or population variables. LB3, LB4, lifetime, and DBD consumers
inherit rather than close those premises.

P092 therefore promotes only `C-DYN-001`: the exact oscillator classification,
observable-specific envelope and energy semantics, the normalized
sine-Gordon-mode mapping, the sub-gap countermodel, and the positive-damping
periodicity obstruction. LB2 remains durable qualified evidence, and all
physical threshold and survival readings remain excluded.
