# P092 impact analysis

P092 adds a new canonical module and claim identifier; it changes no accepted
symbol or existing API. Direct governed consumers are the new focused tests,
primary verifier, independent review, claim registry entry, release manifest,
generated documentation, and synchronized memory.

Accepted dependencies are `C-SG-011` for the normalized gap-one linearization
and `C-SG-012` for the exact damped energy balance. Their statements and source
modules remain unchanged. `C-SG-016` remains a qualified adiabatic nonlinear
family law and is not used to assert a global oscillator reduction.

Hash-pinned LB3 and LB4 and the lifetime and DBD engineering modules are direct
narrative consumers of LB2, but none is accepted authority. P092 does not edit
them. The consumer audit records that their survival and discharge conclusions
remain pending or noncanonical. The separately named barrier-scaling `N_coh`
is a population threshold and does not consume the oscillator cycle API.

Replay scope is therefore the new module tests, sine-Gordon tests supporting
the two dependencies, P092's two verifiers, repository governance validation,
generated-document freshness, and the one unchanged integrated promotion gate.
No PDE replay is required because C-DYN-001 makes only exact symbolic claims and
explicitly rejects promotion of a finite-time nonlinear survival classifier.

Exact P092 code performs no numerical integration and introduces neither
`np.trapz` nor another version-specific NumPy integration alias.
