# CM2 Source Adjudication

CM2 is qualified. Its 21 checks reproduce, and several individual factor
identities survive under existing accepted premises, but the assembled object
is not a coherent-medium nuclear rate and its open support is not a positive-
loss sweet spot.

The exact source specialization has three symmetric detuning pairs with equal
positive coupling product. C-RES-001 gives
`K(Gamma)=Gamma*sum_j c_j/(Delta_j^2+Gamma^2/4)`. Multiplication by CM2's
nominal `omega/(2*pi*Gamma)` factor cancels Gamma and gives a strictly
decreasing positive rational sum. Its right limit at zero is finite and
positive, while CM2 assigns zero at zero. Its left limit at `2*omega` is also
positive, while CM2 again assigns zero. The piecewise function therefore has
two jumps, a nonattained supremum at zero loss, and no maximizer. The 41-point
grid merely selects its smallest positive node, and refinement moves that node
toward the excluded endpoint.

C-DYN-001's actual underdamped cycle count replaces omega by the damped
frequency. That product is also strictly decreasing and tends continuously to
zero at critical damping. It remains a count in a declared envelope window,
not phase coherence or a nonlinear breather-existence theorem.

The remaining factors do not close a rate. PN2 has no accepted physical
subdivision claim and CM2's numeric route simply sets n to one. C-SPN-002
explicitly withholds a rate interpretation and retains state, rung, coupling,
and operator normalization. The finite resolvent is a matrix element, while
the activation and thermal gates and count multipliers are dimensionless.
Zero interaction or final-state density gives zero physical rate; a free
kinetic prefactor fits any positive target. B1 is pending and unused, and the
H5b Boolean marker supplies no normalization.

C-CMP-001 is distinct because it governs the exact cross-factor cancellation,
endpoint extensions, monotonicity, actual-cycle alternative, and dimensional
ceiling not stated by either C-RES-001 or C-DYN-001 alone. Promotion must retain
only this conditional theorem. It establishes no nuclear or phonon state,
interaction, bath, coherence observable, probability, rate, yield, heat,
material realization, or observation.
