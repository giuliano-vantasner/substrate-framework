# W7 source adjudication

W7 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Native execution exits cleanly with
all eleven source checks passing and no NumPy integration compatibility event.
That tally reproduces several matrix identities but does not validate the
finite connection law, projected carrier, action, current, or weak-sector
headline.

W7 declares `D=partial-i*g*W`, `psi'=U*psi`, and
`W'=U*W*U_dagger+(i/g)*(partial U)*U_dagger`. Direct product-rule substitution
leaves an uncancelled derivative of `U`. The covariant law for this convention
has the opposite inhomogeneous sign:
`W'=U*W*U_dagger-(i/g)*(partial U)*U_dagger`. W7 CHECK1 tests only the
homogeneous infinitesimal commutator and imports the derivative shift in prose.

Its local curvature calculation on an unprojected declared doublet is exact:
`F_mu_nu=partial_mu W_nu-partial_nu W_mu-i*g*[W_mu,W_nu]` and
`[D_mu,D_nu]=-i*g*F_mu_nu`. The corrected finite law makes this curvature
transform by conjugation, and cyclic trace makes `tr(F_mu_nu^2)` invariant.
Noncommuting constant connections show why deleting the commutator is a
load-bearing failure. These are conditional gauge-algebra statements, not a
derived action or physical theory.

W7's `T_a*P_L` matrices put isospin and chirality on one two-dimensional
carrier. Two are non-Hermitian and all three cyclic SU2 commutators fail. The
accepted C-REP-002 construction is `T_a tensor P`. It is Hermitian, closes,
annihilates the complementary block, and embeds a local SU2 transformation as
identity there. Parity maps this left construction to a distinct right one;
left alone is not a parity-odd eigenobject.

The charged-basis regrouping and fundamental ladder elements are exact. They
construct no matter field, bilinear current, interaction action, or physical
boson. W7 assigns charges minus one and plus one, whose difference is two,
while the ladder changes T3 by one; its unit event charge is inserted
separately. CHECK8 expands only one selected curvature component and supplies

and P150 rejected W5's piston provenance for `Z=lambda^2/mu`.
