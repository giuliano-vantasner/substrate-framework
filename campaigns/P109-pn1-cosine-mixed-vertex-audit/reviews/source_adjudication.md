# PN1 source adjudication

PN1 is qualified. Its executable classical calculus is correct in its declared
unit-normalized zero-background coordinates: positive odd total degree
vanishes, positive even total degree has the alternating binomial coefficient,
the one-high specialization is nonzero at odd low order with coefficient
`(-1)^((n-1)/2)/n!`, and a finite degree-eight reconstruction has a nonzero
later remainder. P109 promotes the wider exact result as C-SG-019, with
amplitude, background, coordinate scales, coefficient-versus-derivative
factorials, convergence, and interpretation ceilings explicit.

The source's physical headline does not follow. The names `phi_H` and `phi_L`
do not define normalized frequency modes. The words nuclear, breather, phonon,
quantum, multiplicity, medium, coupling, and energy transfer appear only as
labels; they supply no kinetic terms, canonical normalization, operator
expansion, states, occupation factors, spatial overlap, kinematics, resonance,
linewidth, perturbative regime, rate rule, or material map. A nonzero local
classical polynomial coefficient is therefore not a quantum matrix element or
a transition.

The infinite formal subsequence also does not establish accessible unbounded
multiplicity. In unit coordinates its nonzero magnitudes are `1/n!` and tend to
zero. Coordinate rescaling changes the order-`n` one-high coefficient by the
`n`th power of the low-coordinate scale, and changing the expansion background
can exchange positive-order parity support. These are load-bearing conventions,
not optional annotations.

All 32 runtime checks and thirteen static check sites are covered by the
predicate ledger. C-BRK-001 remains the earlier univariate sixth-order theorem;
C-SG-019 adds a nonduplicative all-order mixed surface. FS1, NC1, and NC2 supply
no missing quantum premise. Later PN, WN, and MD consumers may reuse the exact
coefficient only with its declared conventions and must independently establish
any quantum or physical interpretation.
