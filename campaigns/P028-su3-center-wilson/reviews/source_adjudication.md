# Source adjudication: CF3 center and Wilson laws

## Decision

CF3 is qualified. Its exact SU(3) center and abstract triality algebra support
`C-LIE-002`. Its large-time substitutions support conditional `C-WIL-001`, but
CF3 derives neither the Wilson expectation nor a physical confinement phase.

## Check-family audit

CF3.1 exhibits three scalar matrices, checks determinant, commutation, and
closure, but exhibiting central elements alone does not prove they are the
full center. P028 closes that gap by solving the exact commutant of all eight
accepted fundamental generators. It is one-dimensional and scalar; unitarity
and determinant one then restrict the scalar to the three cube roots.

CF3.2 correctly computes the fundamental phase and trivial center conjugation
on the adjoint matrix representation. Abstract trialities add modulo three, so
tensoring with triality zero cannot change a nonzero triality. The source's
quark, gluon, screening, and isolated-particle readings require a separately
accepted physical representation map and state-space argument; none exists.

CF3.3 substitutes the declared expression
`W(R,T)=exp(-sigma*R*T)` into the declared extraction formula and exactly gets
`V(R)=sigma*R`. The source itself calls the Wilson construction and area-law
criterion imported and calls the rectangular exponential model declared. The
substitution is a valid conditional consequence, not a derivation of an area
law, positive physical tension, or confinement.

CF3.4 checks only the orders and cyclic structure for the cited SU(2) case and
the constructed SU(3) case. It does not derive a general SU(N) theorem or show
that a center group selects a phase. The same exact SU(3) center can be paired
with either of CF3's declared loop laws.

CF3.5 correctly derives `V=2*rho` from the declared perimeter law. P028 retains
it as a countermodel: its separation derivative vanishes while all center
algebra is unchanged. CF3.6's determinant guard is exact and is strengthened
by mutations of both phase and alleged group order.

## Exact qualification

Accepted content is limited to the complete center/triality theorem and the
premise-explicit consequences of the two rectangular loop ansatzes. Wilson-law
selection, nonperturbative gauge measure, physical sector assignments,
screening dynamics, string-tension existence, and confinement remain outside
the claim delta.
