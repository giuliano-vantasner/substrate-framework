## Semantic and scientific scope

MK1, MK2, and MK3 are tracked semantic counterevidence to the words “absent”
and “nor derives any quantity.” They remain pending source candidates with no
accepted claims. Their presence refutes KI1's repository statement; it does
not establish their formulas, select a physical coupling, or challenge the
accepted BPS claims.

Conversely, absence from a finite repository snapshot could never prove
physical non-identifiability or uniqueness. Two physical worlds can share the
same tracked tree and differ in an external coupling value. Repository
presence is equally insufficient for scientific acceptance. These are
separate provenance and physics questions.
