# P069 Adjudication of ME3

ME3 reproduces four passing SymPy checks, but only its narrow stencil and
linearized long-wave algebra survive. The wider action, detectability, and
termination-scale readings exceed the constructed oracle.

## Surviving Source Content

The centered nearest-neighbour stencil has coefficients `1`, `1/12`, and
`1/360` on the second, fourth, and sixth derivative terms of its formal even
Taylor jet. For a normalized mass-one linearization, its fixed-wave-number
dispersion begins `1+k^2-a^2*k^4/12+a^4*k^6/360`. The source also writes the
necessary overall spacing weight in its discrete action. P069 retains these
fragments only inside the stronger typed theorem.

## Missing Action and Convergence Proof

ME3 never constructs its written site sum, declares a finite interval or
boundary condition, varies the action, or proves convergence of its value.
Its continuum check reads the leading coefficients of one local stencil,
checks that the onsite potential equals itself, and evaluates one cosine-mode
ratio. This cannot establish an action limit. P069 supplies a periodic
Riemann-normalized action, exact sitewise variation, a smoothness contract, and
an explicit action-error bound.

## Full-Zone and Smoothness Ceiling

ME3 does not assert the exact `-4*sin(k*a/2)^2/a^2` symbol, choose a first
Brillouin zone, or test aliases and the zone edge. Its `cos(x/lambda)` uses
`lambda` as an inverse-wavenumber variation scale; the full spatial wavelength
is `2*pi*lambda`. The local series requires smoothness and a remainder before
it can support a convergence claim.

## Rejected Physical Readings

The phrase that the correction is nonzero iff spacing is positive is false:
constant through cubic fields have no centered-stencil correction at any
positive spacing, and the zero Fourier mode is spacing-insensitive. A shared
stencil checked in physical and Fourier notation is a consistency check, not
an independent source. Nothing in the scalar algebra selects or makes the
spacing detectable, identifies it with an EFT termination scale, derives a
hydrogen medium, or imports ME1/ME2. Those narratives remain unaccepted.

## Disposition

ME3 is qualified and maps to C-LAT-001 only for the exact centered-stencil and
normalized linearized long-wave fragments. Its action-convergence,
detectability, ontology, and termination-scale claims are not source-proven.
The accepted positive object is independently completed by P069's canonical
action, variation, remainder, full-zone symbol, and convergence bound.
