# GC3 Disposition Review

GC3 should be qualified, not accepted wholesale and not refuted wholesale.
Its conditional globally-phased-real matrix theorem is exact and novel; its
universal condensate and physical CP interpretation lacks accepted premises.

The accepted mapping is
`[C-QBL-001,C-QBL-003,C-OVL-001,C-MIX-001,C-MIX-002,C-MIX-003]`. The first
five claims retain only their conditional profile, real-mode, overlap, matrix,
and abstract rephasing ceilings. C-MIX-003 adds both Gram cancellations, real
relative-basis quartets, commutator null identities, and the degeneracy caveat.

All nine source predicates receive individual decisions in
`evidence/check-adjudication.yaml`. Pending GC4 and GC5 cycle edges are
excluded from the derivation, and GC4 through GC6 remain separately
reviewable reverse consumers with no backward authority.

The qualification reason is that GC3 combines correct conditional algebra and
finite matrix examples with undeclared physical premises, a finite-sample
lower-bound overread, external wells mislabeled as coexisting condensates, an
unsupported geometry prediction, and a phase budget that conflates source
count K with matrix dimension N. Durable evidence is the native reproduction,
source audit, claim review, two exact verification routes, compatibility
audit, graph replay, and focused package tests.
