# GC4 Qualified Disposition Review

## Retained Scope

GC4 retains the declared two-profile quartic trial-energy values, the exact
finite interaction reconstructed by C-QBL-006, its leading nonzero-cosine tail
rate, the exact negative perpendicular interaction, and C-PHS-001's sharp
complete scalar-circle capacity theorem.

## Corrected Scope

The finite interaction is not a pure cosine: the quartic term adds a
phase-independent contribution and a cosine-squared contribution. The source's
scaled values are negative rather than its stated positive function. Energy,
its collective-coordinate derivative, merger dynamics, and stability are
different claims; anti-phase and Z3 generalized forces change sign outside
the sampled tail regime.

## Rejected Scope

Three separate pair evaluations are not the energy or field equation of a
three-profile configuration and provide no common solution, equilibrium,
spectrum, Lyapunov function, or evolution. C-MIX-002 explicitly supplies no
physical CP or generation lower bound, while capacity at most three does not
select occupancy three. Sparse graphs and larger internal spaces change the
capacity. No stable three-condensate mechanism, physical generation count,
Standard-Model map, or substrate conclusion is accepted.

## Decision

GC4 is qualified through C-QBL-001, C-QBL-003, C-MIX-002, C-QBL-006, and
C-PHS-001. Every source predicate has an individual verdict, GC5 remains
nonauthoritative, and GC5-GC6 remain separately reviewable consumers.
