# P070 Pre-Change Impact and Duplication Analysis

The impact boundary was evaluated at framework commit `d808fc8` after the
P070 normalization, measure, dimension, dependency, and comparator contract
was frozen and before canonical source was edited.

## Existing Surface Search

C-QBL-001 owns one conditional quartic sech background, and C-QBL-003 owns the
exact even and odd modes of its unconstrained scalar Hessian. C-QBL-003 already
states that the negative and zero eigenvalues are not positive masses or
generations. No accepted module, claim, test, or durable memory supplies a
general normalized multiplier expectation, gamma-ratio overlap, actual-mode
overlap ledger, or dimension/free-rescaling map.

## Duplication Boundary

P070 may reuse the accepted mode shapes and spectral interpretation ceiling.
It may add the nonduplicate normalized expectation theorem, positive-power
gamma ratio, actual even/odd squared-density overlaps, parity cross term, and
dimension/rescaling ledger. It may not restate the spectrum as a new claim or
promote its source labels into a Yukawa or flavor mechanism.

## Impact and Decision

The implementation is additive in `normalized_overlaps.py`, focused tests,
two campaign verifiers, and package exports. No existing accepted symbol or
signature changes. Direct consumers are the P070 tests and primary verifier,
governance, generated artifacts, MH1 disposition, and future typed overlap
audits. The independent verifier deliberately imports no canonical overlap API.

## Post-Change Detection

GitNexus is indexed at commit `1a94738`, ten commits behind the P070 worktree.
Its staged detector sees the 31-file transaction but maps only the touched
package `__all__`, reports low risk and no affected process, and cannot see the
new unindexed module. That incomplete graph result is not evidence of zero
impact. Direct symbol search finds consumers only in the additive export,
focused tests, and primary verifier. P070 contains no NumPy quadrature alias.
Targeted and full promotion replays are recorded separately so graph staleness
cannot substitute for executable evidence. The unchanged promotion boundary
passes all 582 repository tests and every repository workflow check.
