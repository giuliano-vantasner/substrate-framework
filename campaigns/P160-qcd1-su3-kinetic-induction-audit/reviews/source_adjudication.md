# P160 QCD1 Source Adjudication

QCD1 exits cleanly and all eleven implemented predicates pass. The tally
validates the standard fundamental SU(3) trace metric, exact antisymmetric and
symmetric trace tensors, commutator and anticommutator reconstruction, a
nonzero-momentum transverse projector, algebraic color factorization, and one
longitudinal counterexample. It does not validate the headline one-loop local
Yang--Mills induction.

The exact new algebraic surface survives. In the standard `T_a=lambda_a/2`
basis, `d_abc=2*Tr({T_a,T_b}T_c)` is fully symmetric,
`{T_a,T_b}=delta_ab*I_3/3+d_abc*T_c`, every component restricted to the
standard `lambda_1,lambda_2,lambda_3` embedded SU(2) vanishes, and components
outside that restriction are nonzero. These are representation-algebra facts,
not a unique characterization of SU(3) or a physical color identification.

The loop and action headline does not survive as written. QCD1 declares no
quantum matter action, statistics, determinant, regulator, bubble, seagull,
subtraction, counterterm, or field normalization. It imposes a transverse
projector rather than deriving the Ward cancellation. Its `u(1-u)` numerator
is fermion-shaped, not the declared complex-scalar numerator `(1-2u)^2`; the
accepted scalar fixed-positive-momentum massless limit diverges. A constant
coefficient multiplying the projector becomes a nonlocal inverse-momentum
curvature kernel, while the source never constructs the noncommuting cubic and
quartic curvature completion. Its Abelian guard also changes the trace
normalization manually.

The corrected positive loop object is conditional and generic. For a supplied
finite exact Hermitian Lie representation with explicitly real fully
antisymmetric structure constants, exact commutator closure, and positive
isotropic trace metric `tr_R(T_a*T_b)=T(R)*delta_ab`, separately declared
massive complex-scalar multiplets give the accepted scalar-QED2 kernel times
that trace metric. Bubble and seagull cancel before projection. The leading
local component coefficient is `N_s*g^2*T(R)/(48*pi*m^2)` and the trace-density
coefficient is `N_s*g^2/(48*pi*m^2)`; an independent proper-time calculation
completes the full non-Abelian curvature. The existing SU(2) theorem remains an
exact backward-compatible specialization.

The loop term stays additive to bare and counterterm coefficients. It supplies
no physical quark or gluon sector, unique total coupling, massless pole,
preferred dimension, QCD identification, observation, or substrate mechanism.
QCD2 and all later physical-induction sources remain separately queued. QCD1
is therefore qualified only through the exact claims C-LIE-003 and C-NVP-002,
along with already accepted duplicate algebra and kinematics; its headline
physical Yang--Mills induction is rejected.
