# S3 source adjudication

S3 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Its immutable source runs natively
and reports ten passing predicates, but the tally combines correct SU(3)
formula fixtures, a hand-written seven-representation table, an imported
collective constraint, hard-coded physical labels, a mass formula inconsistent
with its own displayed Hamiltonian, and literal WZW periods.

The positive result is C-IRR-001. For every pair of nonnegative Dynkin labels
`(p,q)`, the canonical module derives the Weyl dimension, quadratic Casimir in
C-LIE-001's convention, C-LIE-002 center triality, every interlacing
Gelfand--Tsetlin basis state, exact `(I3,Y)` weight multiplicities, and the
SU(2)xU(1) isospin rows. An independent route uses the positive-root Weyl
product, the inverse-Cartan highest-weight inner product, and semistandard
Young tableaux of shape `(p+q,q)`. It agrees over 36 irreps without importing
the canonical representation implementation.

The complete hypercharge filter is explicitly conditional: the caller supplies
an exact rational target and finite label bounds. At `Y=1`, the octet `(1,1)`
is the unique minimum-dimension match with `I=1/2`. The next distinct dimension
is ten, but it is a tie: `(0,3)` has `I=1/2` and `(3,0)` has `I=3/2`. S3's own
dictionary contains both and then excludes the antidecuplet by prose. A weight
filter therefore cannot select the physical decuplet or identify baryons.

S3 also mixes hypercharge conventions. With its stated fundamental weights,
the sextet `(2,0)` has distinct hypercharges `{2/3,-1/3,-4/3}`, not
`{4/3,1/3,-2/3}`. Consequently, the complete `Y=2/3` guard has the
antitriplet `(0,1)` as the unique minimum and also admits the sextet. The source
and dossier variously call the triplet, antitriplet, or sextet the selected
representation.

The physical constraint remains an import. The executable substitutes
`N_c=3` and `B=1` into `Y_R=N_c*B/3`; it constructs no SU(3)/U(1)
configuration space, right action, WZW velocity-linear term, canonical
momentum, generator sign, wavefunction measure, or state dictionary. Primary
Guadagnini derivation makes these ingredients load bearing. Accepted
C-WZW-001, C-WZW-002, and C-TOP-002 intentionally do not identify the
mathematical level with `N_c`, winding with physical baryon number, or weights
with particle representations.

The spin and mass checks do not repair that gap. Exact branching at the
constrained row gives the displayed octet and decuplet isospins, but S3's
claimed universal SU(2) range is false: the fundamental already branches into
an isodoublet and an isosinglet. Under the dossier's displayed rotor
Hamiltonian, both `Delta C2` and `Delta[J(J+1)]` equal three between decuplet
and octet, so the gap is `3/(2I1)`, not `3/(2I2)`. The latter belongs to the
same-spin antidecuplet-octet pair in that conditional model.

The finite exponential sample does not prove the quantified integer-level
theorem, and `k=N_c` remains physical input. The last predicate assigns the
nonzero and exact periods as `1` and `0`; narrower exact cohomology and
primitive-period results already live in C-WZW-001 and C-WZW-002.

Decision: promote C-IRR-001 and qualify S3 through C-IRR-001, C-LIE-001,
C-LIE-002, C-WZW-002, and C-TOP-002 under their ceilings. Do not promote a
collective-WZW, QCD-color, baryon, physical octet/decuplet, mass-spectrum, or
substrate claim. The exact representation object, conditional tie-complete
filter, predicate audit, dependency and consumer graph, and governance state
close the source unit with no hidden scientific debt.
