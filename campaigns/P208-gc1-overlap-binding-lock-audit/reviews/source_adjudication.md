# P208 Source Adjudication: GC1

GC1 executes all nine predicates, but only its fixed-quartic local identity and
several conditional algebraic observations survive. The claimed universal
mode-tower refutation does not.

## Retained Mathematical Content

For the declared quartic potential, direct differentiation gives the local
curvature deficit `D(f)=f^2/4`. If the multiplier is separately declared as
`c=lambda*f`, exact elimination gives `D=c^2/(4*lambda^2)`. The source's
`lambda=1` form is correct. On the accepted quartic sech profile, the
fluctuation well has one analytic global minimum at the supplied center.

Independent binding and multiplier fields break the identity, and a second
fixed-depth well paired with a remote core multiplier is a valid conditional
countermodel. The native exact-sine finite calculation also reproduces its
reported sampled spectra and overlaps; those values remain numeric evidence
for the stated grid and parameters only.

## Corrected Types and Models

The source headline conflates the pointwise multiplier `c(x)` with the
integrated overlap `y_n`. It derives `D(x)=c(x)^2/4`, not `D=y_n^2/4`.
Furthermore, “arbitrary profile” means arbitrary substitution into one fixed
quartic potential and fixed linear multiplier map. Alternative potentials and
coupling maps change the result exactly.

The numeric section solves the accepted exact-sine background. Its
fluctuation curvature deficit is `(1-cos(f))/2`, not `f^2/4`; the two coincide
only at leading small amplitude. Calling that section a direct measurement of
the quartic identity mixes C-QBL-002 and C-QBL-003 conventions.

## Rejected Universal Inferences

An arbitrarily shallow attractive well can bind in one spatial dimension.
C-OVL-002's exact Pöschl--Teller ledger gives a negative ground eigenvalue for
every positive depth. The quartic scale family supplies a second correction:
its two accepted bound levels remain below threshold for every positive
kappa, while the declared profile overlaps both tend to zero with kappa. Their
ratio is fixed at 2/3, so this is not a hierarchy, but “small absolute overlap
and bound are contradictory” is false.

The source relocation check computes RMS about the origin divided by mean
absolute coordinate. That ratio is at least one for every normalized density
by nonnegative variance; a point density localized at any nonzero radius
saturates it. The predicate is therefore insensitive to the claimed
distinction. Eight frequency samples, one pointwise grid refinement, and the
undeclared universal thresholds `0.1` and `10` cannot prove any-frequency or
power-law behavior.

C-QBL-001 and C-QBL-002 provide no stability window. The two-profile unlock
does not establish a coexisting stable multisoliton or identify MH2's
separately planted isospectral wells as solutions of a common field equation.
