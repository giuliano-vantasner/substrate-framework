# Source adjudication: HE5 static consumer parse

## Decision

HE5 is out of scientific-claim scope and retained as reproducible migration
impact evidence. Its exact lexical census is verified against the pinned source
tree, but a regex token match or non-match is not a mathematical or physical
claim and cannot establish or exclude semantic dependency under other names.
No accepted framework claim maps to HE5.

## Check-family audit

HE5.1 correctly identifies 213 Python bridge files after explicitly excluding
phase 45. P015 independently derives that count from the 218 hash-pinned bridge
records and records the full source-tree digest, so the null scan is not an
empty-root artifact.

HE5.2 and HE5.7 are valid positive controls for the declared regexes. Both the
source and its frozen mirror contain the same five action-token filenames. P015
records their content hashes and reproduces the energy/action intersection.

HE5.3 exactly states that its four action-related regex alternatives have zero
matches outside phase 45 at `substrate@6d1f4e0`. This is a lexical fact about
that immutable snapshot. It does not prove that no equivalent action-scale idea
appears with different symbols or phrasing.

HE5.4's token set finds exactly 36 files and P015 preserves every path, group,
and SHA-256. The source's description of all of them as semantic `E0`
consumers is broader than the parser: comments, docstrings, definitions,
imports, and computations are not distinguished.

HE5.5 correctly computes an empty set intersection between its energy-token and
action-token path sets. The further prose that every consumer uses `E0` “as an
energy and nothing more,” never divides an equivalent expression by frequency,
and cannot be altered by a conceptual correction is not established by the
regex oracle. P015's comment-only fixture is an explicit counterexample to
inferring semantic dependency from lexical intersection alone.

HE5.6 correctly records four charge-token files in the pinned snapshot and an
empty lexical intersection with the action-token set. Its Phase-46 annotation
also correctly demonstrates why a live corpus census cannot pin a timeless
count. The stronger claim that no file computes equivalent charge and action
objects together is outside the token set's reach.

## Exact scope disposition

HE5 is terminally `out_of_scope` for the scientific claim registry because its
positive object is repository-static navigation and impact metadata rather than
a physics predicate. Its useful content remains durable in P015's hashed report,
20-check verifier, six-check independent traversal, reusable source-audit API,
and tests. Its empty lexical intersections may guide audits but never waive
claim-level dependency review.
