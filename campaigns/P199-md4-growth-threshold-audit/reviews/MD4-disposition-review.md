# MD4 Disposition Review

MD4 should be qualified through C-CMB-003 and proposed C-MKV-001. Its 34
native predicates reproduce. A separately declared immigration-death process
then supplies a genuine positive time-dependent mechanism with local drift
`r*(S-n)`, exact stationary mass, PGF, mean, and transition kernel. An explicit
second reversible process with the same stationary mass establishes that the
static law does not select unique dynamics.

The source itself does not construct that process. Its ratio
`w(n+1)/w(n)=S/(n+1)` compares neighboring state labels and is not a time
derivative. At positive integer `S`, the static mass has tied modes `S-1,S`, so
`S=n` is not a unique modal boundary. Even in the selected process, positive
local drift below `S` coexists with death jumps and therefore does not imply
monotone sample-path growth.

MD4's two displayed threshold forms are exact rearrangements only after its
MD2 asymptote and identifications of `beta`, `beta_a`, `ell`, and `a` are
supplied. C-QFL-001 does not supply those material values or a growth law;
C-DOS-001 does not turn a target-matched continuum cutoff into microscopic
granularity; C-VOP-001 does not prepare a material coherent state; and PN2 has
no accepted occupation or band premise.

The crossover point mass is ordinary C-CMB-003 arithmetic. Its comparison to
a hard-coded WN6 value does not yield a total event probability, an open or
rescued channel, a branching fraction, or a rate. The source's explicit
`check(..., True)` cannot validate the discrimination headline.

The exact eleven-node graph covers 347 native checks without duplicate native
execution. No node has a legacy NumPy quadrature surface, so no `np.trapz`
version event contributes to the scientific verdict. MD5 and MD6 remain
pending.
