# S4 source adjudication

S4 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Its immutable source runs natively
and reports eleven passing predicates, but the tally combines correct scalar
series and Pauli special cases with an inserted effective operator, an assigned
`I1-I2` tensor, literal coefficient ratios, an imported target, backward
solution for `J1`, and a dimensionally invalid value for the Skyrme coupling.

The positive result is C-VEC-001. For exact real components in
`L_i=i*x_i^a*sigma_a`, the canonical module derives the ordered identities
`sum_ij |x_i cross x_j|^2=I1-I2` and
`sum_ij Tr([L_i,L_j]^2)=-8*(I1-I2)`. A rank-one current gives an exact zero
counterexample, while independent spatial and internal orthogonal changes
preserve the full invariant.

The leading connection result is explicitly conditional. A declared positive
quadratic mass density selects `v_i^a=x_i^a/2` with positive Hessian. For the
half Maurer--Cartan connection, `F_ij=-[L_i,L_j]/4`. Substitution into the
separately declared curvature density
`-sum_ij Tr(F_ij*F_ij)/(2*g^2)` gives
`-sum_ij Tr([L_i,L_j]^2)/(32*g^2)=(I1-I2)/(4*g^2)`, so the equally normalized
Skyrme coupling is `e=g`.

This is a leading derivative result, not exact elimination of the full kinetic
vector equation. With `L=O(p)`, curvature is order `p^2`, the kinetic equation
residual and leading field correction are order `p^3` (the latter divided by
the supplied heavy scale squared), the displayed energy is order `p^4`, and
the first backreaction energy is order `p^6/M^2`. The mass coefficient cannot
be fitted to change the leading quartic coefficient.

KSRF remains a premise. Conditional on `m_V^2=a*g^2*F^2` with equal mass
dimensions for `m_V` and `F`, the exact dimensionless relation is
`e=g=m_V/(sqrt(a)*F)`. Neither the source nor C-VEC-001 derives that relation,
selects `a=2`, or identifies the symbols with physical particles. S4 instead
uses `e=F_pi/2`, which equates a dimensionless coupling with an energy, and
its dossier notices this mismatch without repairing the executable claim.

S4's B1 route is circular. It imports `B1` and `c4=8/B1^2`, solves backward
for `J1`, and never uses rho or KSRF variables in the corresponding condition.
Pending B1 supplies no accepted authority. The source also constructs no HLS
coset, vector field, transformation law, complete action, field equation,
controlled inverse remainder, current-conservation premise, physical field
dictionary, medium pole response, or substrate map.

Decision: promote C-VEC-001 and qualify S4 through C-VEC-001, C-EFT-001, and
C-CHI-001 under their stated ceilings. Do not promote S4's physical rho
mechanism, `c4=8/B1^2`, `e=F_pi/2`, fitted `J1`, KSRF derivation, in-medium
closure, or Option-C interpretation. The exact theorem, independent
rederivation, source audit, dependency and consumer graph, compatibility
classification, and governance transaction leave no hidden scientific debt.
