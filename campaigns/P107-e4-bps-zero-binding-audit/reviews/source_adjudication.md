# E4 source adjudication

E4 is qualified. Its positive-branch square identity is exact, and P107 extends
it to both orientations with the normalized target measure, signed degree
pairing, absolute-degree lower bound, dimensions, equality condition, and
sextic-coupling convention made explicit. These results support C-BPS-001.

The source does not prove that the bound is attained in every degree sector.
Its zero-binding check hard-codes the linear mass formula and then tests that
formula. C-BPS-002 therefore states the valid result conditionally: if the
sector infima actually attain the same bound in the compared positive-degree
sectors, their signed linear difference is zero. Exact nonnegative slack
counterexamples show that bound linearity alone leaves either sign possible.

The formal near-BPS cancellation also survives only under a controlled
expansion. C-BPS-003 keeps the correction coefficient and remainder explicit.
The coefficient can have either sign or vanish, and an uncontrolled remainder
can dominate epsilon. More strongly, P107 independently rederives the standard
`V=1-cos(chi)` compacton and finds that its naive L2 first-order correction
diverges logarithmically while the L4 edge factor remains finite. E4's claimed
L2+L4 correction is therefore not established for that allowed potential.

The later convention audit is exact: E4 uses
`lambda_B^2*pi^4*B_mu*B^mu`, whereas a convention without `pi^4` uses
`lambda_A=pi^2*lambda_B`. E4's bound is correct in its own convention. Applying
its displayed coefficient to `lambda_A` creates one spurious factor `pi^2`.

No accepted physical action, coupling values, saturation existence theorem,
degree-to-baryon or nucleus map, reaction, empirical scale, perturbative
correction, or yield mechanism closes E4's broader headline. All five source
predicates are covered in `evidence/check-adjudication.yaml`; the clean
five-check tally is reproduction evidence, not blanket promotion.
