# MD5 Disposition Review

MD5 should be qualified through C-BRN-001, C-CMB-003, and proposed C-BRN-002.
Its 63 native predicates reproduce. The constant-positive-weight allocation is
already C-BRN-001, the static factorial-one membership and complete modes are
C-CMB-003, and the new exact total-derivative criterion supplies the honest
generalization when the weight is a separately declared differentiable
function of population.

The source's population derivative holds its selected weight fixed. If the
weight depends on `N`, the sign is controlled by `w+N*w'`; positivity alone is
not sufficient. The three positive families `N^(-1/2)`, `N^(-1)`, and
`N^(-2)` make the comparison fraction decrease, remain stationary, and
increase. For an integer-only population, the source would additionally need
an adjacent-step law for `N*w(N)` rather than silently treating the count as a
continuous variable.

The source's displayed `S` expression omits `N`, but free-symbol absence
inside a selected formula cannot derive the underlying material independence
or parameter map. MD2 supplies no accepted material `K`, `a`, population law,
or granularity. PN2 supplies only conditional floor-and-remainder arithmetic
and no accepted occupied band or final state. At positive integer `S`, the
factorial-one law has adjacent modes `S-1,S`, so the source's unique
`floor(S)` branching minimum is incomplete.

MC3's accepted correction retains independent host curvature, inertia, and
phase scale. Doubling an inserted effective mass therefore does not establish
an exact hydrogen/deuterium `sqrt(2)` handshake. MD3 supplies a declared
coherent-state measurement law but no material preparation, interaction, or
transition matrix element. Static positive mathematical membership does not
make a physical channel, prove exhaustiveness, or supply a reaction or rate.

The exact ten-node graph covers 354 native predicates without duplicate native
execution. No node has a legacy NumPy quadrature surface, so no `np.trapz`
version event contributes to the scientific verdict. MD6 remains pending.
