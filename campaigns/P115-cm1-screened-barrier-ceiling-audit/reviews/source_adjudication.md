# CM1 source adjudication

CM1 reproduces all thirteen predicates. Its exact identity survives: for
positive collision energy `E` and barrier energy `G` and nonnegative shift
`U`, the bare factor, shifted factor, and enhancement are

`B=exp(-sqrt(G/E))`, `P=exp(-sqrt(G/(E+U)))`, and `F=P/B`.

P115 proves their common energy dimensions, exact composition, open range,
all derivative signs, endpoint limits, scale covariance, and stable direct
evaluation. At positive `U`, `P` increases with `E` and `U`, decreases with
`G`, and tends to the finite nonzero floor `exp(-sqrt(G/U))` as `E` tends to
zero. The enhancement diverges in that limit while the product remains finite.
Thus CM1's low-input-null wording is false even when its selected floor is
numerically tiny. Both shifted factor and enhancement tend to one at high
energy.

If an independent premise establishes `0<=U<=U_max`, monotonicity gives the
upper factor `P(E,U,G)<=P(E,U_max,G)`. CM1 does not establish a universal
`U_max`: it takes the maximum of four selected Thomas-Fermi models using
assigned one-electron conduction counts, imported constants, and no
uncertainty. Those values are regression evidence only.

The source's symbolic energy-monotonicity and `is_gamow_shaped` guards inspect
one substituted point. Any increasing rational function passes the latter,
so it does not characterize inverse-square-root barrier form. Its nine-point
sweep is regression, its low/high thresholds are selected dimensionless
cutoffs, and the neutral-uncapped predicate repeats the same function call.
The firewall residual proves dependence inside one constructed expression,
not a systemwide physical dataflow property.

Most importantly, `B`, `P`, and `F` are dimensionless. Multiplying by a zero
rate prefactor gives zero, and an arbitrary prefactor rescales any alleged
rate without changing the barrier theorem. CM1 therefore establishes no cross
section, S factor, collision flux, attempt frequency, density, branching,
physical reaction rate, maximum yield, heat, material realization, coherent
channel, or observation.

C-SCR-001 accepts the exact conditional factor theorem with this ceiling. CM1
is qualified. CM3, CM6, CM7, GB6, and WN7 remain pending and inherit only the
accepted dimensionless algebra after their own adjudications.
