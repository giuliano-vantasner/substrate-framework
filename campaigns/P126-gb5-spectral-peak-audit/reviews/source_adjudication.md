# GB5 Source Adjudication

## Exact result

GB5 assigns `peak=omega_ph`; its derivative one and monotonicity are exact by
definition. Qualified GB2 and PN2 evidence supplies only floor quotient and
half-open remainder arithmetic. It does not assign constituent energies.

## Spectral counterexamples

At total energy ten and divisor three, the arithmetic ledger is quotient three
and remainder one. An added equal-quanta ansatz with three entries at three and
one at one peaks at three. Equally energy-conserving spectra of five entries at
two or two entries at five peak elsewhere while the arithmetic identity remains
true. Quotient zero supplies no divisor-energy entry, quotient one plus a
remainder can tie, detector weighting can move the mode, and zero occupation
has no mode.

At fixed total, changing the divisor changes quotient and remainder by plateaus
and jumps. Common scaling preserves the quotient while moving the asserted
peak. Large multiplicity changes energy or line intensity, not the location of
the identity's line.

## Source and data gate

All thirteen predicates reproduce. The conservation check rearranges a defined
remainder without testing its bound. The sweep copies its input list, empirical
tuple checks accept arbitrary tuple values, and the final no-fit check is
literal `True`. The local source does avoid fitting the named values, but that
does not validate a spectrum.

The pinned paper text does describe order-1–2-keV soft X rays and optical
excitation energies near 23 and 27 eV. GB5 neither digitizes its spectrum figure
nor extracts a modal value, uncertainty, or common observable. These inputs do
not enter selection or validation.

## Physical ceiling and decision

No states, interaction, matrix element, density of states, occupation,
linewidth, broadening, detector response, material mapping, or measurement map
is supplied. The GB2-GB5 candidate cycle grants no authority. No distinct
positive spectral theorem or API survives, so GB5 is terminally qualified with
no claim, package, or release change. No NumPy compatibility event occurs.
