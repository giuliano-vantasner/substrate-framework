# P071 Source Adjudication: MH2

MH2 executes successfully, but its terminal tally does not establish the
headline radial or generation mechanism.

## Retained Mathematical Content

For its declared finite grids and parameters, MH2 numerically obtains
order-one same-center overlaps and attenuated overlaps for normalized ground
states of separately translated attractive wells. Its late log ratios agree
with `-kappa*d` because the supplied core sech has the slower tail. A constant
multiplier has equal expectation against every normalized density. These are
useful regression observations once their inputs and scope remain explicit.

## Rejected Interpretations

The calculation is not radial: its coordinate ranges from `-6` to a positive
wall, its measure is `dx`, and it has neither an origin condition nor a radial
Jacobian. Rung `n` changes the external potential to a new well centered at
`n*d`; solving each planted well does not derive the centers or make its ground
states the spectrum of one operator. Translation makes the infinite-line
versions isospectral, so their labels are not energy levels or generations.

The exponent is the product of a tail rate and the free spacing. Calling
`kappa` derived does not determine `kappa*d`. The finite log-line thresholds
depend on the chosen spacing, rung count, well width/depth, and asymptotic
window. The lepton predicate merely checks that two supplied positive log
spacings and the constructed log ratios exceed loose thresholds; it is not a
fit, prediction, or discriminator. The flat-condensate result is normalization
algebra and cannot validate the source of the wells.
