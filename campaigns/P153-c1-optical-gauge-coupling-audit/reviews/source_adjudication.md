# P153 C1 Source Adjudication

C1 reproduces all nine predicates natively. That successful tally validates
its declared constant-coefficient algebra and hard-coded comparison, not its
headline physical interpretation.

C0 exactly reproduces C-OG-001's metric and inverse. C1 restores the missing
positive charge `e` and is exact only as C-GAU-001 minimal coupling. C2 becomes
the positive conditional result in C-OG-005 after an explicit charged-scalar
action is declared. The plane-wave mass shell depends on `w+e*A_t` and
`k-e*A_x`, not on the canonical labels separately.

C3 expands the mass shell at fixed `k`, but that difference changes under a
gauge relabeling. A constant connection on the simply connected line is
removed by `chi=-A_t*t-A_x*x`; there is no local spectral shift. A circle can
retain a flat-connection effect only with a declared circumference, boundary
phase, charge representation, and holonomy. Large-gauge connection changes and
mode relabeling leave the resulting spectrum invariant.

C3b is vacuous as evidence because G was not in the declared equation. G1 is
the free massive-scalar limit, not a derived breather. G2 omits its declared
`eps0`, does not construct the stated SI curvature, and compares against an
`A_eff^2/(n*L_med^2)` scale imposed without a material action. G2b checks symbol
membership created by construction. C4 correctly recognizes dependence on the
invariant spatial momentum but does not establish a physical vector potential,
Berry material, or Aharonov-Bohm observation.

For varying `n(x)`, the exact spatial operator is
`D_x[(c0^2/n)D_x Psi]`; C1's simple form misses
`-c0^2*n_x*D_x(Psi)/n^2`. Finally, C-BER-001's convention transforms with the
opposite same-phase sign from C-GAU-001. A separately declared texture must use
`e*A_x=-B_phi*partial_x(phi)`. C1 inserts the dimensionless local half
component directly, without that pullback, charge, sign, or units.

C1 is therefore qualified through C-OG-005, C-OG-001, C-GAU-001, and
C-BER-001. No material coupling, analog-gravity observation, real-gravity
effect, electrogravitic estimate, or breather realization is promoted.
