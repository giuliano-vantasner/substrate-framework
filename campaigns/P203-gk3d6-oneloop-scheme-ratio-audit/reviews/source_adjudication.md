## Normalization and accuracy correction

GK3D6.3a computes a relative normalization shift, not a relative coupling
shift. Relative to `c=1`, the source branch gives

\[
\frac{Z(c)-Z(1)}{Z(1)}=-\frac{\log c}{L},\qquad
\frac{g^2(c)-g^2(1)}{g^2(1)}=\frac{\log c}{L-\log c}.

`c` alone supplies neither that bound nor the nonzero positive domain: at
`log(c)=L`, the zero-boundary normalization vanishes. No two-loop coefficient,
scheme transformation, remainder oracle, or perturbative-domain proof is
