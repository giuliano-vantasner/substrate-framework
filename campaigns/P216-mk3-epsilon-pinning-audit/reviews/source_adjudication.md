# P216 MK3 Source Adjudication

## Decision

MK3 is qualified rather than accepted wholesale. Its exact local quotient and
all-premise substitution algebra survive as conditional composition, while its
physical epsilon pinning, fixed-theory flow-breaking, and near-BPS
self-consistency conclusions do not have accepted dependency closure. No new
claim or package API is promoted.

## Source Reproduction and Oracle Scope

The pinned 19,470-byte source at SHA-256 `64254d0f...16404` imports SymPy and
the standard AST module, has six literal and runtime checks plus one fail-fast
assertion, and reaches `ALL 6 CHECKS PASS` natively in 0.47 seconds. It has no
NumPy integration-name surface. That tally proves reachability only; its checks
substitute disputed premises into their own consequences and do not establish
the headline dependency graph.

## MK3.1

MK3.1 is valid exact definition algebra. For positive supplied inputs,
`1/[e*(lambda*F_pi)*(mu/F_pi^2)]` equals
`(F_pi/e)/(lambda*mu)` and is dimensionless in KI2's convention. P172 already
showed that an arbitrary dimensionless normalization multiplies this monomial,
that it is not identified with C-BPS-003 epsilon, and that C-BPS-001 admits the
whole positive coupling family. The predicate earns conditional exact scope,
not a selected physical observable.

## MK3.2

MK3.2 is valid only as all-premise source-convention algebra. The displayed
`lambda_A=N_c/(4*F_pi)` and `mu=m_pi*F_pi/2` give
`lambda_A*mu=N_c*m_pi/8`. P214 and P215 reject the physical derivations of
both inputs. More importantly, KI2 and C-BPS-001 use the accepted BPS
coefficient `lambda_BPS`, while C-VEC-002 proves
`lambda_A=pi^2*lambda_BPS`. Therefore the conditionally composed accepted
product is `lambda_BPS*mu=N_c*m_pi/(8*pi^2)`, and the corresponding quotient
is `8*pi^2*(F_pi/e)/(N_c*m_pi)`. MK3 never applies this conversion.

## MK3.3

MK3.3 is conditional substitution, not a zero-input derivation. C-SK-001 owns
`F_pi/e=16*pi*E_e` only iff two separately supplied positive mass formulas are
equated; NY1 is duplicate evidence and retains the empirical electron rest
energy. MK3 additionally supplies a pion mass and `N_c=3`, neither derived by
its accepted dependencies. Its source-convention result is
`128*pi*E_e/(3*m_pi)`; the accepted-convention conditional result is
`128*pi^3*E_e/(3*m_pi)`. The prose also falsely equates `128*pi/3` with
`64*pi/3` and alternates approximately 0.496 with 0.248, a contradiction its
executable predicate never examines. Its non-tautology guard tests prior
dependence only on `F_pi,e,lambda,mu`, despite saying the expression previously
depended on `g,m_V` too.

## MK3.4

MK3.4 does not prove accepted flow breaking. The source correctly reproduces
the free quotient's `t^-2` scaling. Its alleged derived branch then
differentiates `eps_final_Nc3`, an expression constructed without `t`, so the
zero derivative is true by definition. The separately forced scaled couplings
still give `eps_step2/t^2`. A supplied fixed positive product would restrict
the simultaneous flow to `t=1`, but MK1 and MK2 do not supply that accepted
product; the C-BPS-001 parameter family therefore remains.

## MK3.5

MK3.5's physical inference is rejected. C-BPS-003 defines an abstract positive
expansion coordinate tending to zero under separately controlled sector
expansions; it neither maps that coordinate to MK3's local quotient nor states
that a value below one proves self-consistency. Even granting every source
premise and its empirical masses, the source convention gives about 0.496,
whereas the accepted BPS convention multiplies it by `pi^2` to about 4.90.
Thus the source's own `<1` verdict is convention-sensitive and reverses under
the framework convention.

## MK3.6

MK3.6 is partly useful but false as stated. The epsilon expression itself is
algebraically independent of the kappa comparator. However, executable code
constructs `KAPPA_EMP_VALUE=929/1000.0` and reads it in the guard while the
claim says no comparator is read anywhere. The lowercase case-sensitive token
`kappa_emp` does not match uppercase `KAPPA_EMP_VALUE`; splitting 0.929 into
929 and 1000 also defeats the numeric-literal scan. The supposedly independent
recomputation repeats the same `128*pi/3` formula. This guard cannot validate
its literal headline.

## Verification and Sensitivity

Twenty-nine primary and sixteen fresh independent exact checks pass. They
mutate the local normalization, each coupling, their product, the simultaneous
flow, the source-to-BPS convention, C-SK premises, empirical energy input,
`N_c`, pion mass, physical closure, and comparator guard. The strongest
counterexample is exact: the accepted convention multiplies the same
all-premise quotient by `pi^2` and reverses the source threshold verdict.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

The fifteen-node graph pins 108 source predicates and 16 assertions. MK3
replays freshly. B1 and E3's inherited integration-name shapes are

MR2, and MR6 remain pending reverse consumers and cannot grant authority.
Their hashes match the expensive clean executions already recorded by P215,

## Terminal Scope

MK3 maps conditional algebra to C-BPS-001, C-SK-001, and C-VEC-002. It does
not promote a physical epsilon, coupling closure, fixed-theory symmetry
breaking, near-BPS self-consistency theorem, no-import prediction, paid debt,
or substrate mechanism. A terminal `qualified` disposition is therefore the
smallest honest state.
