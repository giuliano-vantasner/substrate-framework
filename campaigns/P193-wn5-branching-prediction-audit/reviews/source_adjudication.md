# WN5 Source Adjudication

WN5 is mixed and should be qualified through C-BRN-001 and C-OSC-001 rather
than promoted as a new physical prediction or duplicated as C-BRN-002.

The exact accepted composition is
`B_gamma(n)=rho/(N*S^n/n!+rho)` for supplied positive N, rho, and S. Its exact
adjacent difference has the sign of `n+1-S`, so noninteger S has one minimum
at `floor(S)`, positive integer S has the adjacent tied minima `S-1,S`, and
`0<S<1` has its minimum at order zero. Relative odds against order one equal
`N*S^(n-1)/n!` on positive orders. These are direct compositions of
C-BRN-001 and C-OSC-001 and need no new claim or API.

The following source edges are qualified or rejected:

- The fixed-weight population partial is negative only with order and weight
  held fixed. Along a continuous population-dependent path its sign is
  opposite `w+N*w'`; the exact inverse-population path is constant.
- The finite `S=25` first-argmin grid hides the tied minima 24 and 25 and omits
  order zero from the headline's declared all-nonnegative domain.
- Finite positivity and monotonicity samples are regression evidence, not the
  universal proof supplied by the exact adjacent difference.
- WN5 cannot rehabilitate GB4's rejected unrestricted regime-independent
  headline; only C-BRN-001's fixed-weight domain survives.
- A turnover distinguishes the three declared mathematical weight families
  only at fixed positive N, rho, channel definitions, and order meaning.
- No physical measured-turnover prediction follows without accepted states,
  exhaustive channels, rates, interactions, spectral data, an order
  observable, cross-order protocol, and parameter provenance.
- The rho-free relative-odds ratio is not a physical suppression magnitude or
  unique no-suppression floor.

The recommended WN5 disposition is `qualified` with mappings C-BRN-001 and
C-OSC-001. WN7, MD5, and MD6 remain pending.
