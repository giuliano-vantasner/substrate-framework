The impact boundary was evaluated at framework commit `beb6ed2` after the
P068 domain, boundary, topology, stiffness, core, and comparator contract was
frozen and before canonical source was edited.

## Existing Surface Search

withholds spatial condensates and defects. C-VTX-001's
`angular_log_coefficient` belongs to a local Abelian-Higgs field with an
asymptotic gauge profile; it neither gives a global phase/director Dirichlet

integer charged-scalar winding and makes fractional flux a separate premise.
GitNexus returns the Abelian-Higgs verifier and unrelated angular-mode flows,
but no half-quantum, matched-shell, or polar deck-transformation process.

## Duplication Boundary

P068 may reuse C-SPN-001 only to name the projective `RP2` ray orbit. It may
add the nonduplicate annular energy, fixed-total-charge shell bookkeeping,
full polar cover, and stiffness/core comparison. It may not restate the
gauge-vortex BVP, identify a global condensate phase with a local gauge field,
or infer a material half-quantum vortex.
