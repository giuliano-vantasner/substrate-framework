# Source adjudication: EL3 conditional mass-length coordinate

## Decision

EL3 is qualified. Its premise-explicit unit-product composition supports
`C-DIM-004` and composes with `C-DIM-003`; its unit identification remains
conditional `C-SK-001` content. The source does not eliminate an independent
mass coordinate, derive its coupling or unit premises, or identify a physical
electron.

## Check-family audit

EL3.1 exactly solves the equal-coefficient equation after declaring the L2 and
L4 coefficients. The result is valid within that reduction, but the coefficient
convention and physical Skyrme applicability remain premises; a local solve does
not promote them as framework dynamics.

EL3.2 correctly multiplies the two declared natural-unit formulas and restores
the only dimensionally admissible action-speed factor. Cancellation of `F_pi`
leaves the free dimensionless coupling `e`; it does not eliminate mass
information by itself.

EL3.3 is the exact conditional content accepted as `C-DIM-004`: composing
`U*L=S*c0/(2*e^2)` with `U=4*pi*m*c0^2` gives
`m=S/(8*pi*e^2*L*c0)`, coordinate `N_m=1/(8*pi*e^2)`, and reduced wavelength
`S/(m*c0)=8*pi*e^2*L`. Both equations and `e` remain explicit inputs.

EL3.4 correctly fixes the dimensionful monomial `S/(c0*L)`. Its conclusion of
a unique full expression is too strong because arbitrary dimensionless
functions of `e` multiply that monomial without changing dimensions. P020
exhibits this freedom directly.

EL3.5 is a valid sensitivity family. P020 generalizes it by requiring both
declared coefficients and rejecting changes to the coupling power as well.

EL3.6 derives exact conditional alternative and consistency formulas. Calling
the form “e-free” does not make it input-free: it depends on the separately
unproved proton mass and shape coefficient. The route moves rather than removes
dimensionless and physical information.

EL3.7 is useful provenance navigation showing that the `4*pi` relation is
reused, not derived. Its marker and AST scans are lexical facts about a pinned
tree, not evidence that no other physical anchor or equivalent premise enters
under different syntax.

EL3.8 correctly finds rank three before and after dropping the mass column. The
four-column matrix nevertheless has a one-dimensional nullspace generated by
the mass coordinate `m*c0*L/S`; the three-column matrix has none. Dropping the
column therefore drops one independent dimensionless datum unless a physical
equation fixes it. Rank preservation alone proves dimensional redundancy, not
information elimination.

EL3.9 is a functioning forbidden-comparator guard and appropriately confirms
that the narrow conditional identity requires no measured mass. It cannot
establish the physical premises or a value for `e`.

## Exact qualification

Accepted content is limited to the explicit conditional unit-product
composition, its equivalent coordinate and wavelength forms, coefficient
sensitivity, and prior set-local dimensional theorems. EL3 does not establish
that the Skyrme equations describe an electron, that its length is the framework
granularity, that the coupling is derived, or that an imported mass datum has
been removed rather than reexpressed.
