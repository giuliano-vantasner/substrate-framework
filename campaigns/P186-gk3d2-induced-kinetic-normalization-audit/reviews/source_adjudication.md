# GK3D2 Source Adjudication

GK3D2 is qualified, not accepted literally. Its exact scalar-to-Dirac factor
four, conditional one-third and four-thirds weights, affine differential
equation, common-scale invariance, and explicit zero-boundary specialization
survive through C-VAC-003 after complete derivation and scope correction.

The source does not derive the scalar loop it advertises. Its helper integrates
a numerator and a Gamma recurrence but contains no scalar determinant, bubble,
seagull, common gauge-preserving regulator, subtraction scale, Laurent pole, or
counterterm. P186 supplies those objects and derives the Ward cancellation
before the transverse tensor is used.

The load-bearing boundary claim is rejected. Hash-pinned rung25 defines no
kinetic coefficient, cutoff, charged threshold, local counterterm, or matching
surface. It constructs exact-connection witnesses and checks their curvature;
this is not an Euler-Lagrange proof that all connections are pure gauge.
C-MAX-001 gives the governed statement: removing the kinetic term yields the
current constraint and does not force the connection to be pure gauge.
Introducing and integrating out charged matter also changes the theory and
operator basis. Therefore rung25 cannot supply `Z_ref=0` in the new theory.

The corrected general result is affine:
`Z(mu)=Z_ref+b*log(mu_ref/mu)/(8*pi^2)`. `Z_ref` includes independent local and
finite matching coordinates. Setting it to zero is a separately declared
compositeness or matching premise. Only on that branch and for positive `b`
does scale ordering determine the sign. Off that branch the source's total
normalization, unique remaining logarithm, general positivity, physical
coupling, charged spectrum, group, and substrate conclusions do not follow.

GK3D3, GK3D4, and GK3D6 are direct pending consumers of the invalid boundary
reading; GK3D5 is transitive. They inherit only the conditional loop slopes,
weight formula, affine family, and explicitly assumed zero-matching branch.
No future source may retroactively select the reference values or turn this
qualification into a physical gauge-sector promotion.
