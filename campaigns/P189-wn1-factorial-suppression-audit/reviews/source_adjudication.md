# WN1 Source Adjudication

WN1 is qualified rather than accepted literally. Its unit-background mixed
coefficient and parity grid duplicate C-SG-019. Its exact inverse-square
factorial values, decimal floors, positive-versus-machine-zero distinction,
and exposed decade ceilings survive after normalization and quantifiers are
repaired by C-CMB-001.

The source tally is not a universal oracle. Eight partial sums and four
moderate-order mpmath comparisons are finite regression evidence. Three
sixty-digit decade evaluations have no interval enclosure. The function named
`decays_superpolynomially` tests only order 21 and powers two, four, and eight.
C-CMB-001 replaces those underencoded predicates with the positive-series
argument, exact rational and integer decade proof, and a geometric half-tail
for every fixed nonnegative integer power.

The unit coefficient square is not normalization invariant. For the accepted
classical potential it is zero for even low order and equals
`A^2*a_H^2*a_L^(2*n)/(n!)^2` for odd order at zero background. Changing the
background changes the parity support. WN1's `1/(n!)^2` table is the unit
specialization, not a universal transition weight.

The rate and physical-band readings are unaccepted. PN2 is qualified quotient
bookkeeping with no accepted physical subdivision mode or band. C-SPN-002
explicitly states that a squared ladder coefficient is not a rate. WN1
supplies no normalized quantum states, complete interaction, on-shell measure,
spectral density, linewidth, or Golden-rule validity regime. Zero interaction
and zero spectral-density countermodels preserve the coefficient square while
making a conditional rate vanish.

Every runtime family is adjudicated in `evidence/check-adjudication.yaml`.
WN1 maps C-SG-019 and C-CMB-001. It does not promote a physical subdivision
process, transition rate, branching weight, PN2 band, material mechanism, or
any of the twelve pending reverse consumers. All thirteen sources replay 568
checks and have no legacy NumPy quadrature surface.
