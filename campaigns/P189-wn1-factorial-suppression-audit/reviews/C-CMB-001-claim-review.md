# C-CMB-001 Claim Review

## Claim Under Review

C-CMB-001 is the exact positive-integer inverse-square factorial theorem and
its normalization-complete application to C-SG-019's zero-background
one-high cosine coefficient. It owns the recurrence, strict exponential
ceiling, every-fixed-power geometric tail, exact decade ceiling, and
exact-versus-machine representation boundary. It does not type the coefficient
square as a matrix element, rate, probability, or physical subdivision law.

The maximum verdict is symbolic verified. The primary route passes 67 checks
and the independent route passes 27. The universal arguments are analytic:

reciprocal-gap identity fixes its direction, and a consecutive ratio bounded

conditional Golden-rule expression vanishes. Exact `q_171` remains positive
when the corresponding machine calculation produces zero.

## Framework Compatibility

The claim is a compatible extension with dependency C-SG-019. It reuses the
accepted cosine API rather than restating the coefficient theorem and adds no
dimensionful constant, fitted parameter, physical state, material, or
comparator. The positive sequence `q_n` is defined for every positive integer;
its cosine application is zero at even low order and uses `q_n` only on the
odd nonzero subsequence. Background and coordinate normalization remain
explicitly load-bearing.
