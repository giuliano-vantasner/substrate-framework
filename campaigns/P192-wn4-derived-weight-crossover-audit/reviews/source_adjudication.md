# WN4 Source Adjudication

WN4 is a mixed source and should be qualified rather than blanket-accepted.

The exact adjacent ratio and complete mode set map C-OSC-001. The distinct
strict log-concavity, PGF, falling-factorial moments, geometric majorants, and
fixed-power decay map proposed C-CMB-003. WN4's normalized mean and variance
are retained as mathematical distribution moments only.

The following source edges are rejected or qualified:

- Strict log-concavity does not imply one mode at positive integer S; the modes
  are S-1 and S.
- The exact adjacent-step residual is S-(n+1), not S-n.
- The PN2 band checks do not evaluate the weight and supply no accepted claim.
- Mathematical growth and suppression limbs do not derive phase-space or
  energy-gap dynamics.
- The source does not define or prove that a power law interpolates across the
  turnover.
- A normalized Poisson mass does not derive a Poisson process or make S a
  medium's physical mean subdivision order.
- One finite interior-mode guard cannot select a universal physical family.

The recommended WN4 disposition is `qualified` with mappings C-OSC-001 and
C-CMB-003. WN5 through WN7 and MD1 through MD6 remain pending.
