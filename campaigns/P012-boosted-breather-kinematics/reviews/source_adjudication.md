# Source adjudication: HE1 boosted secant-scale bridge

## Decision

HE1 is qualified. Its field and rest-energy reconstruction are duplicate evidence
for `C-SG-001/002`; its exact secant behavior maps to `C-SG-006`; and P012
promotes the stronger Lorentz-vector statement as `C-SG-008`. The source's
“effective Planck” and “de Broglie guiding wave” names do not establish quantum
kinematics or a universal constant.

## Check-family audit

HE1.1–HE1.5 reproduce the accepted breather residual, one-time-slice Hamiltonian
integral, energy, and endpoint limits. These are duplicate evidence for
`C-SG-001/002`, not new claims or independent oracles beyond P001's existing
exact and independent derivations.

HE1.6 boosts the rest phase to `(Omega,k)=(gamma*omega,gamma*omega*v)` and checks
its phase velocity and derivative along the Lorentz dispersion family. HE1.7

`(E,P)=H(omega)*(Omega,k)` and both invariant norms. This remains valid at
`v=0`, where the source's `P/k` expression is undefined.

HE1.8 defines `lambda=2*pi/k` and rewrites the accepted spatial proportionality.
It is exact algebra once `C-SG-008` is known, not an independent derivation of a
quantum wavelength law.

HE1.9–HE1.13 compute the secant scale, two exact anchors, its derivative and
range, and the failure of a fixed scale across the family. These map to the
stronger global classification `C-SG-006`; the anchor decimals are regression
coverage. The source itself correctly rejects universality, despite retaining
the symbol `hbar_eff`.

HE1.14 mutates the boosted frequency and momentum. The failures are preserved
as sensitivity evidence for `C-SG-008` and are strengthened by phase-sign,
frequency, energy-normalization, and boost-factor mutations in P012.

## Exact qualification

Accepted mappings are `C-SG-001`, `C-SG-002`, `C-SG-006`, and `C-SG-008`.
HE1 is terminally qualified because its Lorentz kinematics and family-dependent
scale are fully represented, while the Planck/de Broglie physical terminology
does not derive quantization, universality, or a quantum ontology. The exact
component and invariant identities do not depend on those names.
