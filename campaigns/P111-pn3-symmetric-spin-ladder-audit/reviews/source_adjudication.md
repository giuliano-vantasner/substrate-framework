pinned source hash. It uses exact SymPy algebra and has no NumPy or trapezoidal
compatibility event.

the ground-edge raise is `hbar*sqrt(N)`, the N=1 coefficient is `hbar`, and a
linear-in-N candidate is wrong at that edge. P111 retains those statements at
their exact conditional ceiling.

source's broad rejection of linear amplitude scaling is false outside its

the ladder coefficient unchanged and makes a conditional rate zero.

and phases give a pure symmetric bright vector and square-root ground-edge

complete one-excitation norm remains nonzero.
