# PN2 source adjudication

PN2 is qualified. For declared positive commensurate energies, its floor
quotient and remainder are exact conditional bookkeeping. P110 independently
rederives them with Fractions and scaled integer `divmod`, supplies the sharp
inverse plateaus `Omega/(n+1)<omega<=Omega/n`, identifies the one-sided jumps,
and preserves the common-scale covariance and rectangular-input count bounds.

The seven named inputs give exactly 24,000,000 through 24,000,000,000, not the
sharp endpoints of the source's looser 10^7-to-10^11 envelope. Thirty meV gives
800,000,000 for the supplied rounded 24 MeV total. These are external-input
coordinates, not framework predictions. The source converts them to binary
floats before flooring. Although its selected divisions happen to close,
`floor(0.3/0.1)=2` in binary float while the exact decimal quotient is three;
exact representation is therefore load bearing near thresholds.

The quotient does not construct a subdivision process. It is the maximum
number of equal declared units with a residual. General final states may have
unequal energies, and even equal-unit kinematics supplies no operator, initial
or final state, matrix element, occupation, overlap, selection rule, phase
space, resonance, linewidth, probability, rate, branching, deposition, or
material realization. A zero matrix element leaves the quotient unchanged and
the transition rate zero.

PN1 and FS4 do not repair this gap. C-SG-019 expressly limits PN1 to a
normalization-dependent classical local coefficient. FS4 terminally duplicates
constant-offset derivative cancellation. A nonzero cosine derivative retains
a free amplitude; its squared magnitude can approach zero or infinity and does
not prove that modulation carries the entire energy budget.

The primary paper is also narrower and differently worded than PN2 claims.
arXiv:2501.08338v2 contains no explicit retraction. Its direct coefficient
changes from `0.0362` to `0.0362+i*0.000477`: the imaginary-to-real ratio is
1.3177 percent, but the magnitude changes only 0.00868 percent. The paper still
states that pathway-interference changes can produce a large acceleration of
the excitation-transfer rate. P110 neither accepts nor refutes that model; it
records that PN2's retraction summary is not a faithful substitute for it.

All fourteen static sites, 25 runtime predicates, narrative claims, and fifteen
pinned consumers are covered. P090 already established the same mathematical
floor/remainder ceiling without a claim or API, so P110 adds no duplicate
canonical object and leaves v0.92.0 unchanged.
