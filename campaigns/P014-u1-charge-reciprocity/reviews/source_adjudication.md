# Source adjudication: EM1 and HE3 conditional U(1) charge bridges

## Decision

EM1 and HE3 are each qualified. EM1's general conditional current theorem maps
to `C-U1-001`, while its declared-profile charge and exact energy composition
map to `C-U1-002`. HE3's exact product, drift cancellation, boosted algebra,
and exponent-kernel classification also map to `C-U1-002`. Neither source
derives the complex-field ontology, a potential whose equation produces the
profile, a physical electric-charge map, or a universal quantum relation.

## EM1 check-family audit

EM1.1–EM1.3 correctly compute `j^0=2*omega*f^2`, zero stationary flux, and
kinematic time-independence for `Psi=f(x)*exp(-i*omega*t)`. These checks alone
do not establish a general Noether theorem. EM1.4 supplies the required
arbitrary-field off-shell identity. `C-U1-001` states it with raised current
components, so the spatial metric sign is explicit rather than split between a
lower spatial expression and the divergence operator.

EM1.5 and EM1.5b correctly establish on-shell conservation when
`Box(Psi)=F(|Psi|^2)*Psi` has real `F`, and reject a complex restoring
coefficient. EM1.8a–b independently show the nonzero leakage
`-2*lambda*f^2*sin(2*omega*t)` from a phase-breaking conjugate-field term.
These are symmetry-sensitive evidence for `C-U1-001`, conditional on the
declared complex scalar theory.

EM1.6a–d correctly integrate the declared `sech` profile and correct the older
power error: `Q=4*omega/sqrt(1-omega^2)`, not
`4*omega/(1-omega^2)`. `C-U1-002` restores the suppressed amplitude and records
`Q=4*A^2*omega/sqrt(1-omega^2)`. EM1.7 correctly proves a real field has zero
current. EM1.9 gives the exact conditional composition `Q*E=64*omega` at
`A=1`; the accepted statement is `64*A^2*omega`. The numerical anchor is
regression coverage, not independent evidence.

EM1.10 differentiates the accepted real breather profile and observes a shared
`sech` envelope. That shape match does not identify the independently declared
complex profile with the real sine-Gordon field or prove the profile's complex
equation of motion.

## HE3 check-family audit

HE3.1–HE3.5 correctly rederive the declared-profile charge, form
`Q*H=64` at `A=1`, and show exact opposite logarithmic drifts. `C-U1-002`
retains `A` and therefore promotes the normalization-honest form
`Q*H=64*A^2`. The frequency-independence is exact; the numeral is not invariant
under profile normalization.

HE3.6 correctly composes the accepted boosted vectors algebraically. The
promoted statement is conditional on carrying the same internal scalar charge
through the boost and reads `Q*(E,P)=64*A^2*(Omega,k)`. Calling this a universal
de Broglie law would add quantum ontology and a physical charge interpretation
that the equations do not supply.

HE3.7–HE3.9 correctly solve the exponent equations for monomials in
`(Q,H,E,omega)`: every integer kernel member is
`(a,b,a-b,b-a)` and is generated by `Q*H` and `Q*E/omega`. Their ratio is the
definition `H=E/omega`, so the classification is exact but does not establish
two new independent conservation laws. HE3.10 correctly exposes the amplitude
normalization and is retained as a load-bearing ceiling rather than a footnote.

## Exact qualifications

EM1 maps to `C-U1-001` and `C-U1-002`. It is terminally qualified because the
complex enrichment, named real potential, profile self-consistency, physical
electric-charge identification, and 3+1 stability are not derived. The accepted
claims are conditional mathematical statements and do not close those objects.

HE3 maps to `C-U1-002` together with `C-SG-006` and `C-SG-008`. It is terminally
qualified because its exact normalization-aware composition survives, while
the source's universal-constant, effective-Planck, and quantum de Broglie
interpretations do not follow from its accepted dependency closure.
