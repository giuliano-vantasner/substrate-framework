# S2 source adjudication

S2 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Its immutable source passes ten
predicates under an isolated compatibility alias backed by `np.trapezoid`, but
the tally combines a conditional radial profile, imported collective-rotor
premises, an incomplete fixed-wall Hessian, a fitted physical splitting, and
two previously accepted algebraic identities.

The declared massless radial energy and ODE are the C-MOD-001 model. The source
reproduces a nontrivial profile and useful finite-difference stationarity
evidence, as already governed by C-MOD-002. Its inertia integration, however,
discards the core below 0.3 and the massless tail above 12. The stated upper-tail
comparison cannot test those omissions. Completing the same positive functional
with the leading analytic tail gives 6.372322514, 6.372339919, and 6.372341818
at outer walls 12, 18, and 24, rather than S2's truncated 5.8853.

S2's fluctuation operator is not the second variation it describes. Before
integration by parts the quadratic density contains `A eta'^2+B eta eta'+D
eta^2`; the self-adjoint potential is `C=D-B'/2`. S2 uses only D and incorrectly
says stationarity removes the mixed quadratic term. With the accepted complete
Hessian and physical origin, the lowest finite-box levels are 0.131132401,
0.061072240, and 0.034754127 as the outer wall grows from 12 to 24. Their
wall-squared products stabilize, all levels remain above the exact zero
continuum edge, and algebraic residuals stay below 3e-9. This is a discretized
continuum ladder, not a bound breathing mode.

The rotational arithmetic is exact only after importing
`Hrot=J(J+1)/(2I)`, the one-half and three-halves labels, and the
Finkelstein-Rubinstein constraint. S2 derives none of them from a collective
action. Its 293 MeV predicate also bypasses the solved shape functional: it
sets `Lambda=5.12 GeV^-1`, a value fitted to the same N-Delta splitting, and
then evaluates `3/(2 Lambda)`. For any positive target, choosing
`Lambda=1500/target` reproduces it. The result is a comparator round trip, not a
parameter-light prediction.

The machine-epsilon EOM residual substitutes the ODE right-hand side into the
same equation; the finite-difference residual is the actual numerical check.
Likewise, subtracting 50 times the generalized mass matrix shifts every
eigenvalue by minus 50 by construction. The shared `E0/sqrt(N)` notation is one
scalar identity and supplies no intertwiner between fluctuation operators.
Check ten is exactly the conditional C-SK-001 mass matching.

Primary spectral literature treats radial Roper candidates as resonance or
quasi-normal-mode problems and distinguishes discrete modes from an infinite
scattering continuum. S2 computes no phase shift, outgoing-wave pole, width,
complete angular-channel operator, or vacuum small-field mass matrix. It also
supplies no action/time scale, quantization rule, or physical particle map.

Decision: qualify S2 through C-MOD-001, C-MOD-002, C-SCL-001, C-SG-002, and
C-SK-001 under their exact ceilings. Do not promote provisional C-MES-001.
Reject the positive-bound-mode, Roper, baryon-normal-mode, meson-spectrum, and
fitted-splitting prediction readings. The positive deliverable is the accepted,
independently verified radial composition and explicit spectral typing; no
unresolved debt remains within S2's adjudication scope.
