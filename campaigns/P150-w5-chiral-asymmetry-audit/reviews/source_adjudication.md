# W5 source adjudication

W5 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Native execution exits cleanly with
all twenty-seven source checks passing and no NumPy integration compatibility
event. That tally reproduces its rational algebra but does not validate its
boundary sign, wave orientation, action derivation, or weak-sector headline.

On the declared domain `x >= 0`, `exp(i(kx-omega*t))` travels toward increasing
`x` and is outgoing from the boundary at zero; `exp(i(-kx-omega*t))` is
incoming. W5 calls them the reverse. For canonical scalar energy on this
right half-line, the boundary contribution is `-c^2*phi_t*phi_x`. The displayed
condition `phi_t+zeta*phi_x=0` with positive `zeta` therefore injects energy.
The passive condition is `phi_t-zeta*phi_x=0`.

Correctly oriented incoming and reflected harmonics under that passive
condition give `z=zeta/c`, amplitude `r=(z-1)/(z+1)`, reflected power
`R=r^2`, and conditional absorbed complement `T=1-R=4z/(1+z)^2`. W5 reaches
the same amplitude because its reversed wave roles and active sign each invert
the amplitude ratio; the two errors cancel. This is why its formula checks
pass while its physical interpretation fails.

The cited piston equations do not repair the derivation. Eliminating
`phi_x=-lambda*psi_dot` from
`mu*psi_ddot+V'=lambda*phi_t+J` gives
`phi_t+(mu/lambda^2)*partial_t(phi_x)=(V'-J)/lambda`. The inertial derivative
remains even when drive and potential are set to zero. A boundary-only action
also cannot fix the bulk variational trace without the bulk action and domain
normal.

The power data have an exact identifiability ceiling. Reciprocal impedances
`z` and `1/z` flip the sign of `r` while leaving `R` and `T` unchanged. W5's
third formula additionally sets a separate reference reflection to one. Its
contrast is `(1-R)/(1+R)=2z/(1+z^2)=T/(2-T)`, a deterministic C-BRN-001
allocation transform, not an independent third prediction. Equal assigned
channels give zero arithmetic contrast without establishing parity symmetry,
and the same scalar ledger can be given different current, charge, detector,
or interaction semantics.

Decision: promote C-SCT-001 and qualify W5 through the corrected conditional
scattering theorem and C-BRN-001. Do not promote the displayed active boundary
as absorptive, the piston-to-local-impedance derivation, three independent
observables, physical chirality, intrinsic parity violation, V-A or weak
interaction, detector measurement, numerical weak scale, or substrate
mechanism. Primary, fresh independent, focused, and graph routes pass 32, 17,
15, and 46 checks; the graph pins 154 source predicates and fourteen
assertions, with no unresolved campaign debt.
