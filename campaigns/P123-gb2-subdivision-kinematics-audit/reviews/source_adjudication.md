# GB2 Source Adjudication

## Exact result

GB2's conditional quotient and remainder arithmetic is exact for a
nonnegative total and positive same-unit divisor. P123 adds the omitted zero
quotient, uniqueness, exact plateaus, left continuity, right downward jumps,
binary-representation ceiling, finite mean error, and limiting-path split.

For positive quotient, the arithmetic mean is the declared unit plus the
remainder divided by the quotient. Its relative excess is below one over the
quotient. A fixed-unit, increasing-total path approaches that fixed unit; a
fixed-total, shrinking-unit path only makes the moving mean-to-unit ratio tend
to one.

## Source oracle

The source executes nineteen predicates from eleven static sites. Its rational
fraction and bracket examples reproduce, but do not prove the general theorem.
Its four mean checks use binary floats. The seven-point monotonicity and guard
are finite regression: an overfit lookup table passes, while the valid floor on
one quotient plateau fails the guard's unnecessary variation clause.

The frozen proposal incorrectly called the divisor staircase right-continuous.
Attempt 0003 preserves that defect; the active contract corrects it to
left-continuous without rewriting the immutable freeze.

## Physical interpretation

The identity is bookkeeping, not a constructed energy-conserving transition.
An arithmetic mean does not identify individual constituent energies; unequal
partitions can have the same total and count. A zero matrix element preserves
the quotient and gives zero rate, while common energy scaling preserves the
count and moves the alleged characteristic energy.

GB2 therefore supplies no quantum state, mode count operator, interaction,
matrix element, phase space, resonance, linewidth, probability, rate, phonon
identity, emitted spectrum, soft channel, material response, yield, heat, or
observation.

## Dependencies and consumers

PN2 is terminally qualified arithmetic evidence with no accepted claim. GB2
names GB5 as the intended spectral use, while GB5 names GB2 as its premise;
that candidate cycle grants no authority. GB5, GB6, and WN7 reproduce 101
checks, but GB5 merely relabels the free unit as a spectral peak and the latter

## Decision

P090 and P110 already govern the exact floor-and-remainder ceiling. GB2's mean
error and relative limit are immediate consequences and have no accepted
consumer requiring a package API. GB2 is terminally qualified without a new
claim, module, or release. Exact conditional arithmetic remains campaign
evidence; the physical subdivision and spectral readings are rejected.
