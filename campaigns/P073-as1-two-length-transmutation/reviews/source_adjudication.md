# P073 Source Adjudication: AS1

AS1 executes successfully, but several assertions do not test their headline
semantics and the source reverses its own physical length labels.

## Retained Mathematical Content

Two length columns and one speed column have rank two and a one-dimensional
dimensionless-monomial kernel generated by a length ratio. C-RGE-001's formal
positive-`b0` scale gives `Lambda/mu0=exp(-X)` with `X>0`. Under a separately
declared common inverse-energy conversion, the lower energy maps to the longer
length and the length ratio is the reciprocal energy ratio. Supplying one such
ratio relation fixes one relative log coordinate and leaves common rescaling.

## Structural Defects

The opening prose calls `a` a UV lattice length and `xi` an IR soliton length,
which requires `xi/a>1`. The executable then defines `xi=hbar*c0/mu0` and
`a=hbar*c0/Lambda`, so it instead obtains `a/xi>1`. Check AS1.2c calls that
`a/xi` expression exactly the earlier `xi/a` group but merely checks that no
dimensionful symbols remain. The reciprocal mismatch therefore passes.

The alleged reduced parameter set `{a,beta^2}` still contains `b0`; fixing it
requires separately declared loop weights, matter content, and flavor count.
More basically, `beta^2` remains free. A nonzero derivative with respect to a
free input shows functional dependence, not prediction. Solving `beta^2` from
a supplied ratio is an inverse inference and is positive only for `R>1`.

## Rejected Interpretations

Neither the accepted one-loop theorem nor dimensional rank identifies `mu0`
and `Lambda` with lattice and soliton energies, supplies a common Compton
conversion, derives QCD or confinement, selects `b0` or `beta^2`, fixes either
length, or removes their common scale. Later AS4/AS6/AS7, OD, G-sector, and S5
narratives provide no accepted premise. AS1 therefore cannot close a physical
two-length debt or establish an operating point.
