# NY1 Source Adjudication

NY1 is duplicate evidence for C-SK-001. Its exact conditional equality is
valid, but its zero-import nuclear-scale, proton-prediction, quantum-correction,
and engine-yield interpretations are not accepted.

The legacy executable exits zero with `ALL 9 CHECKS PASS`. Exact canonical and
independent routes both derive
`M_top=M_ANW` if and only if `F_pi/e=16*pi*E_e`, and substitution proves the
reverse implication. The linear `B1` factor cancels. C-SK-001 has already owned
this exact statement, its positive-premise ceiling, reusable APIs, focused
tests, and prefactor/power mutations since P008. NY1 adds no distinct theorem,
API, formal object, or consumer.

The cancellation does not eliminate the measured electron energy. The result
has free-symbol set `{E_e}`, derivative `16*pi` with respect to that input, and
zero limit when the input vanishes. Its 25.6856 MeV value appears only after the
source inserts `M_E_MEV=0.51099895`. A lossless dimensionless reparameterization
would retain one free mass coordinate, exactly as C-DIM-003 warns. The source's
“zero free params” and “no import” labels therefore contradict its visible
dependency rather than classifying it.

The reported 936.96 MeV proton closure is also the defining equality evaluated
after solving it. It holds for every positive `B1` and `E_e`, so its loose
900--970 MeV band does not independently validate either mass premise. More
generally, matching `a*pi^3*B^p*E` to `c*pi^2*B^q*X` gives
`X=(a/c)*pi*B^(p-q)*E`: equal powers cancel `B`, while both prefactors and the
remaining energy input stay load bearing.

After the preregistered structural verdict was frozen, the 34.1 MeV comparator
gave the stated 24.68% gap. The primary ANW record says calculated static
baryon properties are generally within about 30% of experiment; it does not
derive a universal 30% multiplicative quantum correction to this scale. NY1
itself explicitly computes no correction. A free factor in
`Y=kappa*16*pi*E_e` realizes every positive target, including both 34.1 MeV and
the engine's 24 MeV with different comparator-derived values of `kappa`.

B1, S2, and NY2 remain pending with empty accepted mappings. NY2 is a later
consumer and cannot close NY1; neither unit supplies a multi-soliton solution,
binding coefficient, physical state map, reaction energy, or engine
implementation here. Every source predicate has an individual verdict in
`evidence/source-audit.yaml`. The campaign therefore retains NY1 as
noncanonical duplicate evidence, changes no accepted claim or release, and
uses no numerical quadrature or NumPy integration alias.
