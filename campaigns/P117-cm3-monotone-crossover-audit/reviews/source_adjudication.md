# CM3 Source Adjudication

CM3 is qualified. Its ten checks reproduce and its core mathematical insight
survives: continuity plus strict increase and an in-range horizontal level give
one crossing. The source's particular exponential inverse is also exact. The
runtime tally does not, however, establish the physical A/B crossover claimed
in the prose.

For `S(E)=1-exp(-E/E0)` on nonnegative E with positive E0, the exact range is
`[0,1)`. The lower level zero is attained at E=0, each `0<c<1` has the unique
positive inverse `-E0*log(1-c)`, and level one occurs only as an infinite-input
limit. Levels outside the range have no finite crossing. The inverse increases
and is convex in c, scales linearly with E0, diverges as c approaches one, and
is covariant under common energy rescaling. The reported `log(2)` value is only
the specialization E0=1 and c=1/2.

The surrogate is not CM1's actual accepted factor. C-SCR-001's positive-shift
factor has finite floor `exp(-sqrt(G/U))>0`, not zero, and its interior inverse
at level c is `G/log(c)^2-U`. A positive crossing exists exactly when c exceeds
that floor. Its derivatives with respect to c and G are positive, its shift
derivative is -1, zero shift recovers the zero-floor limit, and common energy
rescaling is covariant. These identities recover the conditional formula cited
from CM7 without using pending CM7 as authority.

The physical interpretation remains rejected. C-CMP-001 does not supply a flat
coherent-medium rate; its accepted factors vary with loss and retain a matrix-
element dimension. CM3 assigns no common observable, units, normalization,
states, interactions, or kinetic prefactors to its two curves. A zero physical
prefactor produces no channel, while a free positive prefactor or free E0 fits
any selected target. Formal curve ordering therefore proves neither coherent-
channel dominance nor screened-tunnelling sufficiency, and predicts no material
crossover, rate, yield, heat, or observation.

Strict monotonicity, continuity, and range are separately load bearing. A
plateau gives repeated crossings, a discontinuity can skip an interior level,
and a nonmonotone square gives two roots. These exact countermodels replace the
source's reliance on one sampled sinusoid and a dense grid.

C-XOV-001 is distinct from C-SCR-001 because it governs the general range
classification, the exponential inverse and sensitivities, and the conditional
horizontal-crossing semantics. Its shifted specialization depends on
C-SCR-001; no CM2 or CM7 premise enters the derivation. Promotion must retain
only this mathematical theorem and its explicit physical ceiling.
