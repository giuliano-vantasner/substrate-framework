# WN2 Source Adjudication

WN2 is qualified rather than accepted literally. Its unit-activity
parity-thinned mass is the `z=1` specialization of C-CMB-002. C-SG-019 owns
the zero-background parity and coordinate-normalization factors, C-CMB-001
owns inverse-square factorial suppression and exact decade ceilings, and
C-BRN-001 owns the conditional allocation algebra. C-CMB-002 newly owns the
declared positive-integer counting sample space, exact Bessel normalization,
rational odd-tail enclosure, and normalized concentration inequalities.

The source's 97.2% order-one and 99.99% through-three lower bounds are true,
but its proof is coarse. Thirty-six direct factorial evaluations do not encode
the stated induction. P190 instead derives the consecutive odd ratio and a
rational geometric enclosure. At cutoff nine, both inequalities have exact
positive margins. Decimal Bessel evaluations remain regression evidence only.

The physical headline does not survive. C-BRN-001's positive-weight
specialization rejects zero, while its general allocation intentionally
permits either zero endpoint. This API distinction is not a universal axiom
that every mathematical weight be positive at every ambient integer. A
normalized distribution may legitimately have mode one, and C-CMB-002's
declared activity is load-bearing: activity one has mode one whereas activity
four has mode three. Neither fact supplies or rejects a physical channel.

WN2 also mixes two incompatible readings without declaring their common
sample space: a normalized mass across all positive odd orders and PN2's
deterministic floor count at a fixed frequency. Order one failing the displayed
remainder inequality at selected values is exact arithmetic, but it is not a
self-contradiction of the normalized mathematical distribution. PN2 has no
accepted physical band, material mode, or order-exclusion claim.

R1's floor parity sensitivity survives as arithmetic; “physically absurd”
does not. R2's conditional `N/(n!)^2` substitution survives; GB4 does not
require it to exceed one for every supplied weight and population. R3's exact
concentration survives; its physical contradiction does not. R4's guard is
rejected because positivity at every integer and a mode away from one are not
accepted universal criteria, ties are assigned by finite-grid order, and its
passing example imports pending WN3/WN4 semantics.

WN3 and WN7 repeat the rejected guard, and WN6 repeats the overbroad verdict.
All twelve sources replay 524 checks with no legacy NumPy quadrature surface,
but the eleven consumers remain individually pending. WN2 maps C-SG-019,
C-CMB-001, C-CMB-002, and C-BRN-001; it promotes no physical coefficient
weight, PN2 band, WN3/WN4 family, or downstream medium claim.
