# C-OVL-005 Claim Review

## Claim Under Review

C-OVL-005 states a finite translated-family limit. For a normalized complex
mode in L2 of the real line, a bounded continuous multiplier vanishing at
infinity, finitely many mutually separating centers, and unit phase weights,
the matrix formed by summing every phase-weighted translated triple overlap
approaches the common positive self-overlap times the diagonal phase matrix.
Every singular value approaches that self-overlap. At finite separation, an
operator-norm residual epsilon below the self-overlap bounds every singular
value within epsilon and bounds the condition number by the endpoint ratio.
The claim derives no physical mass, hierarchy, species, generation, Yukawa
interaction, selected center, or substrate object.

## Independence

The independent route imports no translated-overlap implementation. It
completes the square for normalized Gaussian translates, derives the exact
three-center exponent, takes every matrix-entry limit, derives equal singular
values from the limiting Gram, and separately constructs a perturbation that
attains both singular-value endpoints.

## Verification Status

The claim earns symbolic verification. Translation leaves the matched
self-overlap exact; displaced density-multiplier convolutions vanish because an
L1 density is convolved with a C0 multiplier; off-diagonal terms vanish by the
translated L2 correlation bound; finite dimension converts entrywise
convergence to operator-norm convergence. Singular-value perturbation then
gives the displayed uniform interval and condition bound. The exact Gaussian
family is an independently soluble witness. GC5's finite-grid result is
regression evidence, not an independent proof.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Unequal self-overlaps produce unequal limiting singular values. A residual at
or above the self-overlap destroys the positive lower bound. Independent
amplitudes, profiles, widths, phases with nonunit magnitude, nonseparating
centers, or an unbounded/nondecaying multiplier leave the theorem's scope. A
diagonal perturbation attains both endpoints, showing the bound is sensitive
rather than ceremonial.

## Framework Compatibility

The theorem is a compatible extension of C-OVL-001's normalized expectation
and C-OVL-002's translated overlap structure. It adds no measured value or
fitted separation. The whole-line Cartesian measure and L2 normalization are
explicit, and the finite count is supplied rather than selected.
