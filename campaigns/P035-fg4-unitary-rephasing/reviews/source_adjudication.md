# FG4 Source Adjudication

FG4 is qualified. Its symbolic budget, two-dimensional factorization,
three-angle unitary, quartet formula, and rephasing cancellation map to the
abstract `C-MIX-002` theorem. Its removable-phase count is only the generic
connected-support orbit dimension, and its physical KM/CP interpretation lacks
accepted fields, spectra, interactions, and nondegeneracy premises.

## FG4.1 and FG4.2: Counts and Rephasings

The polynomial identity is correct: `dim U(N)=N^2`, `dim O(N)=N(N-1)/2`, and a
generic effective left/right diagonal phase orbit has dimension `2N-1`, leaving
`(N-1)^2` quotient dimensions and `(N-1)(N-2)/2` beyond the real angles. FG4
does not derive the action's stabilizer. For a unitary matrix, the stabilizer
dimension equals the number of connected components in the bipartite nonzero-
support graph. Dense support has one component, a `2+1` block has two, and a
permutation matrix has `N`; the corresponding orbit dimensions are `2N-c`.
The accepted count is therefore explicitly generic, not universal pointwise.

## FG4.3 and FG4.6: Two Dimensions

Every two-by-two unitary is diagonal-rephasing-equivalent to a real orthogonal
matrix, including zero-pattern limits, and every quartet imaginary part
vanishes. This is accepted as matrix algebra. It says only that this particular
diagonal-rephasing quotient has no irreducible complex phase. Calling it a
theorem that every physical two-generation theory conserves CP would require a
specific field content, interaction, and CP action not present in the accepted
framework.

## FG4.4: Three-Angle Parameterization

The declared product `R23 R13(delta) R12` is exactly unitary. Direct
multiplication gives the stated quartet imaginary part
`c12*c23*c13^2*s12*s23*s13*sin(delta)`, and its algebraic zero limits are
correct. The parameterization is a declared coordinate chart, not a prediction
of angles, phase, family number, or a CKM matrix. An independent normalized
three-dimensional Fourier matrix supplies a nonzero quartet without using the
source parameterization.

## FG4.5: Quartet Invariance

The quartet `V_ij V_kl conjugate(V_il) conjugate(V_kj)` is invariant under
`V -> D_L V D_R^dagger`; its imaginary part changes sign under complex
conjugation. A missing conjugation or mismatched index destroys phase balance.
This exact invariant is accepted. It is not automatically invariant under the
larger unitary basis freedom available in degenerate singular subspaces from
`C-MIX-001`, so a physical CP reading additionally needs nondegenerate masses
or an appropriate basis-independent construction.

## FG4.7 and Physical Headline

The source guard correctly rejects `N(N-1)/2` as the residual-phase count.
FG4 nevertheless imports FG3's rejected CKM identity, a standard physical
parameterization, quark rephasing freedom, charged-current structure, and a
physical CP interpretation. It declares `N`, angles, and `delta`; it neither
derives observed CP violation nor proves a substrate KM mechanism. Its claim
that CP violation requires a third generation is retained only as historical
source language, not as an accepted framework statement.

## Terminal Disposition

FG4 maps its generic quotient and invariant mathematics to `C-MIX-002` and is
otherwise qualified. Excluded scope includes a physical CKM matrix, quark or
generation map, observed family count, Cabibbo/KM mechanism, physical CP
operation or violation, charged current, GIM, anomaly result, measured angles
or phase, and substrate identity. Durable evidence is the P035 verifier,
independent review, source reproduction, and this adjudication.
