# P100 BD2 source adjudication

BD2 reproduces all sixteen literal checks, but the terminal tally does not
validate its advertised operating optimum or global loading and drive signs.
Its normalized two-level occupation and gate are exactly C-TH-001. Conditional
on separately declaring positive energy quantum `q` and thermal-energy
coordinate `vartheta`, the coth scale
`Theta=q*coth(q/(2*vartheta))/2` has the displayed low- and high-temperature
limits. Those facts survive without a physical medium or bath interpretation.

The displayed rate becomes exact after one capillary elimination. From
`E=pi*tau^2/p`,
`tau/sqrt(p*Theta)=sqrt(E/(pi*Theta))`, so a supplied positive attempt
frequency gives
`R=nu*sqrt(E/(pi*Theta))*exp(-E/Theta)*W`. This is a dimensionful
conditional response family, not a stochastic escape derivation.

Put `u=tanh(q/(2*vartheta))` and `b=E/q`. Apart from positive constants the
source response is
`sqrt(u)*(1-u^2)*exp(-2*b*u)`. Its log derivative is
`1/(2*u)-2*u/(1-u^2)-2*b`; the derivative of that residual is strictly
negative on `0<u<1`. The residual tends to positive infinity at zero and is
`-2*b` at `u=1/sqrt(5)`, so there is exactly one global interior maximum with
`u_*<1/sqrt(5)`. Consequently
`vartheta_*>q/(2*atanh(1/sqrt(5)))`, approximately `1.039*q`. BD2's declared
`vartheta=q/2`, obtained merely by imposing `y=1`, is not stationary and cannot
be the maximum of its displayed rate.

The finite maximum is also premise dependent. Replacing the inverse-square-
root prefactor by an independently supplied constant gives reduced shape
`(1-u^2)*exp(-2*b*u)`, strictly increasing with thermal energy toward a finite
plateau and having no finite maximum. More generally a positive prefactor
exponent `a` gives one root below `sqrt(a/(a+2))`. Dimensions and the gate do
not select `a` or a physical objective.

BD2's loading and wavenumber checks sample one point. For the source prefactor,
the log elasticity in the barrier is `1/2-E/Theta`; if
`E proportional to z^-m`, the response elasticity is
`m*(E/Theta-1/2)`. The amplitude and fixed-q wavenumber use `m=2`, so their
signs reverse at `E/Theta=1/2`. A declared `q(k)` relation can reverse the total
wavenumber derivative as well. The source's sensitivity scan computes a
log-temperature gate slope; ordinary temperature sensitivity carries one more
factor of its reduced coordinate. Neither is the full-rate maximum.

The source's predecessor labels are not authority. Rungs 056, 096, and 097 do
not supply an accepted physical noise, breather-mode, dispersion, stochastic,
or optimization premise. BD3-BD5, CM2, and CM4 remain pending downstream
units. Their literal tallies reproduce, but BD5 does not optimize the gated
full rate. The DBD scaling and pipeline consumers fail under the current NumPy
because they call version-specific `np.trapz`; the accepted exact path imports
no NumPy or quadrature.

BD2 is therefore qualified. C-TH-002 accepts only the declared coth-gated
conditional response theorem, its exact maximum for the source prefactor, its
alternative-model ceiling, derivative regimes, and identifiability limits. It
derives no medium noise, breather or drive mode, physical nucleation rate,
Kramers process, DBD event, isotope effect, power, or operating recommendation.
