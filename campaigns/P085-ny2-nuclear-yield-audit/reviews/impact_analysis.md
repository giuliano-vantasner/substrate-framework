engine. It terminally classifies NY2 as duplicate evidence for C-SK-001.

## Direct Consumers

The direct framework consumers are the P085 primary verifier, independent
review, source audit, and unchanged C-SK-001 Skyrme tests. The hash-pinned C035
calculator/parity pair and separate engineering materials are read-only
predecessor consumers used to establish that the advertised replacement is
neither applied nor consistent. They are not mutated by this campaign.

consume NY2's terminal mapping. Repository validation, source and claim
inventory tests, durable proposal/decision memory, and the parent migration
effort change administratively. Registry, release, generated claim docs, and
accepted-state memory remain unchanged.

## Compatibility

C-SK-001, C-DIM-002, and C-DIM-003 retain their statements, four status axes,
APIs, tests, and release membership. No empirical binding energy, engine
constant, reaction coefficient, or branch probability enters the package.

consumer, `tests/test_skyrme_relations.py`, focused governance and inventory

no numerical integration; any future sampled soliton calculation must route

exothermicity, state map, radiative carrier, branching, deposition, engine and

Unconstructed objects are rejected or excluded, leaving no P085 debt.
