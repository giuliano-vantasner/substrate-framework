# MR5 Duplicate-Evidence Disposition Review

The preregistered candidates, all six source predicates, accepted owners,
canonical and fresh independent numeric routes, mutations, consumers, and
compatibility surfaces were reviewed separately. The disposition is
`duplicate_evidence`, not a new numeric claim.

The source's exact equation, coefficient scaling, and signed difference are
already C-GSK-001 and C-RDIFF-001. Its new coefficient point is generated by an
existing pure API from supplied empirical inputs and carries no new invariant,
method, state map, or predictive reach. Stronger recomputation repairs its
angular and boundary conventions but cannot convert those inputs into accepted
physics.

The bounded branch minimum near `e=4.3263` is reproducible and mutation
sensitive. It remains one continuation-selected stationary branch on `[3,7]`;
it proves neither uniqueness, global profile minimization, an all-positive-`e`
floor, nor a physical mass bound. The source's finite scan supports none of
those extensions.

The guard claim is refuted directly by executable comparator and physical-input
literals outside its needles. This is a source-claim failure, not a NumPy
compatibility event: MR5 uses current SciPy `trapezoid` natively.

The review therefore approves no claim identifier, API, test, or release
change. The source regression, failed shooting attempt, repaired independent
route, and all rejected interpretations remain durable campaign evidence.
