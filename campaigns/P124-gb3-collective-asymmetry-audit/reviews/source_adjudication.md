# GB3 Source Adjudication

## Exact result

GB3's normalized ground-edge coefficient and algebraic square are already
governed by C-SPN-002. Exact canonical and explicit-vector routes reproduce
`s*sqrt(N)` and `s^2*N` under the declared symmetric state and operator scale.
Middle rungs, unequal complex couplings, bright projection, total norm, and
dark remainder remain those of the stronger accepted theorem.

Deterministic site phases give a normalized bright norm
`|sum_j exp(i*phi_j)|^2/N`. For two sites this is `1+cos(phi)`, a smooth phase
factor rather than a binary spacing gate. C-COH-001 separately supplies iid
directional N and N-squared endpoints with explicit normalization.

## Gate and source oracle

All thirteen source predicates reproduce. The wavelength gate fails both
logical directions. With `lambda=2d`, axial phase difference pi cancels the
two-site bright sector although the source gate passes. With `d=2lambda`, the
phase is an integer multiple of `2*pi` and aligns although the gate fails;
transverse geometry also aligns at arbitrary spacing. Roots-of-unity arrays
cancel and extended phase-matched arrays remain bright.

The source's `hbar^2*N` has action-squared dimensions and lacks a Golden-rule
coupling and final-state density. Its exponent checks merely differentiate
expressions after the refuted binary gate assigns exponents.

## Input provenance

BIPM's exact SI defining constants independently give

code and never compared with the full participant cloud.

## Physical interpretation

An incoherent N-emitter directional total at fixed per-source normalization
scales as N, not generically N^0. A physical transition rate additionally
requires states, preparation, interaction, coupling, mode normalization,
energy conservation, final-state measure, spectral density, linewidth,
decoherence, and regime validity. Zero coupling or zero density leaves the spin
algebra intact and kills a rate.

No physical soft/gamma asymmetry, phonon coherence, nuclear transition,
material collective enhancement, magnitude, yield, heat, or observation is
accepted.

## Dependencies and consumers

PN3 maps to C-SPN-002 only. GB4 and GB6 replay 52 checks. The unchanged twelve-
script WN/MD transitive set was already hash-pinned and replayed for P122, so
P124 reuses those 524 checks rather than repeating ceremony. No consumer closes
the phase geometry or physical-rate premises.

## Decision

C-SPN-002 and C-COH-001 already subsume the exact reusable surface. The phase-
gate counterexamples and input audit qualify GB3 but have no distinct accepted
consumer or package API. GB3 is terminally qualified without a new claim,
module, or release.

## Compatibility

compatibility event occurs.
