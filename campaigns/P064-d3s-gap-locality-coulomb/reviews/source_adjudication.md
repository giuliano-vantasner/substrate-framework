# D3S Source Adjudication

D3S exits cleanly with `ALL 13 CHECKS PASS`, but its headline is not
established. The valid fragment is an exact low-momentum series for a
separately declared rational parameter kernel. The source does not derive that
kernel, a charged loop species, gauge action, nonzero leading coefficient,
fractional-term absence, spatial dimension, or electromagnetic dictionary from
accepted framework claims.

The pivotal data-flow defect occurs before the advertised exponent solve. D3S
defines the inverse propagator as `q2 - Pi(q2)`, thereby supplying a local bare
`q2` term, computes `Z=1-e2/(6*pi*m2)`, never requires `Z != 0`, and then assigns
`leading_power=2`. At the allowed exact tuning `e2=6*pi*m2`, the `q2` term
cancels and the next term is proportional to `q2**2`; the claimed unconditional
`s=1` verdict changes to `s=2`. A separately declared lower fractional term
also dominates every analytic correction. These are load-bearing
counterexamples, not tolerance effects.

The source's generality claims also exceed its checks. Two coefficients do not
establish the full series or radius; P064 derives the coefficient
`C*(-1)**(n-1)*(n!)**2/((2*n+1)!*m2**n)`, the exact finite remainder, and the
disk `|Q2|<4*m2`. A gap supports this expansion only after the integrand or a
convergent spectral measure is declared. It does not by itself establish
ultraviolet inverse moments or a nonzero first moment. The fixed-transfer
massless limit and zero-transfer-first limit do not commute.

The endpoint `1/(4*pi*r)` is a correct specialization of the general Riesz
kernel in the convention
`G(x)=integral d^d k/(2*pi)^d exp(i*k*x)/(A*(k^2)^s)`, for `r>0` and
`0<s<d/2`. D3S imports `d=3`, the Riesz dictionary, and the 1+1-to-3-dimensional
lift from pending or declared sources. Its `polyfit` leg constructs `r**(-1)`
before fitting it and therefore provides no independent evidence. P064 instead
derives the general gamma normalization via a Schwinger parameter and confirms
the three-dimensional endpoint with an independently regulated spherical
Fourier integral.

Candidate A is rejected as a physical derivation. Candidates B through E are
selected for claim-level review. D3S should be qualified: its exact conditional
series and Riesz specialization survive, while the gap-to-locality-to-Coulomb
closure and substrate electromagnetic interpretation do not.
