# MD1 Source Adjudication

MD1 reproduces all 27 native predicates. Its d3 per-branch continuum DOS,
gapless limit, radial-ball integral, fixed-cutoff gap independence, and cutoff
algebra survive under the explicitly declared continuum measure and target.

The headline requires correction. Accepted C-MED-003 and C-SG-018 are scalar
one-coordinate claims; D3S and QCD5 do not lift that medium to d3. Spatial
dimension does not determine mechanical branches. The continuum ball volume
is not an exact finite lattice-point count, `V/a^3` is not a derived integer
cell count, and target matching does not derive a microscopic Brillouin zone.
Total configuration-space modes also do not determine which modes participate
in WN6's interaction or their couplings.

C-DOS-001 positively retains and generalizes the exact mathematics with
independent dimension and branch inputs, an open-band DOS, exact declared-
measure integral, and target-count inversion. MD1 should be qualified through
C-MED-003, C-SG-018, and C-DOS-001 while its derived d3 medium, exact finite
rank, derived-cell, microscopic-cutoff, and participating-mode conclusions are
rejected.
