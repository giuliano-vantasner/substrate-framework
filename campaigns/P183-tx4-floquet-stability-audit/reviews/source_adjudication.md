# TX4 Source Adjudication

TX4 does not establish that a rotating B=2 Skyrmion is dynamically stable.
It supplies no full field action, rotating solution residual, linearized field
operator and domain, constraint or gauge slice, boundary data, spectrum,
Jordan audit, convergence study, or energy-momentum coercivity theorem. Its
unconditional terminal headline is not a checked composite predicate.

The co-rotating chain rule survives as the finite-dimensional conditional
theorem C-FLO-001. For `Q(t)=exp(tK)` and laboratory generator
`Q(t) B Q(t)^-1`, `x=Qy` gives `y_dot=(B-K)y`; at a period with `Q(T)=I`, the
monodromies agree. Stability remains separate: finite matrix powers are
bounded exactly when all multipliers lie in the closed unit disk and boundary
multipliers are semisimple. TX4's rotor matrix violates the second condition.

C-ROT-001 corrects that rotor reading. For principal inertias `(A,A,C)` with
`C>A`, the whole transverse equilibrium circle is stable as a set in body
angular-velocity space. A fixed member is not Lyapunov stable: an arbitrarily
small axial perturbation causes phase drift to an order-one distance. The
nonzero nilpotent linearization, defective unit monodromy, exact nonlinear
trajectory, and conserved set distance are derived independently. TX4's
ordinary density-inertia identity is exact but is not a Skyrme collective
rotational metric and does not use TX1's moment value.

C-RMAP-003 recovers the narrow shape result. Exact differentiation and full-
sphere integration give a stationary degree-two angular functional at
`R=z^2`, with Hessian eigenvalues zero five times, `pi`, `pi+16/3` twice, and
`7*pi+64/3` twice. The five kernel directions are independently derived
domain/target Möbius and phase tangents. This is an exact chart-local second-
variation theorem, not a full-field or dynamical stability result.

TX4's energy literals duplicate C-RPROF-002's corrected degree-two versus
degree-one ordering. They establish no fission threshold, barrier, or dynamics.
The token `M2` is an unused local variable, not a dependency on source M2.
TX5 remains separately pending and cannot validate TX4 retroactively.
