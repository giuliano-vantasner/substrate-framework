# C-ROT-001 Claim Review

## Exact Object

C-ROT-001 classifies the declared free oblate symmetric top in body angular-
velocity space. The transverse equilibrium is a circle. Every fixed member
has a nonzero rank-one nilpotent linearization and is not Lyapunov stable, but
the whole circle is stable as a set because its exact distance is conserved.
The ordinary density-inertia/STF relation is retained with an explicit warning
that it is not a Skyrme collective metric.

## Verification and Sensitivity

The primary and independent routes derive Euler's equations, equilibrium,
Jacobian, fundamental matrix, two invariants, a nonlinear exact nearby
trajectory, its order-one fixed-point drift witness, and the constant set
distance. The independent route also rederives the density-inertia identity.
Changing the oblate sign or STF normalization breaks the relevant verdict.

## Source Adjudication and Compatibility

TX4.2's unit multipliers survive but its stability conclusion does not. TX4.3
correctly observes bounded invariant components yet tests no distance to a
fixed equilibrium. TX4.4 proves an ordinary second-moment identity without
using TX1 and cannot identify that tensor with a field collective inertia.
The tennis-racket guards remain declared rigid-rotor regressions only.

## Verdict

Accept C-ROT-001 as `symbolic_verified`, `accepted`,
`compatible_extension`, and `active`, dependent on C-FLO-001 and explicitly
limited to the declared reduced rotor state space.
