# C-FLO-001 Claim Review

## Exact Object

C-FLO-001 is the finite-dimensional constant-generator rotating-frame theorem
and the complete exact matrix-power criterion. It proves the change of
variables, fundamental matrix, and periodic-frame monodromy, then separately
requires closed-unit-disk eigenvalues and semisimple boundary eigenvalues for
bounded monodromy powers. It is not an infinite-dimensional spectral theorem,
a field Floquet calculation, or nonlinear stability result.

## Verification and Sensitivity

The primary route exercises the canonical API on periodic nilpotent, bounded,
and exponentially growing systems. The independent route imports no new
stability API and rederives the frame chain rule, fundamental matrix, Jordan
powers, and exponential counterexample. A unit-multiplier Jordan mutation and
a positive-exponent mutation break boundedness while an exact diagonal unit-
circle matrix passes.

## Source Adjudication and Compatibility

TX4.1 retains only its coordinate chain rule; it does not construct a field
action, rotating solution, operator, domain, spectrum, or monodromy. TX4.2 is
the decisive counterexample to its own prose because the displayed nonzero
nilpotent generator has a defective unit multiplier and secular powers.
C-FLO-001 is a compatible additive theorem with no fitted or empirical input.

## Verdict

Accept C-FLO-001 as `symbolic_verified`, `accepted`,
`compatible_extension`, and `active`, with no supersession relationship.
