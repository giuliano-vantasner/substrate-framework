# P108 E5 source adjudication

E5 reproduces all five checks, but its positive content is conditional
arithmetic on supplied external data. The five binding-energy literals are
read by `Q_of`; those differences become `Qs`; and those values are divided by
the supplied denominator. The binding table is therefore a direct derivation
input to every displayed Q and ratio, contrary to the source's repeated label
"comparator-only, never derivation inputs." Exact arithmetic gives 23.848,
18.354, 17.590, and 8.683 MeV. The code supplies neither mass-table rows nor
uncertainties, and comparing rounded results against nearby rounded values is
not independent validation.

For any positive supplied release `Q_i` and denominator `U`, the only exact
coordinate statement is `r_i=Q_i/U`. Under `U -> a*U`, every coordinate becomes
`r_i/a`; only pairwise quotients `r_i/r_j=Q_i/Q_j` are scale free. For the four
source values, the literal `0.3<r_i<1` predicate holds for every denominator in
the nonempty interval

`max(Q_i) < U < (10/3)*min(Q_i)`.

The source value is one member of that continuum, not a uniquely selected
scale. Denominators 10 and 100 MeV fail the same bracket on opposite sides.
For each reaction, setting `U=Q_i` makes that reaction exactly one and uniquely
closest to one, so D+D's source ordering is conditional on the denominator it
is offered as evidence for. The additive ratio range rescales as `1/a`; the
scale-free multiplicative spread is simply
`max(Q)/min(Q)=23848/8683`, about 2.747. Neither statistic independently selects
`U`.

The sample contains four reactions chosen for positive release and alpha
products. Positivity is therefore a selection predicate, not a population
result. Adding a zero, negative, or distant positive entry defeats the source
statements, and no sampling frame, uncertainty model, exclusion rule, or
predeclared quantitative meaning of `O(1)` is supplied. The exact interval and
finite extrema survive as descriptions of this table only; the robust,
universal, controlling-scale, and independent-corroboration readings do not.

The reaction classification also fails on its own labels. D+T has an explicit
neutron product, yet the source calls all four fuels aneutronic. Its D+D to 4He
shorthand omits the gamma required by the radiative channel already audited in
P085, along with branching, rate, and deposition. The fourth predicate merely
checks that each preselected product list contains the string `4He` and has
positive Q. It does not test an unselected reaction or derive reaction
dynamics.

E2 cannot close the physical interpretation. C-RPROF-002 provides
resolution-bounded evidence for three stationary branches in a declared
reduced model and explicitly excludes an alpha particle, nucleus, physical
binding hierarchy, reaction, and yield. Algebraic or map degree four, baryon
number, nuclear mass number, a physical alpha particle, and product
multiplicity remain distinct. O1 is pending and concerns spin-1 polar
order-parameter topology; it supplies no authority here. The statement that
the reaction energy "is the drop" onto E2's branch is therefore rejected.

The fifth source guard correctly observes that the four supplied ratios are
not all within five percent of one. Its claimed anchor-independence check,
however, subtracts `F_PI_OVER_E` from the identical expression used to define
it. Nuclear binding values do not enter that expression, but it still contains
the empirical electron rest energy and the conditional C-SK-001 coefficient.
Non-use of one data table does not derive the denominator or make the
cross-reaction comparison an independent prediction.

E5 is qualified. It maps the exact conditional ratio and finite-table
arithmetic to the interpretation ceilings of C-DIM-002, C-DIM-003,
C-RDIFF-001, and C-SK-001 without changing those claims. It adds no canonical
API, claim, test, release, physical scale, reaction, state map, or yield.
