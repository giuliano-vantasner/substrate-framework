# CM5 Source Adjudication

CM5 is qualified through existing claims with no registry, release, or package
API delta. Its eighteen checks reproduce, and its elementary harmonic
calculus is exact, but it does not derive a coherent-to-electromagnetic channel,
absolute or total power, a voltage-slew seeding mechanism, or a shared heat
observable.

For
`delta_mu=mu_static+A*cos(Omega*t)+D*sin(Omega*t)`, the static term drops and
the exact squared third-derivative cycle average is
`Omega^6*(A^2+D^2)/2`. Thus a peak-amplitude cosine at
`Omega=2*omega_b` gives `(2*omega_b)^6*B^2/2`, not the later assembled source
expression without the one-half. CM5 computes the correct unit-cosine value
`32*omega_b^6` in one predicate. A proportionality constant may absorb the
factor, but then its units and normalization remain free and no absolute power
has been obtained.

This positive surface is already governed. C-GW-001 owns the exact conditional
STF harmonic third-derivative average and explicitly withholds a physical
radiation action, coupling, field solution, and flux. FS4 is duplicate evidence
only for time-independent offset cancellation. C-SG-017 owns the dimensional
breather frequency `omega_b=w*omega_0` under C-MED-003, not a material or EM
channel. A nonzero derivative and a common numeric frequency do not select an
electric dipole, magnetic dipole, quadrupole, antenna, radiation zone, angular
pattern, detector, or unitful coupling.

CM5 declares rather than derives
`B=b0*sqrt(N)*exp(-C/(A2*Theta))*Gamma`. Squaring it makes the source family
linear in N and increasing in positive A2 and Gamma by construction. The loss
dependence is not inherited from PN4 or CM2: C-RES-001's paired element vanishes
at both loss endpoints, while C-CMP-001's composite is strictly decreasing;
the CM5 family grows like `Gamma^2` without bound. Common labels
`{N,A2,Gamma}` therefore do not establish a common state, amplitude, rate,
heat observable, or cross-channel correlation.

The geometry step is likewise not total-power closure. CM5 never imports or
evaluates `spark_geometry`; it defines a helper that squares a clipped ring
count and assigns `Phi_iso` literals 1.0 and 0.1. C-COH-001 gives N-squared only
as the aligned fixed-per-source directional endpoint. Phase cancellation,
disorder, fixed-total normalization, aperture, angular integration, and
detector collection change the result. The cited geometry module itself says
focusing raises local intensity at the same total power and explicitly warns
not to multiply focal gain into its headline array factor. CM5's
`P_elec*Phi_iso*N_rings^2` therefore cannot be read as emitted total power.

The seeding predicate contradicts its imported finite-width kernel. At finite
positive `tau`, that Gaussian pair has
`chi_b(0)=2*A*exp(-(omega_b*tau)^2)>0`; it vanishes only as `tau` tends to
infinity. CM5's `<1e-9` check proves numerical suppression, not exact zero.
More importantly, CM5 calls only `chi_b`: it evaluates no voltage waveform,
`dV/dt` spectrum, population, constitutive response, interaction, or source
work. DC, ramp, sinusoidal, finite-window, and time-varying-medium cases differ;
finite records retain endpoint terms, and zero coupling gives zero seeding for
any slew.

The shared-frequency guard is a floating value comparison. An independently
constructed equal number passes, while equal frequency is compatible with
independent or zero electrical coupling. Zero coupling, arbitrary unitful
prefactors, alternative derivative functionals, phase disorder, finite
bandwidth, and independent heat couplings preserve the source's formal inputs
while changing its physical verdict.

The exact ledger is retained as P119 audit evidence rather than inflated into
a new claim. Mutable engineering support now uses `np.trapezoid` directly; no
CM5 scientific candidate failed for API compatibility. The electrical,
focusing, seeding, heat, material, magnitude, yield, and observation readings
remain unaccepted.
