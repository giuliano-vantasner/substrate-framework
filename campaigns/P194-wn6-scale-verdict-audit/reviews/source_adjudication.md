# WN6 Source Adjudication

WN6 reproduces all 32 native predicates with exit zero. Its exact normalized
cosine geometry, conditional `S=A^2` positive-amplitude inequality,
factorial-one logarithmic upper bounds, and equal-mode threshold algebra are
retained under explicit declarations.

The headline implications are not retained. The source calls `A` an RMS phase
but never defines an averaging measure, then uses it as a pointwise amplitude.
For a harmonic phase, peak squared and mean square differ by a factor of two.
The full cosine expansion is entire; `pi` is a barrier, not an approximation
validity boundary, and its quadratic relative error exceeds 59 percent.
PN2's physical scale band is external. A coordinate excursion across periods
is not a topological winding without spatial boundary data.

The proposed multi-mode intensity is a definition, not a derivation from an
accepted state. `M*A^2` can be written as one effective amplitude squared, so
the source's partial derivative does not prove separate mode-count
identifiability. A mode count or density of states is not the unique missing
physical premise; states, normalization, per-mode couplings, interactions,
complete channels, and rates remain absent.

C-OSC-002 positively replaces the hard cutoff with the global exact bounds
`0 <= x^2/2-(1-cos(x)) <= x^4/24`, the sufficient relative-error domain
`|x| <= sqrt(12*epsilon)`, and the exact harmonic peak/RMS conversion. WN6 is
therefore qualified, not refuted: its exact formulas map accepted claims while
its physical verdicts are rejected.
