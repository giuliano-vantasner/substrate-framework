# KI5 Source Adjudication

KI5's exact algebra survives, while its variational-premise and physical
narrative require narrower status. Native NumPy 2.5.1 execution passes all five
source checks through the lazy `np.trapezoid` branch.

KI5.1 derives the correct conditional error
`alpha*(n*delta_initial-delta_final)`. Independent nonnegative slacks realize
both signs, so separate upper estimates imply no one-sided bound on their signed
difference. C-RDIFF-001 already owns this exact theorem. Explicit coupled slack
relations recover conditional upper or lower bounds, and componentwise error
control gives convergent signed differences that may alternate around truth.

KI5 does not establish its upper-bound premises on the accepted framework
surface. C-RPROF-002 supplies resolution-bounded stationary branches and
expressly proves no local or global minimum, variational upper bound, or full
three-dimensional solution. The named HMS paper calls the rational-map fields
an ansatz and good approximations and states that the reduced energy can be
minimized, but it does not prove KI5's finite-wall unchecked BVP is a minimizer.

KI5.2 samples four width changes at each of two degrees. All eight source energy
differences are positive, but an exact polynomial counterfamily is positive at
those samples and negative at an unsampled width. The bridge also consumes
`solve_bvp` output without a success or residual gate and uses hard finite-wall
Dirichlet data rather than P105's accepted asymptotic Robin evidence.

KI5.3's two opposite-direction examples are valid finite evidence and agree
with the exact theorem. They refute a universal monotone one-sided rule; they do
not make controlled component improvement or convergence ill-posed. KI5.4 then
feeds 0.929 into both its direction and factor-five pass conditions. Changing
only that comparator flips the verdict. The stronger claims that proximity is
evidence of nothing under every error model and that physical overbinding
therefore stands are not established. KI5.5 correctly isolates checks 1-3 from
the comparator but hard-codes the final bound verdict.

The source reproduces stale hard-wall values `b(2)=2.4154`, `b(4)=4.5452`, and
`kappa=8.4574`. C-RDIFF-002 owns the corrected conditional coordinate
8.482417318795285 and explicitly denies a variational-bound, physical binding,
reaction, or empirical interpretation. The unchanged Lean theorem proves only
positive and negative witnesses for the abstract error `2*d2-d4`.

KI5 is therefore qualified through C-RDIFF-001, C-RDIFF-002, and C-RPROF-002.
No new claim, API, physical point estimate, comparator conclusion, or release is
accepted. MK5 and MR5 remain pending.
