# YM2 source adjudication

YM2 is qualified through existing accepted claims, not accepted wholesale.
Native execution aborts before its first check because Python eagerly evaluates
the legacy default in `getattr(np, "trapezoid", getattr(np, "trapz"))`. An
isolated compatibility alias backed by `np.trapezoid` reproduces all ten checks
and the terminal tally without editing the immutable source. This is a
version-only event and supplies no evidence for the lift headline.

The exact retained object is small. The Pauli-half trace metric is accepted
representation algebra. The scalar `d=3, s=1` Riesz inverse is the conditional
endpoint already owned by C-KRN-001. Their displayed tensor product and radial
derivative are exact once those inputs are independently supplied. The fixed
gamma, slope, finite-difference, and one-dimensional FFT values are duplicate
regressions rather than a map from the accepted two-dimensional loop theorem
to a four-dimensional gauge theory.

The central operator interpretation is reversed. YM2 multiplies an already
inverted scalar Green kernel by `Tr(T_a T_b)`. If that trace metric instead
weights a quadratic kinetic kernel, fixed-source inversion produces the inverse
metric. For the fundamental SU2 normalization, YM2's defined product is
`1/(8*pi*r)`, whereas the inverse of `(1/2) k^2` is `1/(2*pi*r)`. The two differ
by a factor of four. No declared source normalization repairs this because YM2
contains no source or probe at all.

The source also provides no gauge action, action variation, time operator,
signature, gauge fixing, causal or boundary prescription, current, kinetic
coefficient, dimension-selection rule, or nonlinear covariant fractional
operator. Its force label lacks a source-probe energy dictionary. Its Abelian
guard changes the trace factor manually while an `eps_self` switch remains
unused, so it changes conventions rather than taking a limit. Its FFT is a
one-dimensional `s=1/2` packet and does not test the claimed endpoint. The
docstring itself both calls the ceiling closed and states that the 3+1D
kinetic-term ceiling is not closed.

Decision: qualify YM2 through C-NVP-001, C-KRN-001, and C-KRN-002 without a
new claim, API, or release. Retain the trace metric, fixed-input scalar Riesz
kernel, defined tensor product, and exact derivative only under their accepted
types and ceilings. Do not promote a kinetic-kernel inversion, Yang--Mills
action, spacetime propagator, physical force, selected dimension or exponent,
weak sector, dimensional lift, or substrate mechanism.

The primary, fresh independent, and frozen-graph routes pass 31, 20, and 27
checks; 37 focused accepted-API tests also pass. The graph replays all 165
predicates in sixteen hash-pinned units. Compatibility remains isolated from
science, no canonical code changes, and campaign debt is empty at the
no-release boundary.
