# P164 SM2 Source Adjudication

SM2 exits cleanly and all seven implemented predicates pass. They validate
`Q=T3+Y` arithmetic for a fully supplied five-row table, the resulting supplied
state count, spectra of already diagonal matrices, a factor-two charge-coordinate
map, and one wrong-row guard. They do not derive a physical Standard Model
generation or its hypercharges.

The corrected positive object survives generically. For separately supplied
exact multiplet rows, the canonical ledger expands isospin weights with a
positive spectator multiplicity, derives grouped component charges, flattens
them into C-REP-001's trace ledger, and counts the supplied states. A supplied
target spectrum uniquely reconstructs one common Abelian row value only after
the weights, electric coefficient, and targets are fixed; inconsistent target
separations fail, while alternative targets yield alternative exact values.
Charge conjugation negates weights, Abelian coordinates, and spectra but leaves
dimension unchanged. A positive generator rescaling preserves charges only
when the electric coefficient and Abelian coupling transform inversely.

The physical headline does not survive. SM2 hard-codes both the target charges
and hypercharges, imports representation dimensions and labels, cannot
distinguish a triplet from an antitriplet by dimension, and assumes a
fifteen-state table without proving completeness or excluding a neutral
singlet. Its Yukawa sentence is not computed; correctly conjugated terms are
neutral, whereas the naive displayed up-type sum is `4/3`. Diagonality alone
does not establish conservation or dynamics. The source constructs no anomaly
equations, global U1 period or lattice, action, Hamiltonian, current, gauge
transformation, physical matter sector, observed prediction, or substrate
dictionary.

The primary route passes 33 exact checks, a fresh independent route passes 18,
the affected accepted-API suite passes 78 tests, and the frozen fourteen-node
graph passes 25 checks. SM2 is therefore qualified through new exact claim
C-REP-003 plus C-REP-001 and C-REP-002. The derived unique physical generation,
anomaly-selected table, completeness, conservation, full global convention,
and Standard Model mechanism remain unpromoted.
