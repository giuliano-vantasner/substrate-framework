# P097 source adjudication

MC3 is qualified, but its two advertised material maps are not accepted as
written. The physical lattice result survives only after replacing bare mass
with phase inertia, and the mixed self-induced-transparency result survives
only as a coordinate-explicit conditional equation without a laboratory gap
or gas identification.

For a dimensionless phase `u_j`, the per-site kinetic coefficient has phase
inertia dimensions. With exact positive coefficients `(I,K,V0,a)`, direct
variation gives
`I*u_j,tt-K*(u_(j+1)-2*u_j+u_(j-1))+V0*sin(u_j)=0`, and the exact linear
spectrum is
`Omega^2=(V0+4*K*sin(k*a/2)^2)/I`. Its gap is `sqrt(V0/I)`, first-zone band
edge is `sqrt((V0+4*K)/I)`, and long-wave speed is `a*sqrt(K/I)`.

If a physical displacement is declared by `q=b*u`, then `I=m*b^2`; a
displacement stiffness similarly gives `K=kappa*b^2`. Consequently
`sqrt(V0/m)` has speed rather than frequency dimensions and MC3's omission of
`b` is load bearing. For two hosts the exact ratio is
`sqrt(V_A*m_B*b_B^2/(V_B*m_A*b_A^2))`. It becomes `sqrt(2)` only when the two
curvatures and phase scales are equal and the effective D inertia or mass is
exactly doubled. Counterfamilies changing either curvature or phase scale
make the ratio one despite doubled mass. Tabulated atomic masses do not
establish collective effective inertia in a loaded nickel host.

The inequality `0<w<1` makes `w*omega_0` sub-gap in a declared model, and
`V0->0+` closes the linear gap at fixed inertia. Neither statement proves a
nonlinear discrete breather exists or selects nickel, hydrogen, or deuterium.
ME3 supplies only the already accepted normalized finite-spacing theorem, and
rung165's shared mass-law premise is not an independent material derivation.

For physical length `z`, physical retarded time `tau`, and dimensionless
`theta`, the conditional equation `theta_z_tau=g*sin(theta)` requires
`[g]=1/(length*time)`. Its vacuum plane-wave characteristic is `k*Omega=g`,
not `Omega^2=omega_0^2+c^2*k^2`; the positive-`k` branch `Omega=g/k` has no
finite wavenumber-independent floor. An absorption coefficient `alpha` with
inverse-length units is neither `g` nor a laboratory angular-frequency
squared. It needs an independently supplied inverse-time factor and
dimensionless prefactor even to form `g`.

For `xi=z/L` and `eta=tau/T`, the normalized coefficient is `g*L*T`. Setting
it to one leaves `L` arbitrary and `T=1/(g*L)`. The logarithmic scale map has
rank one and reciprocal null direction. The explicit map
`X=xi+eta`, `S=xi-eta` gives the normalized hyperbolic equation
`theta_SS-theta_XX+sin(theta)=0`; it fixes the sign convention but not a
laboratory length, time, or gap. The static `4*atan(exp X)` kink is an exact
cross-check of that normalized equation, not a derivation of the optical
coefficient.

The primary McCall-Hahn records concern a conditional resonant two-level,
plane-wave, short-pulse, negligible-damping model. They do not authorize
MC3's universal gas frequency. Rung176 supplies an illustrative collisional
window under additional assumptions, not a self-induced-transparency
spectrum. PASS counts, a 0.05-bar literal, dictionary strings, and symbol-name
absence remain nonprobative.

P097 therefore promotes only the exact conditional physical phase-chain
spectrum and ratio ledger, and the exact conditional mixed-coordinate
equation, scale family, characteristic, and dimensional ceiling. It derives
no material realization, effective parameter value, exact isotope prediction,
gas gap, nonlinear discrete breather, lifetime, radiation condition,
population, or absolute frequency.
