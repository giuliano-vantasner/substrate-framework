# MD6 Disposition Review

## Object Under Review

P201 reviews whether MD6's finite scans over MD1 through MD5 establish the
semantic honesty firewall and empty scientific debt ledger asserted by its
headline.

## Exact Result

All 40 native predicates reproduce on the pinned source bytes. The scanner
does return empty selected match lists for the five declared sibling paths,
its six injected fixtures have the recorded outcomes, and its conditional
three-dimensional DOS, `A^2=S/M`, and fixed-weight branching identities are
exact. The primary route passes 46 checks, the independent route passes 17,
and the graph route passes 14.

## Corrected Boundaries

The valued matcher recognizes only a direct `ast.Assign` to one selected name
whose right-hand side is a direct positive or negative-free numeric constant.
Annotations, unary values, constructors, destructuring, named expressions,
attributes, subscripts, aliases, imports, configuration, tables, closures, and
dynamic loads lie outside its proposition. The literal matcher is a finite
substring scan after removing only the top-level docstring and full-line
comments; construction and numeric-only comparators evade it while nested
docstrings and negated string data collide.

Tag prefixes are exemptions, not provenance proofs. In the actual MD4 bytes,
`COMPARATOR_WN6_log10_w` controls two pass inequalities and
`ILLUSTRATION_beta_sq` flows through `thr` into three checks. MD6 does not hash
its five input files at runtime, scan itself, or compute import and dynamic
dependency closure.

The scientific ledger also remains conditional. `C-DOS-001` supplies rather
than derives dimension, isotropy, branch count, volume, cutoff, and target.
The `M` cancellation follows only after declaring `A^2=S/M`. Branching
magnitude depends on rho, and `C-BRN-002` shows the total population derivative
is controlled by `w+Nw'`, not by the fixed-weight partial alone. Positive
integer intensity has the adjacent mode tie `(S-1,S)`. The empty-ledger check
is literally unconditional `True`, and the no-residual check contains only
three selected conjuncts.

## Dependencies, Compatibility, and Consumers

All six direct dependencies are qualified only through their individual
accepted mappings. A seven-node byte-reused graph covers 263 prior native
predicates without duplicate execution and has no reverse source consumer.
None of the seven scripts has a NumPy legacy quadrature surface, so no version
event affects the scientific verdict.

## Recommendation

Terminally qualify MD6 with no accepted claim mapping. Preserve its exact
finite predicates and conditional identities as source evidence; reject the
semantic completeness and empty-ledger overreads; add no canonical API,
accepted claim, or release; synchronize the queue, immutable campaign, and
durable decision memory.
