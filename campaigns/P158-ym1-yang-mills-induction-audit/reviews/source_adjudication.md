# P158 YM1 Source Adjudication

YM1 exits cleanly and all nine implemented checks pass. The tally validates
the Pauli-half trace metric, exact nonzero-momentum projector arithmetic, one
declared parameter integral, coefficient rearrangements, longitudinal
annihilation, and one nontransverse counterexample. It does not validate the
claimed one-loop Yang--Mills induction.

YM1 never declares the quantum matter action, statistics, determinant,
regulator, loop momentum, bubble, seagull, subtraction, counterterm, or field
normalization. It defines color factorization and imposes the transverse
projector before testing the Ward identity. Its `u(1-u)` numerator is not the
accepted massive complex-scalar QED2 numerator `(1-2u)^2`; the resulting
finite massless coefficient conflicts with the scalar fixed-positive-momentum
infrared divergence. The action step drops the momentum factor relating
`W*P*W` to curvature squared, and a quadratic zero-background projector does
not prove the cubic and quartic connection pieces. Its Abelian guard changes
the trace normalization from one half to one.

The corrected positive object is conditional. For massive complex scalar
multiplets in a declared exact SU2 representation, the color kernel is
`T(R)` times C-VAC-001's exact Abelian kernel. Bubble and seagull contractions
cancel before transverse decomposition. The leading local coefficient is
`N_s*g^2*T(R)/(48*pi*m^2)` for the component density, equivalently
`N_s*g^2/(48*pi*m^2)` multiplying `tr_R(F^2)`. A fresh proper-time curvature
calculation matches the two-point low-momentum limit and includes the full
non-Abelian curvature inside this leading invariant.

The loop contribution is additive to separately declared bare and counterterm
coefficients. It supplies no unique effective coupling, massless pole,
propagating W sector, scalar-to-kink dictionary, physical weak matter, 3+1D
lift, observation, or substrate mechanism. W2 and W7 retroactive closure prose
is rejected; M1 and M2 remain valid only in their accepted conditional scopes;
pending YM2 and GK1 receive no authority.

YM1 is therefore qualified through C-NVP-001, C-NAG-001, and C-VAC-001. Its
headline physical Yang--Mills induction is not promoted.
