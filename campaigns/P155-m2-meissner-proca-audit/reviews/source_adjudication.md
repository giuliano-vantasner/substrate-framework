# P155 M2 Source Adjudication

M2 reproduces all seven implemented predicates natively. That tally validates
some conditional scalar-component algebra, not the source's headline
substrate Meissner mechanism or a full vector derivation.

CHECK1 repeats C-GSM-001's charged lower-doublet coefficient. It becomes a
free-mode mass parameter only after a complete gauge kinetic action and field
normalization are separately declared. For a positive one-mode kinetic
coefficient `kappa` and quadratic coefficient `q`, the normalized value is
`m^2=q/kappa`; the source formula uses the special case `kappa=1`.

CHECK2 varies a scalar Klein-Gordon proxy. The full mostly-plus vector action
instead gives `partial_mu F^(mu nu)-m^2 A^nu=0`. Its divergence derives
`partial_mu A^mu=0` for nonzero mass, so this is not a Lorenz-gauge choice.
The scalar proxy admits a longitudinal plane-wave amplitude that the full
mixed-index kernel rejects.

CHECK3 is exact for a constraint-compatible static tangential component.
CHECK4 directly verifies one exponential residual, but its branch test accepts
either exponent and therefore passes a growing-only counterexample. On the
half-line, both characteristic roots must be retained until
`A_y(0)=A0` and decay at infinity force the unique solution
`A0*exp(-m*x)`. Copying that profile to longitudinal `A_x(x)` generally
violates the derived divergence condition.

CHECK5 does not test a material identity. Its `m_sc2` expression is unused,
both inverse-length products are definitions, and `e -> g/2` is a parameter
relabeling. London 1935 supplies a material-specific current/field theory and
Anderson 1963 a response-based analogy; neither identifies M2's declared field
with a superconductor, weak boson, or substrate. CHECK6's dispersion is exact
when derived from the full vector kernel, not from the source's asserted
on-shell relation. CHECK7 is the exact declared `v -> 0+` limit, but at zero
mass the algebraic divergence constraint disappears.

EM6 does not force a condensate, accepted EM5 is a different scalar-QED2 loop
theorem, and W7 supplies curvature without an action or mass. M2 is therefore
qualified through C-PRC-001 and C-GSM-001. No London current, Meissner
magnetic response, stationary condensate, physical W boson, Standard Model,
or substrate mass mechanism is promoted.
