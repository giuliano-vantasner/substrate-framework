## Four-Axis Source Decision

The axes apply to MK5's mixed source disposition.

- Verification: symbolic verification for exact survivors, replacement
  numeric evidence for the conditional branch, and counterevidence against
  broader source conclusions.
- Review: audited and individually adjudicated.
- Compatibility: compatible through declared reduced-model surfaces and in
  conflict as physical coupling/binding closure.
- Epistemic: qualified historical source evidence.
- Relationship: maps and motivates new claims; challenges or supersedes none.

tests, source disposition, campaign record, and parent effort to agree. Because
the promotion adds claims, APIs, tests, and release state, the full integrated
suite must pass; no earlier boundary can be reused.
