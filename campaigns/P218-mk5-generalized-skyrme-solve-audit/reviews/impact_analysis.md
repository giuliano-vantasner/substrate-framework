# P218 Impact Analysis

## Result

P218 adds two claims, one canonical module, public exports, one test module,
and a new accepted release. Existing claims and symbols remain unchanged.

## Direct Consumers

Direct consumers are the accepted registry and release, generated claim index
and framework memory, generalized radial package exports/tests, MK5 migration
disposition, decision/proposal/effort memory, and immutable P218 campaign.

## Indirect Consumers

MK6 and MR2-MR6 cite MK5's physical numbers or interpretation. They remain
pending and cannot inherit C-GSK-002 beyond its supplied coefficients and
explicit numerical scope. MR1 remains duplicate C-SK-001 evidence. Existing
C-RPROF and BPS consumers are additive dependencies, not modified claims.

## Validation Boundary

The scientific boundary has 27 primary, 12 independent, 9 graph checks, and
65 focused tests. Promotion changes accepted claims, package APIs, tests,
generated consumers, and release state, so full repository validation and the
entire pytest suite are required once at the integrated promotion boundary.
