# Source adjudication: S5 and the T2B annotation

## Decision

S5 is qualified, not migrated and not wholly refuted. Its exact algebra supports `C-VIR-001`, `C-MED-001`, and `C-SK-001`. Its headline claims that Option-C physical realizability and a predictive magnitude/length chain are closed do not follow from those predicates. T2B is likewise qualified: P007 migrated its exact collective-coordinate content, but its later statement that S5 closes physical realizability is now adjudicated as unsupported rather than left pending.

## Claim-by-claim source audit

S5 checks 1–4 repeat the conditional virial system already accepted as `C-VIR-001`. They solve the two supplied slope formulas but do not derive those formulas, their 3D Skyrmion applicability, or any fitted slope.

Checks 5–6 prove `C-MED-001`: if `epsilon=rho*Theta/c^2` and `mu_inverse=rho*Theta`, their common factors cancel and local wave speed is `c`. This result says the declared microscopic density/temperature route produces no index. It cannot simultaneously serve as a mechanism for a nontrivial continuum index.

Check 7 defines `inverse_e_squared_effective=inverse_e_squared_0/n_effective` and verifies the first-order series of that definition. No equation derives this continuum law from checks 5–6 or from microscopic variables. Consequently, the asserted layer separation is a scope distinction, not a physical realization of Option C. Check 8 is another conditional virial substitution.

Rejection guard R1 does not close the gap. Its own exponent bookkeeping obtains `inverse_e_squared proportional to density^(1/3)`, corresponding to `b=-1/3` under the stated `n^-b` convention, but the check then inserts `(a,b)=(0,-2)` from a dossier without deriving that map. The resulting width slope `+1` is exact for the inserted pair but does not follow from the preceding route.

Checks 9–10 insert an imported hedgehog coefficient and an observed proton/electron mass ratio into a declared formula, then measure closeness. This is a postdiction with visible comparator values, not a derivation or blinded prediction. Checks 11–12 and the reverse/power guard support only `C-SK-001`: conditional equality of the two mass premises is equivalent to `F_pi/e=16*pi*m_e*c^2`, and matching powers of `B1` cancel. Checks 13–18 are unit evaluation, comparator checks, an arbitrary consistency band, or mutations of those premises; they do not establish the premises.

Checks 19a–19d do not resolve an absolute length. They insert `xi=1.46 fm`, accept an extremely broad `10–300 MeV` band, and verify only the algebraic scaling `F_pi proportional to length^-1/2`. Later AS1/AS6/AS7 annotations in the same file alternately claim an order-one and an order-`10^20` hierarchy. Chronology and annotations do not adjudicate those external units. Check 20 correctly states that one ratio equation cannot determine `F_pi` and `e` separately.

## Exact qualification

The accepted content is limited to common-response cancellation, conditional virial linear algebra, and conditional mass-formula cancellation. No accepted claim derives the continuum Option-C coupling from a microscopic medium, the topological mass premise, the ANW premise inside this framework, `B1`, a proton/electron prediction, the individual values of `F_pi` or `e`, the correct soliton/lattice length, or the later dimensional-transmutation/gravity annotations.

The unsupported closures are not treated as successful negative results. They remain source-history evidence while the parent migration effort continues through other positive candidates.
