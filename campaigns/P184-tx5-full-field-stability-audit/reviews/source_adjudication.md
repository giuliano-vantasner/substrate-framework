# TX5 Source Adjudication

TX5 does not establish that its B=2 field is a strict local minimum in the
full three-dimensional field space or that a rotating Skyrmion is linearly
dynamically stable. Its headline is not a checked composite predicate. The
source samples six random and one targeted curve, computes no complete
constrained Hessian eigenvalue, and supplies no continuum coercive theorem.

The decisive problem occurs before Hessian classification. At the source's
declared `N=91`, `L=6`, 600-step field, the source-projected direction has
stable first derivatives `-0.0386469896` and `-0.0386469905`; one more source
flow step lowers the reported energy from `2.340608962303` to
`2.340598883300`. Yet the same normalized curve has positive symmetric second
differences `0.2696802053` and `0.2696802142`. Thus positive symmetric
curvature at a noncritical point does not prove a local minimum, and TX5's
statement that exact stationarity is unnecessary is false for its own object.

The lattice definitions also fail to close one variational problem. `egrad`
is the periodic adjoint of the full forward-difference sum, while `Etot`
removes a pad-three boundary region and `evolve` clamps pad two. `baryon`
takes an absolute value and loses orientation. Fixed relaxation and smoothing
step counts change physical flow time and smoothing length when `dx` changes.
The two-grid sign comparison therefore does not establish a continuum limit.

C-SKY-002 retains the exact positive object. Conditional on the declared real
O(4) gradient density, its quartic part is a complete Gram-minor square sum.
Conditional on TX5's displayed mass matrix, `M>=2*I` follows from a second
complete square sum and the bound is sharp. The exact Derrick scale identity
also survives. None of these supplies action provenance, stationarity, static
Hessian positivity, a rotating solution, gyroscopic terms, or dynamics.

TX5.1 remains a loose two-grid energy and absolute-baryon convention check.
TX5.4--5 retain only the listed selected positive curve curvatures. TX5.6
retains one-grid relative softness, not convergence to a translation zero
mode. The sign-flipped Derrick guard is sensitive only to the scaling family.
TX5.7 retains its qualitative selected-direction sign regression, not a
refinement theorem.

Qualify TX5 through C-SKY-002 and the explicitly scoped historical numeric
evidence. Keep its every-direction, strict-full-field-minimum, rotating
solution, static-to-dynamic operator, linear-stability, fission, physical,
gravity, radiation, observation, and substrate readings unaccepted.
