# G1 source adjudication

G1 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Native execution reaches its first
`np.trapz` call after two checks and stops under NumPy 2.5.1. That is only a
version event: an isolated alias to `np.trapezoid` leaves the immutable file
unchanged, exits cleanly, and reports all ten checks passing. Scientific review
therefore begins after, rather than at, compatibility replay.

The metric-ansatz component algebra, trace simplification, gamma derivative,
and free-wave characteristic are narrow exact calculations. The rest-breather
energy and averaged stress are one-grid regressions consistent with accepted
sine-Gordon surfaces. They do not establish the headline radiation mechanism.

G1's load-bearing retarded statements are assignments. For
`box h=-kappa*J`, the one-dimensional retarded Green function makes `h_t`
proportional to the instantaneous retarded source monopole `J`, not to its time
derivative. The source then states a canonical per-side flux proportional to
`h_t^2/kappa` but calls `h_t^2/(2*kappa)` the two-side sum, undercounting it by
four. Its closed power, multipole language, static guard, and numerical leg all
propagate those inserted choices.

The corrected positive object is C-RAD-001. For the separately declared scalar
density
`A*(phi_t^2-c^2*phi_x^2)/2+B*phi*q(t)*delta(x)`, variation gives
`phi_tt-c^2*phi_xx=(B/A)*q(t)*delta(x)`. Under no-incoming retarded data,
`phi=B*I(t-|x|/c)/(2*A*c)` with `I'=q`. Its derivative jump reproduces the
delta source. The canonical flux sends `B^2*q^2/(4*A*c)` outward on each side,
so total power is `B^2*q^2/(2*A*c)`, exactly the local source work.

Boundary history is indispensable. The static field
`-B*q*abs(x)/(2*A*c^2)` has the same equation and derivative jump but zero
flux. It is not the retarded finite-past field; it is a countermodel proving
that the local sourced equation alone does not select radiation. A field
rescaling changes `A`, `B`, and `phi` while preserving `B^2/A` and the power.

G1 also identifies the spatial integral of a scalar stress trace with boosted
energy. On a fixed lab-time slice a scalar profile's coordinate Jacobian gives
the inverse-gamma integral, not `gamma*E0`. Substituting `v(t)` into a
constant-velocity breather is not an on-shell accelerated solution: at an
instantaneously stationary translated static kink, acceleration leaves the
nonzero residual `-a*F'`. The `solve_ivp` leg integrates the same analytic
energy derivative it later recovers, never checks solver success, performs no
refinement, and solves backward for `kappa` so the weak-field amplitude is the
chosen target.

The dossier's displayed two-dimensional action contains `phi*R-V(phi)` and
matter but no canonical kinetic action for `h`. Primary literature likewise
does not license a generic propagating graviton interpretation in two
dimensions. C-RAD-001 is therefore a mathematical scalar-action theorem, not
a dilaton-gravity, breather-radiation, self-force, physical-gravity, or
substrate theorem.

Decision: promote C-RAD-001 and qualify G1 through C-RAD-001 plus the accepted
normalized sine-Gordon energy, constant-boost, and stress surfaces C-SG-001,
C-SG-002, C-SG-008, and C-SG-012. Do not promote G1's boosted trace monopole,
extra source derivative, power normalization, lowest-multipole claim,
accelerated breather, `kappa=8*pi*G_eff`, radiation reaction, or physical
interpretation. Exact primary and independent derivations, mutations, the
static countermodel, the frozen dependency/consumer graph, and the explicit
compatibility replay leave no hidden scientific debt in the qualified result.
