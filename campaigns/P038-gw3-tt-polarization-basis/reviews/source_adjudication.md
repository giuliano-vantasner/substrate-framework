# GW3 Source Adjudication

GW3 is qualified. Its exact rank-two TT image and axis-basis algebra map to
`C-GW-002`, with normalization and frame coverage repaired. Its physical
graviton/polarization interpretation is explicitly imported, and two source
prose statements disagree with its own executable algebra.

## GW3.1 through GW3.5: Projector Algebra

The transverse projector and the TT operator are symmetric, transverse,
traceless, self-adjoint, and idempotent. These facts reproduce, but largely
overlap, the projector already accepted in `C-GW-001`. P038 reuses the shared
operator rather than duplicating a four-index dictionary and reserves the new
claim for its image, basis, completeness, and rotation structure.

## GW3.6: Rank Two

On the six-dimensional real symmetric-tensor space the exact TT operator has
eigenvalues two ones and four zeros, hence rank and trace two. P038 verifies the
operator spectrum at a rational non-axis direction and independently solves
the four linearly independent transverse/traceless constraints, leaving two
free coordinates. This is a linear-algebraic image dimension. Equality to the
separately asserted formula `D(D-3)/2` does not establish a propagating field,
constraint dynamics, gauge quotient, or quantum graviton state.

## GW3.7: Basis and Normalization

GW3's axis tensors `diag(1,-1,0)` and `E_xy+E_yx` are transverse, traceless,
orthogonal, and fixed by the projector. Their Frobenius norms squared are both
two, exactly as the source checks, so calling them “orthonormal” is false. Their
coefficients require division by two. P038 encodes normalized tensors divided
by `sqrt(2)`, for which coefficients are direct inner products and the dyadic
basis reconstruction equals the TT projector for every symmetric input.

The source only treats `n=z`. P038 adds a deterministic piecewise transverse-
frame construction that covers every nonzero coordinate direction, while
explicitly making no globally continuous frame claim. Rotating the transverse
frame by `psi` rotates plus/cross by `2*psi`; the complex circular combinations
acquire opposite weight-two phases. These are exact representation facts, not
physical helicity evidence absent dynamics.

## Guard and Narrative Consistency

Dropping the in-plane trace subtraction leaves the three-dimensional symmetric
transverse sector, so the wrong operator has trace three and is not TT. This is
a valid mutation. GW3's main guard and executable output say three, but its
provenance paragraph says `Tr(Lambda_wrong)=4`; that durable narrative sentence
is incorrect. Cross-sign reversal, by contrast, is a valid basis convention
and leaves the projector span unchanged, so it is not treated as a falsifying
mutation.

## Terminal Disposition

GW3 maps its exact TT image dimension and corrected basis theorem to
`C-GW-002` and is otherwise qualified. Excluded scope includes the imported
graviton count, propagating equations, gauge constraints or quotient, physical
plus/cross amplitudes, physical helicity, G1/GW2 radiation connectivity,
observations, and substrate identity. Durable evidence is the P038 verifier,
independent constraint review, source reproduction, and this adjudication.
