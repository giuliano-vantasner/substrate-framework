# P222 MR4 Source Adjudication

## Decision

MR4 is duplicate evidence for C-VEC-001 and C-SK-001 rather than a new
accepted claim. Its exact leading coefficient repair and conditional
square-root algebra survive at those owners. Its full-vector, derived-KSRF,
physical-rho, parameter-free, convention-bracket, and comparator-free
conclusions are not accepted.

## Reproduction and Compatibility

The pinned 21,181-byte source at SHA-256 `cefe7192...c9d3d7` has a clean
hash-identical P215 execution: seven checks, one assertion, 7.953 seconds, and
exit zero. Repeating it would add no evidence. MR4 imports neither NumPy nor
SciPy and has no sampled-integration or compatibility surface.

## Predicate Reviews

MR4.1 and MR4.2 evaluate forty-digit residuals only at `x=0.37,y=-0.53`.
Their generic one-form and Maurer-Cartan content is valid under declared action
data but already owned by C-VEC-001; the source does not solve the full kinetic
vector equation. Both receive numeric evidence, audited duplicate review,
compatible leading-order scope, and qualified source status.

MR4.3 derives the one-sixteenth trace factor given the one-form relation and
checks one nonzero numerical trace. MR4.4 matches equally normalized p4
densities and solves `e=g`, while correctly rejecting dimensionful `e=F/2`.
Both are symbolic-verified duplicate evidence, compatible at C-VEC-001's p4
ceiling, and active only at that accepted owner.

MR4.5 exactly combines `e=g`, the declared mass law
`m_V^2=a*g^2*F^2`, and `U=F/e` to obtain
`e^2=m_V/(sqrt(a)U)`. Setting `a=2`, `U=16*pi*m_e`, and supplied masses yields
4.619774866. This is symbolic-verified conditional composition of C-VEC-001
and C-SK-001, not a parameter-free physical derivation: `a,m_V,U` remain
load-bearing, and neither KSRF nor the rho identification is accepted.

MR4.6 changes the F normalization in the mass law while retaining the same
common-unit substitution, producing a `sqrt(2)` endpoint mutation. With `a`
unfixed every positive coupling is possible, so two inconsistent readings do
not form an accepted bound. The ANW fit 5.45 enters its pass predicate and
cannot select a convention. This receives conditional numeric evidence and
rejected physical-bound scope.

MR4.7 excludes selected nucleon, ratio, and kappa needles but contains imported
rho/electron masses and the load-bearing ANW fit. Its `{m_V,m_e}` free-symbol
test demonstrates retained inputs, not parameter-free closure. The advertised
complete comparator guard is audited and rejected.
