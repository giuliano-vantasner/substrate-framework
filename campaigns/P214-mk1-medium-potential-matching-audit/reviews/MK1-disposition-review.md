# MK1 Disposition Review

MK1 is qualified through C-BRK-001, C-CHI-002, and C-BPS-001. The disposition
is mixed because exact normalization identities survive while the physical
medium interpretation and its parameter-closure consequences do not.

## Surviving Content

The SU(2) trace identity is exact for the declared group coordinate. With the
ANW prefactor, requiring the chosen scalar coordinate to have canonical
kinetics selects `q=2`; the resulting `q=2,K=1` generalized-mass match gives
the positive relation `mu_BPS=m*F/2`. The natural-unit dimension is consistent,
and for the separately supplied `V=1-cos(F)` potential the round-S3 average is
exactly `32*sqrt(2)/(15*pi)`.

These are conditional mathematical results. They map existing claims and do
not create C-BPS-004 or a new API.

## Rejected Content

PG2 does not establish a physical substrate pion or decay scale, and MC1 does
not identify its dimensionless 1+1 field or onsite coefficient with an SU(2)
BPS potential. No accepted premise maps action measures or converts
`mu_medium` into `mu_BPS^2`. Equal natural-unit dimensions do not supply those
maps.

MK1.6 assumes the relation it claims the medium forces and then observes that
rescaling one side moves the other symbol. That is not an independent physical
obstruction. MK1.7 is also not independent: its own definitions simplify to
`m_tail=2*mu_BPS/F`, so `m_tail=m` and MK1.3 have the identical positive
solution. The source's passing wrong-factor guard establishes sensitivity of
the same equation, not a second derivation.

Therefore the substrate-supplied coefficient, physical pion map, broken KI2
family, no-free-potential conclusion, paid parameter debt, and downstream
physical closure are rejected.

## Terminal Verdict

The terminal disposition is `qualified` with accepted mappings C-BRK-001,
C-CHI-002, and C-BPS-001. Later MK and MR units remain nonauthoritative and
must treat MK1's coefficient relation as conditional supplied-model algebra.
