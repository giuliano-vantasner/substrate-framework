# Existing-Claim Composition Review

P214 proposes no accepted-claim change. This review adjudicates each claim
whose accepted surface is used or challenged by MK1 rather than treating the
source tally as blanket authority.

## C-BRK-001

C-BRK-001 remains active and unchanged. Its exact formula gives
`m_generalized^2=A*q^2/(K*F^2)`, hence the positive root
`mu_BPS=m*F*sqrt(K)/q` when `A=mu_BPS^2`. MK1's `q=2,K=1` equation is one
specialization. The accepted claim already withholds field ontology, physical
mass, coefficient selection, and substrate mapping, so MK1 adds no claim.

## C-CHI-002

C-CHI-002 remains active and unchanged. It already derives the ANW trace and
kinetic pair, including `q=1,K=1/4` and `q=2,K=1`, with generalized mass
independent of the coordinate multiplier. MK1.2 and the conditional part of
MK1.3 therefore compose this accepted claim. The physical pion, decay scale,
chiral action, derived coefficient, and substrate realization remain outside
its scope.

## C-BPS-001

C-BPS-001 remains active and unchanged. It supplies the exact target-average
definition and bound for declared positive `mu_BPS` and declared `V`. P107
already records `W=32*sqrt(2)/(15*pi)` for the supplied one-cosine potential.
Neither the claim nor that special evaluation selects the potential or its
coefficient, so MK1.5 maps only the conditional mathematical ceiling.

## C-MED-003

C-MED-003 remains active and unchanged, but it is not promoted into MK1's
accepted mapping. Its gap is `sqrt(mu_medium/lambda_medium)` for a declared
1+1 density, and a common coefficient rescaling preserves that gap while
changing energy and action. It contains no `F_pi`, physical pion identity,
three-dimensional BPS measure, or conversion to `mu_BPS^2`. It therefore
serves as counterauthority to MK1.6's physical interpretation.

## C-GMR-001 and C-SK-001

C-GMR-001 and C-SK-001 remain unchanged and are not MK1 mappings. C-GMR-001
is a conditional algebraic ledger with free physical inputs, while C-SK-001
relates supplied Skyrme mass premises and does not determine `m_pi`, `F_pi`,
or `mu_BPS`. Their names or dimensions cannot close MK1's cross-sector map.

## Review Verdict

The affected accepted claims are individually consistent, dependency closed,
and unchanged. C-BPS-004 remains reserved and unpromoted because every exact
survivor is accepted composition, while the allegedly novel medium map and
independent tail oracle fail their stated scope.
