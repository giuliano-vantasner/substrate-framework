changes no accepted claim, package symbol, test API, formal theorem, or release
manifest, so no code-graph index refresh or full release gate is warranted.

The direct records are the two migration registries, MK1 decision memory,
framework-migration effort memory, and campaign artifacts. No generated claim
or release document changes. The reverse consumers are MK2 through MK6, MR2,
and MR6. Their files
remain immutable pending evidence; P214 changes only what authority they may
claim from MK1.

focused accepted-API replay passes 57 tests, and the 17-node source graph
passes every runtime tally after the compatibility preflight. Record-sensitive
repository, skill, documentation, memory, YAML, and diff validation will close the
campaign without repeating the unchanged v0.155.0 full suite.

language corrections: algebraically identical downstream equations are

the migration queue is regenerated from dispositions. These changes encode
the actual P214 failure mechanisms without adding a new ceremony layer.
