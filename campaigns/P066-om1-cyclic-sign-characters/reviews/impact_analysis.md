# P066 Pre-Change Impact and Duplication Analysis

The impact boundary was evaluated at framework commit `e62f213` after the
P066 domain, character, equality, candidate, and comparator contract was
frozen and before opening OM1's executable or editing canonical source.

## Existing Surface Search

GitNexus finds C-TOP-001's canonical `winding_parity` and its P019 independent
quotient review, but no finite-cyclic kernel/faithfulness classifier or binary-
product character flow. The unrelated even/odd-source and graded trace signs
do not share OM1's group-character object.

## Duplication Boundary

C-TOP-001 already owns `(-1)^w` as an integer-winding homomorphism and the
ceiling against physical statistics or particle interpretations. P066 may add
only the nonduplicate classification across C_n and C2 products, including
kernel, quotient, faithfulness, and independent-factor counterexamples. It may
not promote a second parity theorem or infer a shared physical mechanism from
matching signs.

## Impact and Decision

Upstream impact on `winding_parity` is LOW: one direct canonical consumer, no
affected process, and one module. The implementation boundary is additive
within `topological_labels.py`; existing signatures and results remain
unchanged. Focused tests and campaign verifiers will cover the new surface,
followed by staged post-change detection before commit.

## Post-Change Detection

Staged detection reports LOW risk, one changed indexed symbol across 25 files,
and no affected execution process. The new functions are additive and were not
members of the pre-change graph, so this count is not treated as proof of their
correctness; their direct consumers are the focused tests and two exact P066
verifiers. Existing `winding_parity` and `combined_winding_parity` signatures
and behavior remain unchanged.
