# P075 AS4 Source Adjudication

AS4 exits cleanly with `ALL 7 CHECKS PASS`. The tally reproduces the source;
it does not establish the headline.

The displayed 4x2 coefficient matrix has exact rank two and zero coefficient
nullity. It has only two coefficient directions, however: medium is
proportional to gravity and confinement is proportional to hadronic. Its two
left-null conditions are `2*g+r=0` and `-2*h+s=0`. With AS4's generic symbolic
right-hand sides the augmented rank is three, so the system is inconsistent;
both coordinates are identified only after the two compatibility equations
are imposed on separately supplied data. That is conditional inverse inference
and crosschecking, not emergence of an absolute scale or proof that four
physical sources are independent.

The source assertions do not validate those missing premises. AS4.1 omits
three load-bearing x coefficients from its predicate. AS4.2 never inspects the
right-hand side. AS4.4 checks only that two formulas contain named symbols and
accepts wrong coefficients. AS4.0 cannot distinguish a free symbol from a
symbolic fitted input. Relabeling identical algebra as fabricated leaves every
rank and residual unchanged, demonstrating that provenance is an external
obligation rather than a matrix property.

The guards also contain concrete defects. AS4.G1's actual 4x3 matrix has rank
three and nullity zero, exactly as its Boolean asserts but opposite to its prose
claim that a free length reopens underdetermination; changing the final length
coefficient from one to two is what produces a null direction. AS4.G2 encodes
`a` as `(1,0,0)` while declaring M,L,T column order, rather than the correct
`(0,1,0)`. Both matrices happen to have rank three, so the no-dimensionless-
monomial conclusion survives while the convention check does not.

Dependency closure fails independently. C-GRV-001 retains a free induced
coefficient and an allowed additive inverse-G baseline, the latter destroying
AS4's constant gravity log slope except at the separately imposed zero-
baseline limit. Restoring AS4's own admitted `s_G`, `kappa_s`, and `c_CF4`
coefficients as nuisance coordinates gives a rank-four design over five
coordinates with a mixed null direction, leaving both `x` and `y`
unidentifiable. Other cited sector narratives are pending or accepted only
under narrower ceilings. AS4 supplies neither observations nor covariance, so
C-GLS-001 cannot turn its labels into statistical independence.

The valid exact content was already accepted explicitly through C-LIN-001 and
C-IDN-001 in P022 and P065; P065's accepted evidence already contains the same
AS4 matrix and compatibility relations. P075 therefore adds no claim or
canonical API. AS4 is retained as `duplicate_evidence` for those two claims,
while its physical over-determination, causal reduction, independence,
prediction, deferral-expiry, and absolute-scale readings are rejected.
