# GK3D3 Source Adjudication

GK3D3 is qualified rather than accepted literally. Its hash-pinned script runs
all fourteen checks, but only the exact conditional ratio, scale-log,
conversion-cancellation, zero-branch algebra, and common-rescaling identities
survive after accepted premises are restored. These positive results are
rederived and generalized by C-VAC-004.

The scale identification is not derived. C-RGE-003 provides formal reference
and transmuted energies, inverse-energy lengths with independent conversions,
and their relative orientation. It assigns no lattice, granularity, soliton,
Compton, QCD, hadron, or physical matching labels. Setting both conversions to
`hbar*c0`, setting the soliton coefficient to one, and identifying the affine
reference and evaluation scales with those energies are separate premises.

The total normalization is not derived. C-VAC-003 gives
`Z=Z_ref+b*log(mu_ref/mu)/(8*pi^2)` and neither rung25 nor GK3D2 supplies
`Z_ref=0` after charged matter is introduced. C-VAC-004 proves that a
consistently paired formal one-loop map gives
`Z=Z_ref+b/(b0*g^2)`. Only a separately declared zero-matching branch gives
the inverse kinetic coordinate `b0*g^2/b`. The source's `b0`, `beta^2`, matter
weight, scale maps, and boundary remain inputs, not a zero-parameter physical
coupling prediction.

The functional-uniqueness and self-consistency readings are rejected. A
dimensional power exponent zero yields a constant, not a logarithm. Dimensions
do not select a loop form factor, and constants, powers or rational functions
of `log(y)` refute the claim that only the logarithm maps `exp(X)` to a rational
function of `X`. The hardcoded `b0=7`, `beta^2=0.245`, and two sample matter
weights are unaccepted comparator coordinates and do not establish general
perturbative control or leading-log accuracy.

Every check is adjudicated individually in
`evidence/check-adjudication.yaml`. The qualified source maps C-RGE-003,
C-IDN-002, C-DIM-009, C-VAC-003, and C-VAC-004. It does not promote a physical
gauge sector, selected matter content, total coupling, unique logarithm,
absolute scale, QCD identification, perturbative prediction, or substrate
mechanism. GK3D4 through GK3D6 remain separately governed forward consumers.
