M1 reproduces all nine implemented predicates natively. That tally validates a
declared finite-dimensional scalar kinetic quadratic form, not the source's
headline substrate Anderson-Higgs mechanism.

CHECK1 through CHECK3 are exact after the Pauli-half representation, commuting
Abelian generator, couplings, lower-component vacuum, scalar kinetic term, and
one-half real gauge-field convention are declared. The general object is twice
the real Gram matrix of the coupled vacuum-orbit vectors. It is symmetric and
positive semidefinite, and its real coefficient kernel is exactly the coupled
vacuum stabilizer.

CHECK4 through CHECK7 are exact for the canonical gauge kinetic metric. A
general positive metric `K` changes the quadratic-mass question to
`M2*x=lambda*K*x`; raw eigenvalues and the displayed orthogonal mixing angle
are not kinetic-basis invariant. The algebraic null direction and rank-three
orbit establish a stabilizer, not by themselves a physical photon, W, Z,
Goldstone count, spectral pole, or rho measurement.

CHECK8 is mislabeled. Replacing `-g*g_prime` by `+g*g_prime` with unchanged
magnitude leaves determinant zero and is obtained by the basis congruence
`B -> -B`. The implemented mutant is `+g*g_prime/2`, changing magnitude as
well as sign. It is a valid mis-normalization guard but cannot establish a
physical sign. CHECK9 is the exact zero-vacuum limit of the declared form, not
evidence that a medium condensed.

C-QBL-001 does not force a complex condensate, promote it to an SU2 doublet, or
select `Y=1`. C-NAG-001 and C-REP-002 expressly supply no scalar action, vacuum,
mass mechanism, or weak sector. M1 is therefore qualified through C-GSM-001,
C-NAG-001, C-GAU-001, and C-REP-002. No physical condensate, Higgs particle,
photon, weak boson, Standard Model, electroweak unification, or four-force
substrate closure is promoted.
