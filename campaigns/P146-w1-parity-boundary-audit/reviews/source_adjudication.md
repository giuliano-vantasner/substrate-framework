# W1 source adjudication

W1 is qualified, not accepted wholesale. Native execution reaches W1.1 through
W1.3 and then stops only because its immutable source calls the removed NumPy
name `np.trapz`. An alias-only replay to `np.trapezoid` exits cleanly with all
eight checks passing. This is a version compatibility event, not a scientific
failure. Mutable campaign and canonical code use exact algebra or current
interfaces.

The exact positive result is narrower and more useful than W1's headline. For
the scalar boundary residual `R=a*u+beta*v-J`, scalar spatial parity leaves the
temporal trace `u` unchanged and flips the fixed-coordinate spatial trace `v`.
It therefore maps the member with coefficient `beta` to the member with
coefficient `-beta`. That is covariance of a parameter family. At a parity
center a fixed residual is invariant for arbitrary traces only when `beta=0`.

The mixed residual is not parity odd. Its `a*u-J` component is even and its
`beta*v` component is odd. It becomes purely odd only when the entire even
component is absent. W1.7 checks only that the spatial coefficient is nonzero;
it does not test the parity eigenvalue of the complete residual and derives no
charge selection.

An outward-normal coefficient has different semantics. Parity maps a right
half-line to a left half-line and changes the domain normal together with the
coordinate derivative. The outward-normal trace and its coefficient are
unchanged by that combined map. Parity is not automatically an internal
symmetry on one fixed half-line, so the source cannot slide between fixed
coordinates and normal orientation to infer intrinsic breaking.

One scalar boundary equation constrains only one combination of `u` and `v`.
For nonzero `beta`, `v=(J-a*u)/beta` while `u` remains free. For `beta=0`, the
equation constrains only `u` and leaves `v` arbitrary. W1's vector witness
inserts `v=0`; the law does not imply it. Its chiral witness inserts `u=J` and
`v=J`, which for displayed epsilon plus one leaves residual `J` rather than
zero.

W1 also relabels its boundary sign correlation as `Delta Q`. C-SG-013 already
shows that sign correlation and half-line topological transfer are independent:
the latter is fixed by boundary field change and vacuum endpoints. The source
hard-codes `Q(+1)=+1` and `Q(-1)=-1`, then verifies the desired flip by
composition. Its fermion-parity checks likewise evaluate an inserted integer
map, not a derived field theory.

W1.1 remains a valid linear massless characteristic identity under its stated
decomposition. It is not a nonlinear chiral split of sine-Gordon: both exact
characteristic transport defects remain sourced by the nonlinear wave
operator. The dossier also prints one chiral spatial-derivative sign opposite
to its own explanatory note.

Decision: promote C-BND-001 and qualify W1 through C-BND-001, C-SG-011, and
C-SG-013. Retain the exact linear projector, coefficient-family pullback, and
compatibility-correct arithmetic only under those ceilings. Do not promote
intrinsic parity violation, an epsilon-to-charge map, correlation as charge
transfer, vector or chiral dynamics, fermion parity, weak interactions, a
boundary action, or a full nonlinear chiral decomposition. Primary, fresh
independent, focused, and frozen-graph routes pass 39, 23, 21, and 45 checks,
with 129 source predicates, ten assertions, and no unresolved campaign debt.
