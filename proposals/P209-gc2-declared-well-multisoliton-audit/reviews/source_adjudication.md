# GC2 Source Adjudication

GC2 executes all eight predicates, but its headline conflates three different
objects: a family of separately supplied translated Hamiltonians, multiple
levels of one Hamiltonian, and simultaneous nonlinear field solitons. The
source constructs only the first, and it constructs six members rather than
three.

Checks GC2.1 and GC2.2 correctly expose the fixed external depth, width,
spacing, and center list and the exact mismatch with the conditional quartic
core depth. C-OVL-002 and C-QBL-005 already own those mathematical objects.
The mismatch compares declared models; source prose containing
`multi-soliton` cannot supply a common field equation, interaction, boundary
problem, or simultaneous solution.

GC2.3 calls `E|x|` a centroid and refines only that value. Exact whole-line
translation is stronger: the ground-density mean is `R`, its centered variance
is `w^2 polygamma(1,s)/2`, and its spectrum and centered width are independent
of `R`. Width divided by displacement tends to zero for any fixed-width
translated density, so it proves localization relative to an origin but not
multiple field objects.

GC2.4 correctly rederives the quartic translation zero mode. Its exact-sine
count three is hard-coded from FG2's already rejected wall-contaminated result.
For the accepted quartic spectrum, removing the zero leaves one negative
Hessian level, not a positive particle state or generation. GC2.6 correctly
shows that only `p=2` among WM9's literal `p=1,2,3` pure-sech trials is an
eigenfunction of the quartic Hessian; the actual second mode is odd
`sech*tanh`.

GC2.5 correctly finds that neither FG2 nor FG4 derives the observed count
three and that WM9 counts a literal tuple. The relationship is absent authority
plus WM9 misattribution, not a derivational cycle. GC2.7 is an origin-dependent
single-shape guard, and GC2.8's globals-name intersection does not inspect
reachable source strings. No observed comparator selects the accepted exact
adjudication, but that weak guard does not prove its own broader claim.

GC2 is therefore qualified through C-QBL-001, C-QBL-003, C-OVL-001,
C-OVL-002, C-MIX-002, and C-QBL-005. No multisoliton, selected generation
count, accepted exact-sine third level, positive particle tower, hierarchy,
stability, CP mechanism, or physical map is promoted.
