# P200 MD5 Source Adjudication

MD5 reaches a qualified rather than blanket-migrated disposition. Its exact
positive object is the general population-dependent-weight monotonicity
criterion proposed as C-BRN-002, composed where appropriate with C-BRN-001's
allocation and C-CMB-003's static factorial-one mass.

The constant-weight derivative and conditional algebra survive. The isotope
and material handshake does not. Accepted dependencies supply neither a
population-independent material weight law nor two complete host parameter
maps, state preparation, common physical channel definitions, interaction,
matrix element, final-state measure, or rate observable. Symbol absence,
positive mathematical mass, and a substituted doubled mass do not close those
premises. The source also omits the positive-integer adjacent mode tie.

The recommended accepted mapping is
`[C-BRN-001, C-CMB-003, C-BRN-002]`. C-MKV-001 remains an independently
declared process ceiling and is not needed by the allocation theorem. MD6
remains individually pending. All source gaps and counterexamples are
preserved in append-only attempts and evidence.
