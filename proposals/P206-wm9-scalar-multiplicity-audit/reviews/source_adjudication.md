# WM9 Source Adjudication

WM9 executes all eight predicates, but its load-bearing identification is not
an invariant theorem. The exact surviving object is the accepted C-OVL-001
factorization `y_p=A I_p` for three supplied mode powers, whose coefficients
are distinct and whose amplitude is shared by construction.

The source defines `n_condensates=len(amplitude_symbols)` and
`n_modes=len((1,2,3))`. Neither operation counts physical field species or
observed generations. Three labeled fields can share one amplitude; three
independent amplitudes can be constrained equal; one field can carry several
independent mode couplings; and an inert field can expose no overlap symbol.
These countermodels preserve field labels while changing expression-symbol
cardinality, so WM9.4's manually declared three-symbol alternative cannot
establish necessity.

The authority mismatch is independent and decisive. C-QBL-001 explicitly
does not prove stability or forced complex ontology. C-QBL-003's two
negative-or-zero scalar Hessian modes are not generations. C-MIX-002 provides
no observed family map. C-GSM-001 supplies its scalar vacuum and kinetic term
and derives no condensate-to-Higgs identity. C-REP-003 and C-RGE-005 supply
field rows and multiplicities.

Accordingly WM9 is qualified as exact accepted composition under the listed
claims. `N_H=1`, `N_gen=3`, structural exclusion of `N_H/N_gen=1`, and the
empty Phase-39 debt statement are not promoted.
