# P198 MD3 Source Adjudication

MD3 reaches a qualified rather than blanket-migrated disposition. The exact
positive object is the coherent-state theorem proposed as C-VOP-001, composed
with the accepted Fock and factorial-one claims. It survives independent raw
series, recurrence, overlap, moment, and mutation checks.

The source's periodicity equations are exact but do not establish its
“single-vacuum bound removed” physical narrative. No accepted bound supplied a
material `S` in the first place; a compact multiplication vertex preserves
pointwise density, while a Weyl displacement changes a declared Fock state.
These are distinct operations. The Huang-Rhys, Franck-Condon, PN2, material,
growth, branching, isotope, channel, and rate readings remain unaccepted.

The recommended accepted mapping is `[C-OSC-001, C-CMB-003, C-VOP-001]`.
MD4 through MD6 remain individually pending. All failures and corrections are
preserved in append-only attempts and evidence.
