# P204 Accepted-Composition Review

P204 proposes no new accepted claim. Each mapped claim is reviewed separately
for WM7 and retains its existing four-axis status.

## C-RGE-005

C-RGE-005 owns the exact supplied product-group one-loop contribution table,
including the imported Weyl weight `2/3` and complex-scalar weight `1/3`. For
the supplied three-generation rows and `N_H` scalar doublets its matter vector
is exactly `(4+N_H/10, 4+N_H/6, 4)`. WM7's solve recovers weights used to build
the imported coefficient vector and therefore adds consistency evidence, not
independent perturbative provenance.

## C-REP-001

C-REP-001 makes a trace coordinate equal an inverse-coupling coordinate only
under a separately supplied common law and preserves paired Abelian
normalization covariance. WM7's `25/66` is retained only as that conditional
coordinate, not as a physical weak-angle boundary.

## C-REP-003 and C-ANO-001

C-REP-003 treats the finite multiplet rows, labels, hypercharges, dimensions,
and target structure as supplied. C-ANO-001 verifies constraints on supplied
charge variables but establishes neither physical completeness nor a scalar
multiplicity. WM7 may compose those rows arithmetically; it cannot infer that
they exhaust physical induced matter.

## C-MIX-002

C-MIX-002 proves the generic irreducible phase-count formula and gives one slot
at matrix size three. It derives neither a physical generation construction
nor the value `N_GEN=3`; WM7's count remains a declared table input.

## C-VAC-003

C-VAC-003 owns complete affine factorwise kinetic families and keeps matching
boundaries, finite parts, weights, and interpretation explicit. WM7's fixed
ratio follows only after imposing zero boundaries and one common coefficient.
A negative beta contribution cannot by itself force a negative total affine
kinetic coordinate.

## Decision

All six claims remain unchanged. C-RGE-007 remains unpromoted. No canonical
module, API, registry entry, release claim set, or package test changes. The
only governed delta is WM7's individual qualified mapping.
