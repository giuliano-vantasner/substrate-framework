# MK2 source adjudication

MK2 reproduces seven native symbolic checks, but its clean tally combines an
exact conditional elimination with unaccepted HLS, current, anomaly, and
physical-field premises. P215 retains the exact algebra and promotes the new
U(2) invariant-metric classification while rejecting the claimed
medium-supplied BPS coupling.

## Exact conditional vector elimination

For the declared algebraic action
`m_omega^2*w^2/2+g_omega*w*B`, direct variation and substitution give
`w=-g_omega*B/m_omega^2` and
`L_eff=-g_omega^2*B^2/(2*m_omega^2)`. Thus the source convention
`-lambda_A^2*B^2` has
`lambda_A=abs(g_omega)/(sqrt(2)*m_omega)`. This is exact for the algebraic
kernel. When the vector kinetic operator is restored, the inverse is
`(m_omega^2+D)^-1`; the local term is only its leading low-momentum term, and
C-EFT-001's finite inverse residual is nonzero whenever the derivative kernel
is nonzero.

C-BPS-001 uses a different convention,
`-lambda_BPS^2*pi^4*B^2`. Equality requires
`lambda_A=pi^2*lambda_BPS`. MK2 never applies this conversion. Its conditional
formula and every downstream BPS product are therefore a factor `pi^2` too
large when read as C-BPS-001's `lambda_BPS`.

## U(2) metric classification

The displayed Pauli-half basis exactly obeys
`Tr(T_A*T_B)=delta_AB/2`. If one separately declares a single-trace mass term,
its four component coefficients are equal. The group label does not force that
declaration. Exact adjoint-invariance equations show that every real symmetric
U(2)-invariant form has component Gram
`diag(beta,alpha,alpha,alpha)`, equivalently
`2*alpha*Tr(X*Y)+(beta-alpha)*Tr(X)*Tr(Y)`. It is positive for positive
`alpha,beta`, and singlet-triplet degeneracy occurs exactly at `alpha=beta`.
The positive metric `diag(5,2,2,2)` is a decisive invariant counterexample to
MK2.2's forced-degeneracy reading.

## Conditional cancellation and missing authority

If one supplies `m_omega=m_V`, `g_omega=N_c*g/2`, and
`m_V^2=a*g^2*F_pi^2`, exact substitution gives
`lambda_A=N_c/(2*sqrt(2*a)*F_pi)`. The source value
`N_c/(4*F_pi)` is the additional `a=2` specialization. C-VEC-001 explicitly
treats KSRF and `a=2` as premises and supplies no physical rho, omega, pion, or
HLS action.

MK2.3 does not close the other inputs. C-WZW-002 quantizes a mathematical
level but does not identify it with `N_c`. C-TOP-002 supplies a mathematical
winding current but not a physical baryon current, quark-number map, or color
count. P058 rejected WZ3's `n=N_c=3` reading, and P059 rejected WZ4's free
`g_omega`, imported KSRF, and anomaly-normalization claims. General HLS anomaly
solutions retain homogeneous coefficients. Universality plus a generator
normalization is not an accepted derivation of the omega-topological-current
coupling.

MK2.5's inverse-energy dimension is correct but non-identifying. MK2.6 cannot
break the accepted positive BPS parameter family because the proposed
coupling relation lacks accepted closure. MK2.7 is conditional algebra in the
source convention: with MK1's conditional relation it gives
`lambda_A*mu=N_c*m_pi/8`; C-BPS-001's convention instead gives
`lambda_BPS*mu=N_c*m_pi/(8*pi^2)`. MK1 supplies no accepted physical
`F_pi`, pion, or medium map in either expression.

## Decision

convention-complete conditional vector-current match. Qualify MK2 through
