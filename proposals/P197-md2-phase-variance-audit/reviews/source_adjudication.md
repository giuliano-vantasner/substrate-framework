# MD2 Source Adjudication

MD2 reproduces all 26 native predicates. Under a separately declared
three-dimensional canonical scalar action, independent product ground state,
positive stiffness, scalar branch, gapped dispersion, continuum measure, and
UV cutoff, its one-mode variance, J(X) integral, exact continuum variance, and
large-cutoff leading term are correct. For one fixed finite set, total variance
also equals mode count times the arithmetic mean even when individual mode
variances differ.

The headline does not follow. C-MED-003 and C-SG-018 are classical and
one-dimensional. C-DOS-001 supplies a declared-d measure but no quantization,
state, stiffness, branch occupancy, or microscopic cutoff. AS6 and AS7 accept
no beta normalization or granularity. Substituting the definition of beta into
the same asymptotic expression is reparameterization, not independent
overdetermination. The inputs are supplied, so the result is not parameter
free or an unmeasured material prediction.

The fixed-set factorization is also overread. `M*(S/M)=S` holds after fixing
one set and its total. Adding a zero-variance mode changes M without changing
S; adding a positive-variance mode changes both; holding the mean fixed while
adding equal modes changes S. Differentiating a continuous symbol M while
holding S fixed therefore does not prove invariance under changing the
admitted mode set. The rewrite `n<S` has no physical growth content until n,
S, state, cutoff, dynamics, and channel are accepted.

C-QFL-001 captures the exact conditional inverse-frequency moment and the
fixed-set/set-growth ceiling. MD2 should be qualified through C-DOS-001 and
C-QFL-001 while its d3-medium, corpus-beta, derived-granularity, microscopic-
cutoff, parameter-free, overdetermination, mode-count-invariance, growth,
material, channel, rate, and substrate readings remain unaccepted.
