# MD2 Disposition Review

## Object Under Review

P197 reviews MD2's d3 zero-point mode integral, beta and granularity inputs,
claim of overdetermination, elimination of mode count, and corrected physical
growth condition.

## Exact Result

The one-oscillator variance and d3 continuum integral survive under explicit
canonical normalization, product ground state, scalar branch, stiffness,
measure, gap, and cutoff. C-QFL-001 also retains the fixed-set total equals
count times arithmetic mean identity and supplies exact set-change ceilings.

## Corrected Boundaries

The accepted medium is not d3 or quantized. AS6 and AS7 supply no beta value or
granularity. MD1 supplies no microscopic cutoff. Definition substitution is
not independent overdetermination. A fixed-set identity does not make the
total invariant when modes are added or removed, and the rewrite `n<S`
derives no physical growth, material, channel, probability, or rate.

## Compatibility and Consumers

MD2 and its ten-node graph have no legacy quadrature event. The graph covers
282 native checks without duplicate execution. MD4, MD5, and MD6 remain
pending and receive no blanket promotion.

## Recommendation

Accept C-QFL-001 and qualify MD2 through C-DOS-001 and C-QFL-001. Reject its
d3-medium, corpus-beta, granularity, microscopic-cutoff, parameter-free,
overdetermination, mode-set-invariance, growth, material, channel, rate, and
substrate overreads.
