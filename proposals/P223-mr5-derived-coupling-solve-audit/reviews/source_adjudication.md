## Stronger oracle outcome

Twenty-one primary checks use exact angular inputs, the accepted
regular-origin/massive-tail Robin problem, checked collocation status,
equation/boundary/Derrick residuals, isolated domain/tolerance/quadrature
refinements, continuous bounded optimization, and load-bearing mutations. At
the supplied point they give `b=(1.43599879,2.81280605,5.23598337)` and
`kappa=11.53644426`; the difference from the source's `11.490` is material.

Eleven fresh checks independently vary the density and solve the same
outer-radius-14 Robin problem by vacuum-complement DOP853 amplitude shooting
with Simpson quadrature. The degree-four tail-residual failure is preserved;
tighter integration and root resolution repair it without changing its
threshold. The independent coefficients and signed difference agree with the
canonical route.

## Dependency and interpretation ceiling

The source retains supplied electron, pion, and rho masses, `N_c=3`, the ANW
fit, and the MR4 KSRF specialization. P222 accepts no physical derived coupling.
No accepted action/state map identifies degree with a baryon or light nucleus,
and no accepted normalization turns the conditional coefficient into a
particle mass or measured ratio. A stationary branch is not a minimum, and a
bounded scalar search along that branch is not a lower bound over every profile
and every positive coupling.

## Compatibility and consumers

MR5 and its sole pending consumer MR6 use current SciPy `trapezoid`; neither has
legacy NumPy access, and no version event is a scientific failure. Eight graph
checks pin both sources, reuse their clean expensive native executions, retain
MR6 as pending, and confirm that no new MR5 API enters a consumer.

The terminal disposition is `duplicate_evidence`. The exact owners remain
