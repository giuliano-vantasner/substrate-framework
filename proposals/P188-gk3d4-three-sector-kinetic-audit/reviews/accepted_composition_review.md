# P188 Accepted-Composition Claim-Level Review

P188 proposes no new accepted claim. This review assesses every accepted claim
used to qualify GK3D4; none is changed, superseded, or broadened by the source
runtime or by the corrected composition.

## C-LIE-001 — unchanged

The source's eight Gell-Mann-half matrices reproduce the accepted fundamental
SU(3) metric `Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2`. This is a convention-specific carrier
identity only. It does not identify color, charged matter, a loop action, or a
kinetic coefficient. Review remains accepted and epistemic status remains
active.

## C-REP-002 — unchanged

The Pauli-half generators used by GK3D4 have the accepted fundamental SU(2)
metric. C-REP-002 establishes the carrier algebra and representation ceilings,
not a physical weak sector or a loop weight. Review remains accepted and
epistemic status remains active.

## C-PGA-001 — unchanged

The exact product carrier is `C^3 tensor C^2`. Its embedded color and isospin
generators have full-carrier trace blocks `I_8` and `(3/2) I_3`; the Abelian
block is `6 y^2`, and all cross blocks vanish. These spectator dimensions
refute GK3D4's inference from equal raw fundamental indices to equal product
weights. C-PGA-001 still supplies no fields, determinant, action, kinetic term,
matter selection, or physical product gauge group.

## C-DIM-009 — unchanged

The rational one-half trace factor is dimensionless, but dimensionlessness
cannot transport a D2 loop into D4 or supply its determinant, numerator,
regulator, subtraction, counterterm, statistics, or matching law. The source's
fabricated D-dependent counterexample tests only a necessary bookkeeping fact,
not the claimed sufficiency. Review remains accepted and epistemic status
remains active.

## C-VAC-002 — unchanged

C-VAC-002 conditionally owns the D4 Dirac determinant and its exact one-loop
weight only after the charged field, tensor convention, propagator, spinor
trace, and regulator are declared. GK3D4 supplies none of that construction
for a product matter carrier and cannot infer a physical three-sector loop by
substituting three labels into one expression.

## C-VAC-003 — unchanged

C-VAC-003 owns the factor weight `b=W_s/3+4 W_f/3` and the complete affine
family `Z=Z_ref+b log(mu_ref/mu)/(8 pi^2)`. It expressly retains the finite
matching coordinate and factor data. GK3D4's common zero-boundary denominator
is a conditional specialization, not a derived total normalization.

## C-VAC-004 — unchanged

C-VAC-004 composes the accepted affine family with consistently paired scale
maps while retaining `Z_ref`, matter weights, conversions, and physical-label
ceilings. Applying it separately to each independent factor gives the exact
ratio residual

`b_i z_j-b_j z_i+b_i b_j (L_j-L_i)/(8 pi^2)`.

Only zero factor boundaries and a common logarithm remove that residual.
GK3D4 establishes neither condition universally.

## C-REP-001 — unchanged

The supplied fifteen-state table has exact trace coordinate `3/8` and zero
cross trace. Equality with a coupling coordinate holds if and only if the two
inverse-trace coefficients are common. Equal independent couplings give `1/2`
instead, and a paired Abelian rescaling moves the coordinate to `3/23` at
`rho=2` while preserving the conditional law. No physical weak angle or
preferred hypercharge normalization is promoted.

## C-RGE-005 — unchanged

C-RGE-005 requires Dynkin indices with spectator-factor degeneracies and
separately supplied Weyl or scalar multiplets. For one product carrier, the
full non-Abelian indices differ even though both raw fundamental indices are
one-half. The claim imports its loop weights and does not select the source's
matter spectrum, Abelian normalization, thresholds, or boundaries.

## C-ANO-001 — unchanged

The accepted five-row anomaly zero set has three real affine branches, and its
overall Abelian scale remains free. GK3D4's assertion that anomaly cancellation
forces its complete-generation assignment and five-thirds normalization is
therefore rejected. C-ANO-001 remains an exact ceiling, not support for a
unique physical charge table.

## Decision

All ten accepted claims retain their existing four-axis statuses and scopes.
Their exact composition qualifies the narrow algebra in GK3D4 without a new
claim, API, test, or release. Reserved identifier C-VAC-005 remains unpromoted.
