`ea12c1fee0dab254c4d8cdc984ee694622199e7cb5380674d689cf1fe6f0e31a`.

unconditional full-field-minimum or dynamic-stability headline.

## Refuted and Unaccepted Scope

energy after another source flow step despite positive symmetric curvature.
It is not stationary for the reported energy. Six random and one targeted
direction do not classify a full tangent space, and the source computes no
complete Hessian spectrum. Its gradient, energy crop, and boundary clamp are
not one exact variational problem. C-PDE-014 and all strict-minimum, rotating-
solution, linear-stability, fission, physical, gravity, radiation, observation,
and substrate readings remain unaccepted.
