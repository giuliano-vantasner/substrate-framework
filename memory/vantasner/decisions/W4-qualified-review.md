# W4 Qualified Review

## Decision

W4 is qualified through C-KIN-001 and accepted sine-Gordon energy, stress,
boundary, and charge ceilings. Its scalar threshold partition and expression
subtractions survive under those ceilings. No moving hidden free kink, measured
missing fraction, neutrino recoil, charge-conditioned invisibility, physical
V-A current, weak interaction, detector event, or substrate mechanism is
promoted.

## Corrected Positive Object

At total threshold `(m1+m2,0)`, the residual after one on-shell observed
particle has defect `2*m1*(m1+m2)*(1-cosh(theta))`. It is on the second target
mass shell only at zero rapidity, when both particles rest. Nonzero recoil needs
more total energy or a declared non-particle reservoir. This gives a reusable
positive theorem without inventing W4's missing state.

## Retained and Rejected Content

W4.1 and part of W4.6 are accepted duplicate scalar identities. W4.2 separates
expressions but cannot identify a breather. W4.3-W4.5 fail the hidden-particle
mass shell. W4.G1 defines its zero condition and has a both-absorbed
counterexample; W4.G2 turns boosted excess into negative residual rather than
closing drive work. The dossier admits no spatial absorbed/reflected
measurement was performed.
