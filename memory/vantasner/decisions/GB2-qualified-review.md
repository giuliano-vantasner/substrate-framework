# Review of GB2 Terminal Qualification

## Claim Under Review

GB2 claims that energy conservation fixes a number of soft quanta and their
characteristic per-quantum energy through floor division. The review must
separate exact quotient arithmetic from states, dynamics, spectra, and channel
interpretations while closing every predicate, dependency, cycle, and consumer.

## Sourced Inputs

The review reads v0.98.0, C-BRN-001's physical-channel ceiling, PN2's qualified
disposition, P090 and P110's governed no-claim arithmetic surface, P123's frozen
and amended contracts, all append-only attempts, both exact verifiers, and all
evidence records. GB2 is pinned at SHA-256
`e76bf26d134f48b74ba1d23bc90c5ee49d3e980edc10304a24b85af421c2b54c`.

Queue formulas and advertised conclusions were exposed before freeze; source
body, predicate detail, and runtime output were not. No numerical comparator
selected the candidates or verdict.

## Independence

The independent review imports neither P123's verifier nor a campaign helper.
It uses fresh scaled-integer `divmod`, rational cross-multiplication, one-sided
boundary probes, finite-error sequences, a separately written finite guard,
and physical countermodels. Its twenty checks agree with the fifty-five-check
primary route.

## Verification Status

For an exact nonnegative total and positive same-unit divisor, the unique floor
quotient and half-open remainder are exact, including quotient zero. For fixed
positive total, quotient plateaus have open lower and closed upper divisor
endpoints; the staircase is weakly nonincreasing, left-continuous at exact
divisors, and jumps downward immediately to their right.

When the quotient is positive, the arithmetic mean equals the unit plus the
remainder divided by the quotient. Its relative excess is strictly below one
over the quotient. A fixed-unit increasing-total path approaches a fixed unit;
a fixed-total shrinking-unit path establishes only a ratio to a moving unit.

The source executes nineteen predicates from eleven static sites. Exact and
independent routes extend its finite examples and expose binary floor
sensitivity at `0.3/0.1`. No quadrature, NumPy integration alias, numerical
solver, or fitted comparator occurs.

## Preserved Failures

The immutable proposal incorrectly called the divisor staircase
right-continuous. Attempt 0003 corrects the active contract to left-continuous
without rewriting the freeze. An atomic patch-anchor miss, a SymPy fractional-
part normal-form limitation, and an invalid mutation fixture are separately
preserved. None changed a domain, theorem, candidate criterion, or source
verdict.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

The exact quotient changes when only one energy scale changes and is invariant
only under common scaling. Binary representation can change a boundary count.
The source's finite guard accepts a lookup table that is wrong off-grid and
rejects a valid floor restricted to one plateau because it unnecessarily
requires sample variation.

Unequal constituent partitions share the same total and arithmetic mean, so no
individual energy assignment follows. A zero matrix element preserves the
quotient while making a transition rate zero. Common scaling preserves the
count while moving every alleged characteristic energy.

## Framework Compatibility

P090 and P110 already govern the exact floor-and-remainder bookkeeping ceiling
without an accepted claim or canonical API. GB2's finite mean error and
path-qualified relative limit are immediate elementary consequences. No
accepted consumer requires a new module, and no framework invariant changes.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

PN2 supplies qualified arithmetic only. GB2 names GB5 as its intended spectral
consumer while GB5 names GB2 as its premise; that candidate cycle grants no
authority. GB5, GB6, and WN7 replay 101 checks. GB5 relabels the free unit as a
spectral peak, while GB6 and WN7 are finite lexical guards. P110 already pins
the wider fifteen-consumer PN2 surface.

## Competing Candidate Audit

Literal reproduction, exact division, finite-error and limit analysis,
staircase classification, prior nonduplication, cycle audit, physical

exact, countermodel, and no-release candidates win on dependency closure,
correct limits, parameter economy, sensitivity, and physical honesty.

## Four-Axis Decision

The review accepts no new claim and terminally qualifies GB2.

- Verification: exact conditional arithmetic and exact countermodels
