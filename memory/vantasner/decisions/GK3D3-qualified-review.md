# GK3D3 Qualified Review

GK3D3 is qualified. Its exact inverse-length energy ratio, conditional
transmutation-log reduction, conversion cancellation, explicitly imposed
zero-matching formula, and common length-rescaling identity survive after
generalization through C-VAC-004.

Its physical and total-normalization headlines do not survive. C-RGE-003 does
not identify either formal length with substrate granularity or a soliton
Compton scale. Equal `hbar*c` conversions, unit soliton coefficient, and the
matching-scale map are additional premises. C-VAC-003 retains `Z_ref`, so the
general result is `Z_ref+b/(b0*g^2)`, not a parameter-free inverse coupling.
The beta coefficient, coupling coordinate, matter weight, and boundary all
remain supplied.

Dimensional exponent zero is a constant rather than a logarithm, and
dimensions alone do not select a loop function. Several rational functions of
`log(y)` are exact counterexamples to the claimed uniqueness. The hardcoded
`b0=7` and `beta^2=0.245` sample inequality does not establish general
perturbative or leading-log validity.

GK3D1 and GK3D2 are already-qualified narrative-cycle predecessors and gain no
retroactive authority. GK3D4, GK3D5, and GK3D6 remain separately governed
forward consumers; EL2 and HE5 gain no new particle or gauge interpretation.
GK3D3 and its canonical implementation have no NumPy integration surface.
GK3D5's current-first lazy legacy fallback remains compatibility evidence, not
a scientific failure.
