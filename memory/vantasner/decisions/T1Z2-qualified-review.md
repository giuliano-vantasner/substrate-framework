# Review of T1Z2 Terminal Qualification

## Claim Under Review

T1Z2 claims that a half-strength linking holonomy, kink and antikink integer
parity, an RP2 deck action, and an SU2 central rotation are one common physical
C2 character rather than selected equal scalar evaluations.

## Verification and Independence

All ten source predicates reproduce. Thirty primary and twenty-one independent
exact checks reconstruct the restricted sign arithmetic, enumerate complete
finite character tables, inspect the source AST, compare the cited formal
exchange value, and test typed-domain, host, map, kernel, representation, and
input mutations. One primary prose-versus-AST and one independent lambda-name
verifier failure are preserved before repair.

## Exact Surface and Ceiling

The half-strength holonomy restricted to integer links equals the accepted
integer parity character. Odd charges map to minus one, even links to plus one,
and the SU2 two-pi doublet matrix is minus identity. These are already
C-TOP-001 and C-CHR-001 surfaces.

The headline set-cardinality check compares only scalars. C4 and independent
C2-product characters share its selected values while differing as functions
and in their kernels. T1Z2 supplies no common source domain, quotient maps,
generator orientation, representation action, or intertwiner. Its deck matrix
is returned from a host-name branch, so no topology or medium enforcement is
derived.

## Exchange and Physical Scope

The final predicate usefully keeps parity separate from exchange but assigns
`i/4`. The cited Lean theorem gives the phase factor `exp(i/4)`; unlike `i/4`,
it has unit modulus. The Finkelstein-Rubinstein primary theorem has quantum
kink-field, continuity, state-functional, and angular-momentum premises absent
from T1Z2. Pending S2 supplies no accepted premise.

## Four-Axis Decision

No new claim is accepted. T1Z2 is terminally qualified as exact restricted
character-composition evidence, audited, compatible with C-TOP-001 and
C-CHR-001, and epistemically qualified. It challenges or supersedes no claim.
