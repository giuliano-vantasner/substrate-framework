The review checks whether FS4 changes `C-SG-009`, `C-MOM-002`, or `C-GW-004`,
and whether its static form-factor narrative warrants a distinct claim. The
proposed source-unit disposition is `duplicate_evidence`.

## Sourced Inputs

the hash-pinned FS4 source, its successful reproduction, and its source
adjudication. Pending T2C and G4 remain outside accepted closure.

## Independence

The independent route performs trace subtraction, differentiation,

without calling the new campaign verifier or source routines.

## Existing-Claim Decisions

The three existing claims retain their statements and verification axes.
`C-SG-009` is unchanged because it already gives the entire exact moment and
FS4 adds only a sampled mean. `C-MOM-002` is unchanged because its positive-
derivative theorem subsumes arbitrary constant offsets. `C-GW-004` is
unchanged because its convention-correct derivative-based TT readout and power
are already invariant under those offsets.

## Verification Status

Twenty-six exact/audit checks and eight independent checks support the
duplicate classification. They do not add verification status to existing
claims. The source's finite-difference comparison is regression evidence for
an exact identity and its power magnitude uses the wrong convention.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Quadratic and cubic time-dependent offsets change the waveform and power
derivatives. Arbitrary distinct constants produce equally valid decompositions
of the same mean, proving that differentiation cannot identify one piece as a
form factor. A static nonzero scalar radiates only after a separate time law is
inserted.

## Framework Compatibility

backreaction, and physical-radiation readings conflict with explicit accepted
scope boundaries unless new source and interaction dynamics are derived.
