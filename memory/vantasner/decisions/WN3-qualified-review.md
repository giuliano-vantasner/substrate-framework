# WN3 Qualified Review

WN3 is qualified through C-SG-019, C-CMB-001, and C-OSC-001. Its classical
activity scaling, exact algebraic Fock ladder, factorial vacuum norm, finite
edge defect, and conditional positive-odd one-factorial composition survive
after domain, coordinate, parity, and sample-space conventions are explicit.

The physical headline does not survive. The factorial norm is a ladder
normalization or contraction factor, not a density of distinct single-mode
states. The low-sector coefficient square lacks a high-sector element,
interaction Hamiltonian, energy rule, spectral density, units, and parameter
provenance, so it is not a Fermi-Golden-Rule rate.

Bosonic multiplication cannot reopen a zero even cosine order. WN3's guard
switches from positive-odd composition to a distinct all-positive family, and
its first-argmax grid hides exact integer ties. WN2's guard remains rejected.
WN4 through WN7 and MD1 through MD6 replay within the sixteen-node 637-check
closure but remain individually pending. No source has a legacy NumPy
quadrature surface, so compatibility changes no scientific verdict.
