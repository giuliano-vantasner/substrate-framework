# WN1 Qualified Review

WN1 is qualified through C-SG-019 and C-CMB-001. Its unit-background
coefficient, parity, exact inverse-square factorial values, selected decimal
floors, rigorous universal suppression bound, exact decade ceilings, and
exact-versus-machine-zero distinction survive after normalization and
quantifier repair.

The source verifier does not itself prove its universal headlines. Its
exponential comparisons use finite grids, its decade results use sixty-digit
mpmath values without enclosures, and its superpolynomial helper tests only
one order and three powers. P189 supplies the analytic positive-series,
rational/integer block, and every-fixed-power geometric-tail proofs.

The source's Golden-rule and PN2-band readings remain unaccepted. A classical
Taylor coefficient square is not a complete matrix element or rate, and PN2
supplies no accepted physical subdivision band. Amplitude, coordinate scales,
background, quantum states, interaction, on-shell measure, spectral density,
and validity regime remain separate premises.

WN2 through WN7 and MD1 through MD6 replay cleanly but remain individually
pending. No source in the thirteen-node graph has a legacy NumPy quadrature
surface, so compatibility produces no scientific failure or alias debt.
