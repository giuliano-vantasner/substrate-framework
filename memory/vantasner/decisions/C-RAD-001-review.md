two outgoing fluxes, total source-work balance, field-rescaling invariance, and
a static same-equation boundary countermodel from one explicitly declared
canonical scalar action. It establishes no dilaton, graviton, breather,
physical coupling, radiation reaction, observation, or substrate mechanism.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Changing the two-side sum to one side, one quarter, or twice its value breaks
source-work equality. Doubling, halving, or zeroing B breaks the field and power
ledger. Reversing B changes the field but not power, while q=0 gives zero.
Field rescaling preserves B squared over A. The static absolute-value field has
the same source jump and zero flux, proving boundary history is load bearing.
Under G1's own normalization the checked power is off by four even after its
derivative substitution, and a constant retarded source defeats its extra time
derivative. The fixed-time scalar-trace integral has an inverse-gamma Jacobian,
and an accelerated static kink has nonzero residual at its center.
