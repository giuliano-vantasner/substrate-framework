# Review of CM5 Terminal Qualification

## Claim Under Review

CM5 claims direct coherent-to-EM output at twice the breather frequency,
tracking of CM2 controls, N-rings-squared geometric concentration, voltage-slew
seeding, and a frequency shared with the heat channel. The review requires the
exact waveform, average, units, radiator, array, input-coupling, dependency,
predicate, and consumer ledgers plus a novelty decision.

## Sourced Inputs

The review reads v0.97.0, C-GW-001, C-COH-001, C-SG-017, C-CMP-001,
C-RES-001, their canonical modules and immutable audits, FS4's duplicate
disposition, P087/P088's seeding audits, P119's frozen contract, all attempts,
both verifiers, and every P119 evidence record. CM5 is pinned at SHA-256
`8af42e5229ba59b31dfb30dbf94e904a2670c4f2f2b57373f9dd25ab169c2841`.

Every one of the eighteen runtime predicates is audited. Exact conditional
harmonic algebra survives; electrical radiation, total-power, Dicke, geometry,
seeding, heat-correlation, material, magnitude, yield, and observation readings
remain outside the accepted surface.

## Independence

The independent route imports no P119 implementation. It rotates the
cosine-sine coefficient pair under three derivatives, applies exact cycle
orthogonality, reconstructs dimensions and competing derivative functionals,
expands the declared B family, compares accepted loss endpoints, derives fresh
phasor and normalization counterexamples, and reads the finite Gaussian and
registry directly.

Its first array endpoint failed because an unsimplified polynomial was compared
structurally. Two later registry probes used paraphrased rather than exact
ceiling substrings. All three implementation defects are preserved; their
repairs use exact algebra and authoritative registry text rather than weakening
any physics gate.

## Verification Status

The surviving conditional surface is symbolic verified but earns no new
claim. Sixty-six primary checks and thirty-four independent checks establish
the exact one-half, phase and static limits, dimensions, alternative
functionals, loss mismatch, array normalization, finite-width DC value,
finite-window terms, zero-coupling countermodels, shared-value semantics,
mutations, and accepted-claim match.

CM5 itself uses no sampled integration. Its mutable seeding and geometry
support now uses `np.trapezoid` directly. One ideal-coherence aggregate replay
was blocked before the changed modules by absent Matplotlib; direct module
self-tests and imports pass, so no scientific candidate is charged with that
unrelated optional dependency.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

The harmonic one-half, derivative order, frequency exponent, B composition,
and finite-width DC positivity are mutation sensitive. A second-derivative
functional and a third-derivative functional can each fit one selected target
through arbitrary unitful couplings. Zero EM coupling removes output without
changing the waveform.

Four equal emitters give directional intensity sixteen when aligned and zero
for quarter-turn phases. Fixed-total normalization removes one power of N, and
a narrowing N-squared focus can retain constant integrated power. The source
growth inequality accepts N to the three-halves and its focus helper accepts
noninteger and nonpositive ring counts.

At finite width `chi_b(0)>0`; DC, ramp, and sinusoid derivatives differ;
finite-window Fourier differentiation retains endpoints; varying constitutive
response adds a product term; and zero interaction maps every slew to zero
seed. An independently constructed equal frequency passes the source's shared
guard, so it tests value rather than provenance or channel identity.

## Framework Compatibility

C-GW-001 already owns exact conditional harmonic third-derivative calculus and
its physical-radiation ceiling. C-COH-001 owns directional phase-ensemble
scaling and normalization. C-SG-017 owns the conditional physical breather
frequency. C-CMP-001 and C-RES-001 own the accepted loss formulas and explicitly
withhold rates, heat, and channels. No accepted invariant is changed.

The source's B family conflicts with accepted loss behavior, and its geometry
and seeding imports do not close physical premises. A new claim or module would
therefore manufacture novelty around an unaccepted interpretation.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

FS4 is duplicate evidence; CM2 and PN4 are qualified only through accepted
conditional algebra. `medium_omega0`, `seeding_kernel`, and `spark_geometry`
remain noncanonical source support. CM1, CM6, and CM7 are direct cycle or scan
consumers; CM3, GB6, and WN7 are indirect descendants.

Three direct consumers replay sixty checks and three transitive consumers
replay ninety-eight, for 158 clean checks. They inherit no EM, power, seeding,
heat, or observation claim. The debt ledger is empty.

## Competing Candidate Audit

Literal reproduction, exact harmonic calculus, competing radiator laws, array
normalization, input coupling, accepted-claim reuse, countermodels, and

on dependency closure and parameter economy. Source agreement and prior tally
exposure select nothing.
