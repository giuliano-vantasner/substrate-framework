# WN4 Qualified Review

WN4 is qualified through C-OSC-001 and C-CMB-003. Its normalized factorial-one
mass, adjacent ratio, complete modes, strict log-concavity, PGF,
falling-factorial moments, exact geometric majorants, and every-fixed-power
tail decay survive on the declared all-nonnegative counting sample space.

Its headline does not survive intact. Strict log-concavity permits the exact
adjacent mode tie at positive integer S, the step residual is `S-(n+1)` rather
than `S-n`, and the PN2 band predicates do not evaluate the weight. The source
defines no power-law interpolation and supplies no interaction, spectral,
dynamical, rate, time, unit, or medium premise capable of turning the
mathematical mass into phase-space, energy-gap, Poisson-process, subdivision,
material, or prediction physics.

The source graph covers seventeen pinned nodes and 662 native predicates.
P191's sixteen byte-identical executions are reused; newly relevant PN2
freshly passes 25 checks. No node needs a legacy quadrature alias, and WN5
through WN7 plus MD1 through MD6 remain individually pending.
