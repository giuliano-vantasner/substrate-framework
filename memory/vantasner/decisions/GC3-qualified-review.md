# GC3 Qualified Review

## Decision

GC3 is qualified through C-QBL-001, C-QBL-003, C-OVL-001, C-MIX-001,
C-MIX-002, and C-MIX-003. Its globally-phased-real matrix theorem survives;
the one-condensate ontology and physical CP headline do not.

## Retained Scope

Both Grams cancel scalar global phases, real left bases may be chosen, the
relative basis then has real quartets, and the Gram commutator supplies stable
odd-trace null identities. Differently phased real source matrices can produce
nonzero finite examples, and the pinned source contains no measured input.

## Corrected and Rejected Scope

Accepted EM6 does not force complex ontology, MH1 defines no Yukawa
interaction, and FG3/FG4 define no physical CKM or CP operation. Finite random
samples are not bounded-away theorems, external wells are not coexisting
condensates, and one equal-spacing comparison predicts no geometry. Source
count K is distinct from matrix dimension N; two sources already suffice
algebraically for a nonzero quartet at N=3. No physical flavor, stability,
generation count, observation, Standard Model, or substrate claim is accepted.

## Evidence and Continuation

Every source predicate has an individual verdict. The primary, independent,
compatibility, impact, nonduplication, and source-graph artifacts are materialized
under P210. GC4 through GC6 remain pending separately reviewable consumers. The
single integrated v0.152.0 gate passed all 1,837 tests, validated 851 memory
files, and closed the P210 debt ledger.
