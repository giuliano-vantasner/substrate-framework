# W5 Qualified Review

## Decision

W5 is qualified through C-SCT-001 and accepted branching, boundary, scalar,
representation, and kinematic ceilings. Its rational power formulas survive
under corrected conventions. No piston-derived local impedance, independent
three-observable prediction, physical chirality, parity violation, V-A or weak
interaction, detector measurement, weak scale, gauge sector, or substrate
mechanism is promoted.

## Corrected Positive Object

For correctly oriented waves on `x>=0`, the passive condition is
`phi_t-zeta*phi_x=0`. It gives `r=(z-1)/(z+1)`, `R=r^2`, conditional
`T=1-R`, and nonpositive boundary energy rate. Reciprocal impedances flip `r`
but preserve the power data. A separately declared unit-reflection channel
gives `A=(1-R)/(1+R)=T/(2-T)` through C-BRN-001.

## Retained and Rejected Content

W5 reverses wave roles and uses an active boundary sign. Each mistake inverts
the amplitude ratio, so their composition hides the problem in the formula.
The piston equations retain an inertial derivative. `T` and `A` are transforms
of `R`; equal assigned channels give arithmetic zero without a parity theorem.
No physical state, current, charge, interaction, or detector is computed.
