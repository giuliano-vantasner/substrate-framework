# EM5 Qualified Source Review

## Claim Under Review

This review decides all eleven EM5 predicates and its constructive gauge-sector
headline. It is separate from accepting C-VAC-001: a correct conditional
massive scalar loop cannot blanket-accept EM5's statistics, local-action, pole,
photon, dispersion, charge, or substrate interpretations.

## Sourced Inputs

The review reads the pinned EM5 executable and dossier, native reproduction,
predicate adjudication, primary and independent derivations, source/dependency/
consumer/nonduplication/provenance audits, compatibility replays, canonical
tests, and the C-VAC-001 claim review. Both source hashes are fixed in P135.

## Independence

The decision uses a fresh scalar loop and constant-field proper-time route
rather than EM5's check booleans. It retains the inverse momentum factor in
`A*P*A`, derives the Ward cancellation before imposing transversality, mutates
statistics, and constructs field-normalization and zero-action counterexamples.

## Verification Status

EM5 executes eleven checks, but its claims are adjudicated individually.
Checks 1, 2, 7, and 10 retain only projector or counterexample algebra. Check
4 retains a narrow massive zero-momentum statement. Check 3 is refuted for
scalar statistics; check 6 is refuted by the missing inverse momentum and
dimensions; checks 5, 8, and 11 inherit an undeclared bare kernel and wrong
loop; check 9 retains only the vanishing coefficient limit.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

The scalar numerator, seagull sign, mass, charge, multiplicity, next series
term, tensor convention, field normalization, and zero-action connection all
change load-bearing verdicts. In particular the scalar massless limit diverges,
`A*P*A` retains `1/Q`, `e^2` alone changes under field rescaling, and vanishing
loop weight permits nonzero curvature when no action constrains the connection.

## Framework Compatibility

The corrected conditional theorem is compatible with C-GAU-001. EM5's physical
closure is not native: it imports or omits the quantum field, regulator, bare
kinetic term, continuation, charge identification, dimension lift, and degrees
of freedom. C-MAX-001 explicitly confirms that a local action coefficient is a
supplied premise unless independently derived in a complete theory.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

EM1, EM2, and EM3 are qualified through their existing mappings; EM7 and M1
are pending. M1 and the EM7-D3S path form cycles back to EM5. The full direct
dependency/consumer set reproduces 219 predicates. YM2 and QCD2 need immutable
alias-only legacy integration compatibility; no pending source gains authority
and no source imports EM5 executably.

## Competing Candidate Audit

The statistics-correct Candidate B and explicit-family Candidate D beat
literal Candidate A on quantum closure, Ward derivation, dimensions, limits,
and parameter honesty. Candidate C precisely describes the surviving
kinematic subset. Candidate E closes the disposition without promoting failed
physical clauses.

## Four-Axis Decision

EM5 is a qualified source unit, not an accepted claim object.

- Verification: mixed exact construction and refuted interpretation, adjudicated per predicate
- Review: audited
- Compatibility: compatible only after statistics and physical-scope correction
- Epistemic: qualified
- Accepted mapping: C-GAU-001 and C-VAC-001
