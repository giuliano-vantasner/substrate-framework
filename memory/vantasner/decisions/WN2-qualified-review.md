# WN2 Qualified Review

WN2 is qualified through C-SG-019, C-CMB-001, C-CMB-002, and C-BRN-001. Its
unit-activity parity mass, exact floor arithmetic, conditional
`N/(n!)^2` ratio, zero allocation endpoint, and normalized concentration
inequalities survive after sample-space, normalization, and API domains are
made explicit.

The universal physical refutation does not survive. A parity zero and a mode
at one are not accepted inadmissibility axioms. Activity four moves the exact
mass mode to three, and a normalized mathematical distribution may
legitimately have mode one. WN2 also mixes a global order distribution with a
fixed deterministic floor count without deriving one common physical sample
space. PN2 supplies no accepted physical band or channel.

WN3 and WN7 repeat WN2's rejected guard, while WN6 repeats its overbroad
verdict. WN2 and its eleven semantic consumers replay 524 checks, but the
consumers remain individually pending. No source in the closure has a legacy
NumPy quadrature surface, so compatibility produces no scientific failure or
alias debt.
