# EM7 Qualified Source Review

## Claim Under Review

This review decides all seventeen EM7 predicates and its constructive
dimensional-bridge headline. It is separate from accepting C-KRN-002: a
correct conditional critical limit cannot blanket-accept EM7's geometry,
endpoint selection, gauge lift, force, or substrate interpretations.

## Sourced Inputs

The review reads the pinned EM7 executable and dossier, native reproduction,
predicate adjudication, primary and independent derivations, source/dependency/
consumer/nonduplication/provenance audits, compatibility replays, canonical
tests, and the C-KRN-002 claim review. Both source hashes are fixed in P136.

## Independence

The decision uses fresh Schwinger/Gaussian, critical-limit, radial-flux, and
distributional routes rather than EM7's check booleans and regressions. It
keeps every domain, reference, source, probe, and dimension concept explicit.

## Verification Status

EM7 executes seventeen checks, but its claims are adjudicated individually.
The declared-symbol algebra, subcritical formula, and d=3 endpoint survive.
The critical Boolean, supercritical examples, smoothed periodic residual, and
fixed-d guards do not establish the headline. C-KRN-002 supplies the missing
critical normalization and exact family nonidentifiability.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

The unsubtracted limit diverges; reference changes add a constant; source,
probe, sign, and coefficient mutations move distinct outputs. A valid
noninteger inverse-square pair shows why fixed-d guards cannot select one
endpoint. The d=1 distributional branch and invalid FFT proxy expose the
source's regime mixing.

## Framework Compatibility

The corrected theorem is compatible with C-KRN-001 and C-MAX-001. EM7's
physical closure is not native: it imports or omits the space, measure,
operator realization, dimension identification, gauge action, source, charge,
force dictionary, dimensional lift, and observational map.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

EM3 is qualified through accepted Maxwell and kernel mappings; D3S grants only
C-LOC-001/C-KRN-001; QCD5 remains pending. D3S and QCD5 are circular with EM7.
The full direct dependency/consumer set reproduces 140 predicates. YM2 and QCD2
need immutable alias-only integration compatibility; no pending source gains
authority and no source imports EM7 executably.

## Competing Candidate Audit

Candidates B through E beat literal Candidate A on domain closure, boundary
normalization, source/probe honesty, parameter identifiability, and dimension
semantics. Candidate F closes the disposition without promoting failed
physical clauses.
