# Review of C-RES-001

## Claim Under Review

C-RES-001 states the exact finite complex block resolvent and its specialization
to two intermediates at symmetric real detunings with one declared common
negative imaginary shift. It fixes the block ordering, `E*I-H_QQ` convention,
spectral parameter, complex coupling products, loss regimes, cancellation
locus, unique positive-loss peak, and pair-count normalization. It explicitly
withholds every microscopic open-system, probability, rate, and physical
channel interpretation.

PN4's exact `E=0` equal-product pair identity falls inside the claim. Its

observation readings remain outside the claim delta.

## Independence

The primary route calls the canonical API and also reconstructs declared
blocks. The independent reviewer imports no canonical paired-resolvent
implementation. It combines denominators afresh, partitions and inverts a
complete four-by-four matrix, differentiates the magnitude independently, and
derives the lossless short-time transfer from the squared full Hamiltonian.

## Verification Status

The maximum verdict is `symbolic_verified`. Exact complex rational algebra,
finite matrix inversion, derivatives, and limits establish the displayed
theorem. No unevaluated integral, numerical solver, floating comparator, or
simulation enters the accepted proof. PN4's NumPy and SciPy run is regression
and source-predicate evidence only. It contains no sampled quadrature and has
no `np.trapz` compatibility event.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Changing the `E-H` orientation, common-shift sign, half-width, spectral energy,
or equality and phase of coupling products breaks the corresponding verdict.
Zero loss away from `E=0` and unequal products at `E=0` disprove generic
cancellation. Exact boundary limits disprove monotone opening. Fixed-total
coupling disproves automatic growth with pair count. The lossless full model
has `(H^2)_BA=2*L*g^2`, disproving zero lossless transfer despite the on-shell
effective cancellation. Nonunitary norm balance separates a raw endpoint
component from a normalized probability.

## Framework Compatibility

The theorem is an additive finite-dimensional complex-matrix surface and
changes no accepted invariant. C-EFT-001 uses real stationary elimination and
does not supply a complex spectral shift. C-DYN-001 distinguishes abstract
damping from physical decoherence or material loss. Their ceilings remain
unchanged and naturally require the claim's interpretation boundary.
