# Review of CF5 Duplicate Evidence

## Claims Under Review

The review checks whether CF5 changes `C-VTX-001`, `C-VTX-002`, or
`C-FLX-001`, and whether its effective-area composition warrants a distinct
claim. The proposed source-unit disposition is `duplicate_evidence`.

## Sourced Inputs

The review read `v0.25.0`, all three accepted claims and their canonical APIs,
P029, both exact routes, the hash-pinned CF5 source, its failed reproduction,
and the check-family adjudication.

## Independence

The main route composes accepted package APIs and audits mutations. The
independent route solves the ideal-tube equation, eliminates the area, derives
the dimensionless ratio and source window, and checks profile absence without
calling vortex or flux-tube helpers.

## Existing-Claim Decisions

The three accepted claims retain their existing statements and status axes.
`C-VTX-001` is unchanged: CF5 repeats its flux and vector inverse length but
adds no physical field map. `C-VTX-002` is unchanged: CF5's current source
fails before a check, and its repeated same-family solve is weaker than the
accepted refinement evidence. `C-FLX-001` is unchanged: it already records the
effective-area inversion as input reconstruction, exactly the CF5 closure.

## Verification Status

Twenty exact main checks and six independent checks support the duplicate
classification. They do not add verification status to any accepted claim;
the source reproduction itself exits one with no tally.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Coefficient mutations break the inverse identity, confirming what the algebra
depends on. But every positive alternative tension passes after its area is
redefined. The source window accepts a factor-1000 interval and passes large
tension mutations. Distinct scalar/vector lengths and a free area factor reject
a unique profile-core interpretation.

## Framework Compatibility

The narrow algebra is compatible but already accepted. Physical equivalence is
incompatible with the explicit interpretation boundaries of all three claims
unless a new profile geometry and sector map are independently derived.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

CF5 has no distinct scientific consumer beyond the three subsuming claims. The
campaign uses their existing APIs directly and adds no package helper. Focused
vortex and flux tests replay the consumers.

## Competing Candidate Audit

Candidate A is selected because exact elimination proves duplication. Candidate
B lacks independent information and consumer reach. Candidate C lacks both a
smooth-to-uniform area map and a physical sector identity.

## Four-Axis Decision

No new claim receives four-axis promotion. The existing claim axes remain
unchanged, and CF5 is retained as noncanonical duplicate evidence.

## Promotion Transaction

There is no registry or release promotion. The transaction freezes P029,
changes only CF5's migration disposition and duplicate mappings, updates parent
memory, and replays the unchanged accepted boundary.

## Continuation if Not Accepted

A future profile-defined area would require a new proposal with a frozen
support criterion, independent geometric observable, and sensitivity tests. It
cannot retroactively turn this inversion into a prediction.

## Done Gate

Information elimination, window sensitivity, geometry, source failure,
subsuming claims, consumers, terminal disposition, and debt closure are complete.
