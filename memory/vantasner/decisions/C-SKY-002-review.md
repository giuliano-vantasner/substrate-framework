# C-SKY-002 Review

## Claim Under Review

C-SKY-002 gives exact conditional pointwise square certificates for the
declared O(4) quadratic-plus-quartic static density and four-component kinetic
mass operator. It includes the sharp `M>=2*I` bound and the exact Derrick
scale-family identity, with stationarity kept as a separate equation.

## Independence and Sensitivity

The primary route exercises the canonical API on generic exact real entries.
The independent route imports no `skyrme_o4` code and freshly expands all
eighteen quartic and eighteen mass-gap minors. A quartic sign flip, deleted
mass identity term, positive-curvature nonstationary Derrick example, and
TX5's direct nonstationary descent all break broader readings. A parallel
equality witness proves the accepted coefficient cannot be strengthened.

## Four-Axis Decisions

C-SKY-002 is `symbolic_verified`, `accepted`, `compatible_extension`, and
`active`, with no challenge or supersession edge. C-PDE-014 is separately
`unverified`, `rejected`, `unassessed`, and `refuted` for TX5 because no
stationary discrete field or complete constrained Hessian exists.

## Source and Compatibility Decision

TX5's pointwise formula and Derrick identity survive only at C-SKY-002's
conditional scope. At its declared `N=91` field, a stable negative first
derivative and lower one-step energy coexist with positive symmetric curve
curvature. The source and mutable campaign have no legacy NumPy trapezoidal
surface, so compatibility creates no scientific failure.
