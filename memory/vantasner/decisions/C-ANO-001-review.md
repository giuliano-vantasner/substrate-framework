# C-ANO-001 Claim Review

## Claim Under Review

C-ANO-001 is an exact conditional theorem for a supplied left-handed
`G_a x SU(2) x U(1)` table. It derives local anomaly coefficient sums, supplied
fundamental-doublet parity, conjugation covariance, and the complete
three-line local anomaly variety of one fixed five-row carrier.

## Sourced Inputs

The review read release v0.126.0, C-REP-001, C-REP-002, C-REP-003, C-LIE-001,
their canonical modules, both source-aware P165 revisions, hash-pinned SM3 and
its dossier, every attempt, the primary literature audit, source and predicate
audits, dependency, nonduplication, consumer, impact, and fifteen-node graph
records. The quantum anomaly conditions remain explicit external theory.

## Independence

The canonical route uses `chiral_anomalies.py`. The independent route imports
none of its APIs and separately performs linear elimination, a Gröbner-basis
calculation, factorization, three branch substitutions, bounded exact
enumeration, homogeneity checks, and representation mutations.

## Verification Status

All charges, dimensions, indices, coefficients, residuals, factors, and branch
memberships are exact SymPy expressions. The primary route passes 31 checks,
the independent route passes 12, and the source graph passes 25. The strongest
verdict is symbolic verification of the conditional coefficient and affine
zero-set theorem, not formal derivation of quantum anomaly theory or physical
matter.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Electron-charge removal reopens cubic and gravitational coefficients. A wrong
conjugate cubic sign reopens SU3 cubed; removing the lepton doublet makes the
supplied fundamental count odd. Common scaling has the expected degrees. The
row-exchanged and zero-q vectorlike lines exactly refute uniqueness up to
scale, and a neutral singlet refutes carrier completeness.

## Framework Compatibility

The theorem is a compatible extension. Accepted representation and trace
claims supply exact normalizations and scope boundaries without selecting the
table. No existing production module imports the new API. GitNexus reports LOW
risk, one internal direct caller, and no affected execution flow.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

The fifteen-node graph replays 132 lexical and runtime checks plus fifteen
assertions, all natively. Downstream scripts retype or reuse supplied tables or
carry SM3 only in prose. Existing accepted claims already exclude the rejected
anomaly-forced interpretation; pending consumers gain no authority.

## Competing Candidate Audit

Eight candidates were registered before renewed source audit. Literal replay
missed solution completeness; accepted composition lacked a coefficient and
branch API; Yukawa and physical-generation routes added undeclared premises.
The generic ledger plus complete variety wins by exact closure, assumption
economy, mutation sensitivity, and API novelty rather than familiar charges.
