# Review of PN5 Terminal Qualification

## Claim Under Review

PN5 claims that selected external energy inputs reproduce a named kinematic
count magnitude and separately regresses the loss optimum of one declared toy
pair. The review asks whether either surface supplies a distinct positive claim
beyond P110's exact quotient evidence and C-RES-001, and whether the source's
reproduction, physical, literature, and consumer readings survive.

## Sourced Inputs

The review reads release v0.94.0, the full accepted registry, C-RES-001, the
immutable P110 and P112 campaigns, P113's frozen contract, PN5 at SHA-256
`8b2a35a873d9414653f9add48ceeed50d0d32142064bd67b95845b92aeaf87eb`,
all append-only attempts, primary and independent verifiers, every source
predicate verdict, the literature and dependency audits, the PN5-PN4 cycle,
and two direct plus twenty-two transitive pinned consumers.

PN5's exact selected counts and pair optimum are inside existing governance.
Its magnitude-reproduction, quantum-subdivision, mechanism, prediction, rate,
yield, material, observation, and retraction readings remain outside that
surface.

## Independence

The primary route uses exact Fraction arithmetic and the canonical C-RES-001
API. The independent reviewer imports no paired-resolvent implementation. It
uses scaled-integer `divmod`, Decimal, Fraction threshold families, and a fresh
symbolic derivative, peak, and endpoint calculation.

## Verification Status

The maximum verdict for the surviving surfaces is `symbolic_verified`, but no
new claim earns promotion. Exact integer and rational arithmetic establishes
the count ledger, while exact calculus already establishes the pair theorem in
C-RES-001. PN5's binary64 grid is regression coverage only. Its source imports
NumPy but calls no integration routine, and no `np.trapz` compatibility event
occurs.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

Changing decimal-prefix exponents, total-energy scale independently, plateau
endpoint orientation, factor two in the optimum, detuning, or coupling power
breaks the corresponding verdict. Exact 0.3/0.1 versus binary floating floor
exposes representation sensitivity. Common rescaling leaves all counts
unchanged, and `unit=total/n` manufactures arbitrary target counts. Zero
coupling preserves the count list and removes the toy element. The source's
numeric optimum tolerance accepts nearby wrong values, while exact limits
replace its two finite endpoint samples.

## Framework Compatibility

Terminal qualification changes no framework invariant. P110 already records
the quotient, remainder, plateau, scale, and representation ceiling without a
new claim. C-RES-001 already owns the pair identity, unique peak, limits,
normalization, and physical ceiling. Natural composition therefore requires no
new claim, API, or release.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

PN2 contributes only immutable P110 bookkeeping evidence; PN4 contributes only
accepted C-RES-001. Their source-level cycle grants no authority. Qualified PN4
and pending PN6 are direct consumers, and twenty-two transitive consumers are
pinned. None gains derived energy inputs, states, a bath, interaction,

## Competing Candidate Audit

Literal reproduction, exact arithmetic, accepted pair specialization,
representation and input countermodels, set semantics, literature and consumer
closure, and nonduplication were frozen before PN5 execution. Exact dependency
closure selects terminal mapping to existing governance; numerical overlap
with a named decade does not select or validate a concept.

## Four-Axis Decision

The review accepts no new claim and terminally qualifies PN5 as mixed evidence.

- Verification: existing exact surfaces remain `symbolic_verified`
- Review: PN5 terminal disposition `qualified`
- Compatibility: native mapping to existing governance
- Epistemic: no new claim; source evidence qualified
- Relationship: challenges and supersedes none
