# EM3 Qualified Source Review

## Claim Under Review

This review decides the disposition of all eleven EM3 predicates and its
Maxwell-sector headline. It is separate from accepting C-MAX-001: a correct
conditional action theorem does not blanket-accept EM3's signs, dimension
selection, source ontology, comparator, or substrate interpretation.

## Sourced Inputs

The review reads the pinned EM3 executable and dossier, native reproduction,
predicate adjudication, primary and independent derivations, source/dependency/
consumer/nonduplication audits, compatibility replays, canonical tests, and the
C-MAX-001 claim review. The executable and dossier hashes are fixed in P134.

## Independence

The review uses fresh action and flux derivations rather than EM3's check
booleans. It expands the source's omitted `d>=4` family, substitutes both
potential signs into the actual action equation, constructs a neutral dipole,
and constructs a non-pure connection allowed by the source-only zero action.

## Verification Status

EM3's executable status is eleven checks passed, but its claims are adjudicated
individually. Checks 1 through 3 survive under explicit action premises; check
4 needs the opposite A0 convention; check 5 survives with source normalization;
check 6 is regression; check 7 is only partly correct; check 8 is conditional;
check 9 mixes exact dimensions with imported comparison and medium mapping;
checks 10 and 11 overstate their premises.

## Sensitivity and Counterexamples

The source sign, dimension, kinetic coefficient, source charge, probe charge,
boundary, neutral dipole, and non-pure connection probes all change the
load-bearing outputs. In particular `d=4` decays, zero net charge can retain a
field, and deleting the kinetic term supplies `j=0` rather than `A=dchi`.

## Framework Compatibility

The corrected action theorem is a compatible extension of C-GAU-001 and
C-KRN-001. EM3's physical closure is not native: it imports the kinetic term,
current normalization, dimension, point source, test charge, force dictionary,
medium map, and CODATA values. Pending gravity dependencies supply no repair.

## Dependency and Consumer Replay

EM2 is qualified through C-GAU-001; G1, G2, and G3 are pending. All three and
the fifteen reverse consumers reproduce 227 checks, with compatibility-only
aliases for G1, YM2, and QCD2. None imports EM3 as Python. The replay preserves
historical behavior but grants no pending conclusion authority.

## Competing Candidate Audit

The exact conditional Candidates B through D beat literal Candidate A on
action closure, signs, dimension completeness, boundary data, and parameter
honesty. Accepted composition alone misses the action content, while the
countermodel route exposes precisely which source clauses require rejection.

## Four-Axis Decision

EM3 is a qualified source unit, not an accepted claim object.

- Verification: mixed exact and regression evidence, adjudicated per predicate
- Review: audited
- Compatibility: compatible only after corrected conditional boundaries
- Epistemic: qualified
- Accepted mapping: C-GAU-001, C-KRN-001, and C-MAX-001

## Promotion Transaction

The editable disposition registry marks EM3 qualified with structured reasons
and evidence. The generated queue is rebuilt from that registry. No source file
or dossier is rewritten.

## Continuation if Not Accepted

This clause remains active for excluded physical claims: a future proposal may
construct a charged material or electromagnetic sector, but cannot inherit it
from EM3. It must separately derive every action, source, dimension, and
observational premise.

## Done Gate

Every predicate, source import, sign, dimension, guard, comparator, dependency,
consumer, compatibility event, mapping, and interpretation ceiling is resolved.
EM3 may therefore leave the pending queue as qualified.
