# G3 Qualified Review

## Decision

G3 is qualified through C-STG-001. Its exact canonical scalar stress, static
scalar variation, on-shell conservation identity, and wrong-sign kinetic guard
survive. No one-point coupling solution, breather source, refractive map,
independent route around an optical no-go, physical gravity, observation, or
substrate claim is promoted.

## Corrected Positive Object

The declared mostly-plus Einstein-Hilbert plus canonical scalar action fixes
the standard stress and coupled equations. For `V=0`, positive `kappa`, and
`t>0`, the flat-FLRW solution has scale factor proportional to `t^(1/3)`, scalar
proportional to plus or minus `log(t)`, `rho=p=1/(3*kappa*t^2)`, Ricci scalar
`-2/(3*t^2)`, and Kretschmann scalar `20/(27*t^4)`. Direct four-dimensional
curvature and package routes agree componentwise.

## Retained and Rejected Content

G3's canonical stress, trace, static scalar Euler equation, stress-divergence
factorization, joint constant-field zero-potential vacuum, and ghost sign are
retained under explicit premises. Its positive `kappa` is never used. The
source fits only `G_tt/T_tt` at one point; the result is negative, other tensor
components fail, and the scalar is off shell. Its optical ratio diverges despite
zero-coupling prose, and its nonminimal Delta is not a canonical stress or a
vacuum classifier.

## Compatibility and Closure

Native G3 passes all eleven predicates and has no NumPy integration event. The
scientific verdict is independent of version handling. Primary, independent,
graph, and focused routes pass 29, 15, 35, and 15 checks. The 14-node graph pins
135 predicates. Immutable G1, G4, and NC4 remain alias-only through
`np.trapezoid`; mutable code has no executable legacy integration access, and
GitNexus risk is LOW.
