# C-CMB-002 Review

C-CMB-002 is accepted as a symbolic compatible extension. For exact positive
dimensionless activity `z`, it defines the parity-thinned mass
`m_z(n)=1_odd(n) z^(2n)/(n!)^2` on the positive integers with counting measure.
The exact total is `(I_0(2z)-J_0(2z))/2`, and division by that positive total
gives a normalized mathematical mass function.

For exact positive rational activity and an odd cutoff whose consecutive-tail
ratio is below one, the claim supplies an exact rational geometric enclosure.
At unit activity and cutoff nine this proves WN2's order-one and through-three
concentration thresholds with exact positive margins. The 56-check primary
oracle, 22-check independent raw route, 76 focused tests, and twelve-node
524-check semantic source replay support the theorem.

Activity is load-bearing: unit activity has mode one, while activity four has
mode three. Therefore the source's mode-one result is not normalization
invariant and cannot serve as a universal admissibility test. A mathematical
normalization does not derive occurrence frequencies, quantum states, a
complete interaction, phase space, spectral density, a transition rate, a
physical channel, a PN2 band, or a material prediction.

C-CMB-002 depends on C-CMB-001. C-BRN-001 remains a conditional composition:
positive odd mass may be supplied to its weighted specialization, while an
even zero is handled by the general allocation endpoint.
