# MD2 Qualified Review

MD2 is qualified through C-DOS-001 and C-QFL-001. Its 26 native predicates
reproduce. The one-mode variance, exact J(X) continuum integral, limits, and
fixed-set total/mean identity survive only after action normalization,
quantization, product vacuum, dimension, scalar branch, stiffness, measure,
gap, and cutoff are supplied.

The source does not derive those premises. The accepted medium is classical
and one-dimensional. AS6 and AS7 supply no beta value or granularity; MD1
supplies no microscopic cutoff. Definition substitution is not independent
overdetermination. Differentiating a continuous M at fixed total is not a
mode-set change, and `n<S` is only an algebraic rewrite without accepted
growth dynamics, state, participation, material, channel, or rate.

MD4, MD5, and MD6 remain pending. The graph has no NumPy quadrature event, so
no `np.trapz` compatibility issue contributes to the scientific verdict.

in 189.45 seconds with exit zero. The regenerated migration queue has 27
