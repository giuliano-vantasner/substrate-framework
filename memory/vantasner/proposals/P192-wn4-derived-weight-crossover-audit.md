## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-OSC-001 owns the all-nonnegative, positive, and positive-odd factorial-one
totals, exact adjacent ratios, modes, and sample-space separation for supplied
positive dimensionless intensity S. It already shows that at integer S the
positive all-order family may have adjacent tied modes. C-CMB-001 and
C-CMB-002 own the distinct inverse-square-factorial and parity-thinned Bessel
families. C-SG-019 supplies only its exact classical coefficient, and
C-BRN-001 consumes supplied weights without deriving them.

Strict log-concavity means `w_n^2>w_(n-1)w_(n+1)` on its declared interior;
it does not by itself imply a unique maximum on a discrete domain because two
adjacent equal maxima can occur. A tail statement must declare its starting
index, quantifiers, and whether it bounds ratios, absolute masses, or a
normalized tail. Identifying `exp(-S) S^n/n!` as a mathematical Poisson mass
does not derive a Poisson process, physical event count, time interval, rate,
energy-gap law, medium scale, subdivision mechanism, or material prediction.
WN5 through WN7 and MD1 through MD6 remain pending.

Mutable quadrature uses `np.trapezoid` or `trapezoid_integral`; immutable
legacy access receives an alias-only recorded replay backed by
`np.trapezoid`. A version-only stop cannot reject a scientific candidate.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

The source audit will pin syntax, imports, compatibility, runtime predicates,
ratio and crossover conventions, log-concavity, mode calculations, tail
quantifiers, regime labels, normalized moments, physical interpretations, and
every headline edge. Lexical check-call sites, runtime executions, and
assertion nodes remain separate inventories.

SymPy exact algebra and discrete inequalities are the primary oracle. The
shape route derives `w_n^2/(w_(n-1)w_(n+1))` and the complete integer and
noninteger mode branches. The tail route proves ratio thresholds by exact
order and induction, then establishes fixed-power decay without sampled
asymptotics. The distribution route derives the probability generating
function from the exponential series and obtains factorial moments by exact
differentiation. Direct finite sums are regression only, not independent
evidence for infinite identities.

Mutations change the factorial power, S exponent, support, normalization,
ratio denominator, strict inequality, integer tie, tail threshold, PGF
argument, mean or variance derivative, and physical interaction or spectral
premises. Counterexamples include integer S with two adjacent modes, positive
and positive-odd support changes, alternate supplied S coordinates, zero
coupling, and zero final-state spectral density. An independent review will
derive coefficients and generating functions without importing the candidate
API. Dependency and reverse-consumer replay will hash-lock each source node
and classify compatibility before execution.
