# P164 SM2 Generation and Hypercharge Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P164 must reproduce and independently adjudicate SM2's claim that one physical
Standard Model generation and its hypercharges are derived. The positive
deliverable is either a distinct reusable exact multiplet-spectrum theorem or
a complete accepted composition that types every surviving charge and
convention statement and terminally qualifies SM2. Merely observing that the
table was imported does not finish the campaign.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-REP-001 owns exact finite supplied-state charge arithmetic, trace
decomposition, and Abelian normalization covariance, while explicitly denying
physical representation or anomaly derivation. C-REP-002 owns Pauli-half
doublet weights and the scalar commuting Abelian generator, but no physical
matter content or charge normalization. C-GSM-001 owns the conditional lower-
doublet unbroken generator in one declared normalization, but no derived
vacuum, physical electroweak sector, or matter table. C-PGA-001 owns only the
local product Lie algebra and no hypercharge table or physical carrier.

Allowed inputs are those four claims and exact finite-dimensional weight
spectra, positive integer spectator multiplicities, Abelian coordinate
rescaling, and charge-conjugation algebra. Pending SM3 cannot supply anomaly
selection. Observed charges, field labels, generation completeness, chirality,
and Standard Model identity remain external unless independently declared and
typed.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted dependency closure, explicit supplied-versus-
derived provenance, typed multiplet structure beyond statewise arithmetic,
Abelian convention covariance, conjugation consistency, assumption economy,
mutation sensitivity, API novelty, and downstream compatibility. Familiar
charge values, labels, or a green tally cannot select the concept. Prior value
exposure is recorded and no fresh blinding is claimed.

## Proposed Claim Delta

C-REP-003 is now proposed with dependencies C-REP-001 and C-REP-002. It will
type supplied product multiplets, expand their isospin weights with spectator
multiplicity, derive grouped and flattened charge spectra, compose the accepted

specialization a derived or complete physical generation.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

The primary oracle will hash, parse, reproduce, and map every SM2 predicate and
its assertion. SymPy exact rational weights, matrices, spectra, linear systems,
and rescalings fit the obligations. A fresh independent route will rebuild the
charge spectra and convention map without importing any new P164 helper. It
will distinguish physical right-handed fields from left-handed conjugate rows,
test missing and alternative tables, and prevent pending anomaly equations from
entering as premises.

Replay inventories will record lexical check sites, runtime executions, and
assertions separately. Compatibility preflight will inspect direct, imported,
dynamic, and eager legacy integration access. Mutable code must use exact
algebra, `trapezoid_integral`, or `np.trapezoid`; immutable version-only access
would receive an isolated alias backed by `np.trapezoid` without scientific
demotion. Structural selection and exact equations remain independent of the
already exposed charge values.
