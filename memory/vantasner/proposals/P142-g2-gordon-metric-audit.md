# P142 G2 Gordon-Metric Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P142 must reproduce and adjudicate G2's claim that a moving-medium Gordon
metric supplies a nontrivial three-plus-one dynamical sourcing route around a
fixed optical-metric no-go. The positive deliverable is an importable exact
Gordon-metric theorem with covariant and inverse forms, determinant, signature,
null-cone and limit data, plus independently derived curvature and Bianchi
evidence wherever tractable. If Einstein-source matching does not close, P142
must still construct the strongest distinct metric object and source-
compatibility countermodel rather than treating a no-go as success.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

P142 keeps analog-metric kinematics, differential geometry, Einstein-source
compatibility, matter dynamics, and physical gravity separate. A nonzero
Einstein tensor does not itself construct a stress tensor or solution. The
contracted Bianchi identity requires a matched source to be covariantly
conserved in the same metric and convention. Pending units cannot supply a
coupling, action, field dictionary, or boundary condition.

P142 may use exact pseudo-Riemannian tensor calculus, a separately declared
Gordon metric with normalized medium four-velocity, and primary Gordon/analog-
gravity literature after freeze for convention and scope only. C-OG-001,
C-CC-001, and C-OG-004 are available only under their one-dimensional static
ceilings. No physical medium, gravitational constant, Einstein equation,
breather stress, solved geometry, dimensional lift, or substrate map may be
imported without a separately accepted premise.

conditions, null cones, Christoffels, curvature, Einstein components, Bianchi
contraction, profile limits, and exact component-ratio counterexamples. A fresh
independent route will rebuild the metric and tensors without importing any new
canonical module. Any accepted reusable metric logic moves under
`src/substrate_framework/`; source orchestration and physical-ceiling evidence
remain in P142.

Mutations change covariant versus contravariant Gordon convention, metric
signature, four-velocity normalization, index reciprocal, boost sign, constant
index, rest velocity, profile derivative, and stress component ratios. The
compatibility preflight checks direct, imported, dynamic, and eager legacy
NumPy trapezoid access. Mutable code uses exact algebra, `np.trapezoid`, or the
canonical helper; immutable legacy access receives alias-only replay backed by
`np.trapezoid` before scientific adjudication and never counts as failure.
