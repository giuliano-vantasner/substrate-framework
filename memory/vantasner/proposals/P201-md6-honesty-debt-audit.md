# P201 MD6 Honesty and Debt Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P201 must produce an exact predicate-complete ledger for MD6's finite AST,
lexical, tag, import, and debt checks, independently recompute every semantic
debt verdict from accepted authority, and give MD6 a terminal source
disposition. Scanner weakness alone is not completion; the positive object is
the verified finite ledger plus a dependency-closed debt adjudication.

## Base Release and Provenance

The accepted base is v0.149.0 at framework commit `f0eb421`. MD6 is pinned at
`08b6d263323e3a09da39152c7409795d97477521f2fcf8d57b295922fefa1cbf`
in source commit `6d1f4e0`. Its 40-check tally and scanner categories were
exposed by earlier consumer graphs and the generated queue, so P201 preserves
that exposure and claims no fresh output blinding. Recall found P120 and P195
as the nearest verified scanner campaigns; their exact methods are reusable
only after source verification.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

A finite source scan is typed by its bytes, paths, decoding, syntax, matchers,
tags, and scope. Missing selected syntax does not prove closed data flow,
parameter independence, non-load-bearing constants, empirical independence,
or scientific debt discharge. MD1 through MD5 and WN6 remain governed by their
individual accepted mappings and ceilings. Mutable sampled integration uses
`numpy.trapezoid` or the shared helper; compatibility events are not scientific
failures.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection prioritizes pinned predicate fidelity, accepted-authority debt
closure, data-flow coverage, mutation sensitivity and false-positive control,
nonduplication, dependency economy, physical ceiling preservation, and
terminal governance fit. No numerical agreement selects a concept. Candidates,
semantic falsifiers, and authority rules are frozen before renewed execution
or complete source-body inspection.

## Proposed Claim Delta

No claim identifier is proposed. A scientific claim cannot be created from a
green scanner tally, and a reusable audit claim or API must be distinct from
P120, P195, and `source_audit.py` with an actual consumer before any identifier
is reserved.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

The primary route will inventory AST check sites, runtime predicates,
assertions, paths, hashes, assignments, tags, literals, imports, self-tests,
and debt labels. A fresh independent route will reimplement the relevant
classification without importing campaign helpers. Construction, aliases,
attributes, subscripts, configuration, imported values, tables, closures,
dynamic imports, tag/data-flow, and equivalent empirical inputs will mutate
headline assumptions. The exact seven-node dependency graph and compatibility
surfaces will replay without duplicate native execution. Existing importable
audit APIs will be reused unless a distinct consumer-backed surface survives.
