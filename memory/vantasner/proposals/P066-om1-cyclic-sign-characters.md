# P066 OM1 Cyclic Sign-Character Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P066 must deliver an importable exact classification of sign characters on
finite cyclic groups and binary product groups, with explicit existence,
kernel, quotient, faithfulness, and character-count semantics. It must then
decide whether OM1 constructs a common domain and representation across its
named sectors or only compares equal scalar outputs. Rejecting OM1's collapse
is attempt evidence; completion requires the reusable classification object.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-TOP-001 proves that integer parity `(-1)^w` is a character and explicitly
withholds every physical interpretation. P066 may use finite additive cyclic
groups, binary vector spaces, exact modular arithmetic, kernels, quotient maps,
and multiplicative signs. Source domains, quotient maps, generator choices,
orientations, connections, representations, operators, and evaluation
elements remain separately supplied typed inputs. Matching scalar values is
not function equality, domain isomorphism, representation equivalence, or
physical identity.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted closure; explicit domain, map, generator,
representation, and provenance; exact kernel and quotient semantics; odd/even
and product-group mutation behavior; separation of value from object equality;
parameter economy; and independent finite enumeration. The printed `-1`,
number of named oracles, and OM1's asserted C2 uniqueness cannot select a
candidate.

## Proposed Claim Delta

Provisional C-CHR-001 may classify sign characters of C_n and C2^r and state
the exact quotient/faithfulness ceiling. It may not identify any OM1 source
space, loop, connection, representation, operator, topology, spin-statistics
law, particle, or substrate mechanism. If this is already fully contained in
C-TOP-001, OM1 must be duplicate evidence rather than forcing a new claim.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

An additive extension of `topological_labels.py` may expose pure exact cyclic
and product-character ledgers while preserving existing APIs. Exact finite
enumeration and symbolic modular identities are the strongest oracles. The
independent route will enumerate homomorphisms without importing the new API.
Mutations will use odd order, order four, nonfaithful even order, a trivial
image, independent product selectors, changed generators, missing quotient
maps, and equal scalar evaluations on distinct labeled domains. No numerical
solver or quadrature is required.
