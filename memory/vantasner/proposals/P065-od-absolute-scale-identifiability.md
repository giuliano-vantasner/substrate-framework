# P065 OD Absolute-Scale Identifiability Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P065 must deliver importable diagnostics for positive monomial observations
that constrain logarithmic scale coordinates. The positive object includes
the exact design system and affine freedom, scale-free left-nullspace tests,
incremental information rank, covariance-weighted residuals for a declared
error model, and exact one-coordinate interval feasibility. It must then decide
whether OD supplies independent accepted equations that fix an absolute scale,
or only a diagnostic populated by declared, fitted, shared, or pending inputs.
A failed OD headline is attempt evidence and cannot close the campaign without
the reusable diagnostic.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-DIM-002 fixes unique monomial exponents only relative to declared primitives
`c0,S,a`; it fixes neither their values nor dimensionless coefficients.
C-DIM-003 is a bijective coordinate change, not a scale prediction.
C-LIN-001 already proves exact rank, augmented consistency, uniqueness,
nullity, and duplicate-row semantics and must be composed rather than copied.
Conditional running and sole-scale claims do not derive a nonperturbative
coefficient or observed scale. Pending dependency results are forbidden.

Allowed mathematics is exact finite linear algebra, logarithms of explicitly
positive dimensionless ratios, row and left-null spaces, rational
positive-definite covariance matrices, and closed real intervals. Observable
values, coefficients, exponent rows, covariance, uncertainty intervals, and
provenance labels are supplied inputs. A comparator may test a frozen model but
cannot become a derivation input. Logs must use ratios to declared reference
units so unit changes shift coordinates and offsets covariantly rather than
creating physical information.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted closure, exact logical semantics, provenance
and shared-input visibility, correct mutation behavior, parameter economy,
dimensionless logarithms, unit covariance, and independent rederivation.
Equation count, printed rank, a familiar absolute observable, or agreement with
one value cannot select a candidate. The unknown set, exponent matrix,
independence criteria, covariance model, interval semantics, and thresholds are
frozen before opening the source.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

An additive pure module under `src/substrate_framework/` will call
`diagnose_linear_system` and expose log-design, compatibility, information,
GLS, and interval objects without fitting data or printing at import. Exact
SymPy row reduction, nullspaces, normal equations, projectors, and interval
comparisons are the strongest oracles for rational examples. The independent
route will rebuild the key identities without importing the new module.
Mutations will duplicate or rescale rows, alter offsets, generate rows from
others, add a genuinely new column direction, zero an exponent, correlate
errors, singularize covariance, separate intervals, and shift reference units.
No numerical optimizer or quadrature is required.
