## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

The accepted affine convention is `a_i=A+B*b_i`. A three-line rank-two family
has unification plane spanned by the all-ones and coefficient vectors and a
one-dimensional annihilator. That makes a nonzero signed linear diagnostic
unique only up to scale and sign. Pairwise crossing differences require
nonzero coefficient differences, while a range is absolute and piecewise.

WM3 is accepted only as a conditional inverse reconstruction from supplied
inputs. Percentage and scale-spread reports retain denominator, logarithm,
reference, Abelian, and pair-selection conventions. A shared zero locus is not
numeric equality or physical identity. Pending B1, M1, SM4, and WM6 grant no
authority; WM2 grants no common-induction mechanism.

No integration is expected. Canonical sampled work would use
`trapezoid_integral`, mutable scripts `np.trapezoid`, and an immutable alias-only
replay for legacy compatibility rather than a scientific failure.
