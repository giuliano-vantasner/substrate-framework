# P090 SA4 Threshold and Saturation Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P090 must determine whether SA4 derives a dependency-closed physical law that
is zero below electrical breakdown, rises with voltage slew, and saturates at
an integer breather population, or only combines a supplied spectral scalar,
free gain, accepted breather-energy coordinate, floor operation, and inserted
response family. The positive deliverable is an exact threshold, rounding,
energy-remainder, normalization, monotonicity, discontinuity, saturation,
breakdown, physical-mechanism, dependency, consumer, and nonduplication
classification, individual promotion of any distinct surviving object, and a
terminal SA4 disposition.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-SG-002 supplies the exact normalized energy `E_b=16*sqrt(1-omega^2)` of an
already-existing exact breather; it is not a minimum formation work theorem or
a rule that partitions arbitrary deposited energy into breathers. C-SG-005 is
only the exact complement to the normalized two-kink threshold. C-COH-001's
continuous activation coordinate explicitly requires a separately justified
integer convention and supplies no population, deposition, or event channel.
C-SG-015 supplies an undriven field-trace spectrum, not susceptibility or
absorbed energy. C-PDE-011 supplies one knife-edge mathematical forcing branch,
not a voltage, plasma, threshold, probability, or count map.

SA1, SA2, and SA3 are qualified. Their rejected susceptibility, spectral-
overlap population, voltage-slew trigger, breakdown, broad formation, and
engine interpretations are unavailable dependencies. A supplied positive
spectral scalar becomes energy only through a dimensionally specified coupling
and transform convention. Scaling a waveform or Fourier convention rescales
its squared coefficient, so a free gain can realize any positive threshold
unless independently fixed.

For positive supplied energy `E` and unit cost `E_b`, Euclidean division gives
`E=E_b*floor(E/E_b)+r` with `0<=r<E_b`. This is exact bookkeeping, not a
formation, stability, exclusivity, or maximum-population theorem. Electrical
breakdown is a separate material and circuit event; an energy threshold cannot
be renamed breakdown without an accepted map. A saturating curve requires its
family, normalization, scale, asymptote, and controlled input to be explicit.
Sampled integration, if any survives, must use `trapezoid_integral` rather than
a version-specific NumPy alias; the expected primary audit is exact and needs
no numerical quadrature.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted dependency closure for drive, deposition,
energy, formation, count, electrical breakdown, and consumer; exact dimensions,
signs, domains, transform normalization, floor/remainder semantics, threshold
convention, monotonicity, discontinuities, and limits; mutation sensitivity;
parameter economy; compatibility with C-SG-002, C-COH-001, and C-PDE-011; and
nonduplication. Numerical closeness, source labels, and a thirteen-check tally
cannot select a physical interpretation.

Comparator blinding is partial because P089 exposed the source's upper
construction and example gains. Structural criteria are frozen before opening
the remainder, exact predicate bodies, printed values, or any consumer not
already summarized by P088/P089. Previously exposed values are treated only as
source evidence and never as derivation inputs or pass thresholds.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy and exact integer algebra are the primary oracles for energy division,
floor and ceiling intervals, discontinuities, gain/amplitude/transform scaling,
arbitrary-target inverses, monotonic derivatives, asymptotic limits, units, and
counterfamilies. Direct exact arithmetic will probe values immediately below,
at, and above every integer threshold. AST and hashed-source audits will
classify every literal predicate, unused variable, hard-coded expected result,
dependency, and consumer. Numerical sampling can provide regression coverage
for a genuinely non-elementary source integral, but cannot upgrade exact or
physical claims and will route through the shared compatibility helper.

Load-bearing mutations include gain, waveform amplitude, Fourier convention,
frequency, breather energy, floor versus ceiling, integer boundary, saturation
scale, asymptote, source normalization, breakdown parameter, and consumer input.
Counterexamples include equal scalar slew with different spectra, rescaled
spectra compensated by gain, energy above one unit without a constructed
breather, and a monotone unbounded response with the same low-input slope. The
independent route will rederive the partition and scaling orbits without
calling the primary package helpers. Reusable APIs are added only if Candidate
J survives; one integrated promotion boundary will run `scripts/validate.sh`,
while later record-only changes receive only record-sensitive checks.

## Attempts and Continuation

triggers comparison with the next candidate rather than ending the parent

threshold, energy, and coherence constructions are inspected for transferable
logic and mismatched assumptions.

consumer maps, and nonduplication. A terminal SA4 disposition will name a
structured reason and durable evidence, then regenerate the source queue. Any
accepted claim receives an individual review, collision-free identifier,
package implementation and tests, pinned release, generated documentation, and
accepted-memory synchronization; otherwise `v0.77.0` remains unchanged.

breakdown/mechanism classification exists, every source predicate has an
