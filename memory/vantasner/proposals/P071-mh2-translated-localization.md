# P071 MH2 Translated Localization Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P071 must deliver an importable translated-localization overlap theorem, an
exact or controlled large-separation attenuation law, and a complete parameter
and identifiability ledger. An honest same-center no-go, a short straight log
plot, or a fitted separation does not complete the campaign.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-OVL-001 supplies same-center Cartesian whole-line expectations, and
C-QBL-003 supplies two modes of one translated whole-line Hessian. A genuine
radial problem requires a half-line, radial measure, origin regularity,
self-adjoint operator, and boundary data. Independently centered wells are
separate supplied operators unless one accepted construction couples or
selects them. P071 may use exact translation algebra, convolution identities,
beta/gamma integrals, dominated convergence, explicit tail estimates, and
declared numerical discretizations. Amplitudes, widths, centers, spacing,
well parameters, external scale, and dimensions remain visible. MH3 and O1
supply no premise.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted closure; one explicit operator; exact domain,
measure, boundary, normalization, width and center typing; correct zero- and
large-separation limits; separation of exact, asymptotic, numeric and physical
claims; mutation sensitivity; and parameter economy. The synopsis's observed
ratio and two log spacings cannot select the ladder spacing, well family, or
physical interpretation.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

A pure package module may expose normalized translated-profile overlaps,
special exact formulas, asymptotic-tail ledgers, ladder-ratio maps, translation
intertwining, and free-parameter directions. SymPy exact algebra fits special
convolutions and translation identities. For any genuinely non-elementary
overlap, SciPy adaptive quadrature plus a separately discretized route will
record status, domain, absolute/relative tolerance, integration error,
domain growth, and parameter refinement; sampled regressions call the shared
current-first `trapezoid_integral`, never `np.trapz`. A finite-difference
eigenproblem must declare operator, interval, boundary data, mesh, eigenvalue
residual, normalization measure, and wall/mesh refinement, then cross-check an
exact translated mode or an independent collocation/shooting route. Mutations
will omit normalization or radial weight, move the origin, hide spacing,
change the tail family, copy a log slope, ignore solver status, fit a
comparator, and label unitarily equivalent copies as eigenlevels.
