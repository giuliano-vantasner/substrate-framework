# P173 KI3 Bracket Sharpness Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P173 must determine whether KI3 establishes an exact attainable kappa bracket
under accepted authority. The positive deliverable must distinguish range
inclusion from equality, open limits from attained endpoints, one illustrative
interpolant from every admissible function, and mathematical coordinates from
physical binding or yield interpretations.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-BPS-003 is a local controlled expansion at epsilon tending to zero. It does
not prove that the proposed deformation has such an expansion, construct a
global interpolation, fix a coefficient sign, or supply a large-epsilon limit.
C-BPS-002 supplies zero signed difference only under actual attainment in both
compared sectors.

C-RDIFF-002 supplies the accepted conditional coordinate near 8.4824 with
resolution-bounded numeric status. It is not a variational bound, physical
binding coefficient, or BPS interpolation endpoint. P172 qualifies KI2 only as
freedom of a locally defined ratio across distinct positive-parameter theories.

For a real continuous function, two ordered endpoint limits can force every
intermediate value to occur. They do not exclude overshoot or undershoot, make
preimages unique, prove monotonicity, or establish a separately declared
codomain. C-XOV-001 already owns the exact full-range and unique-crossing result
when continuity, strict increase, attained lower endpoint, and unattained upper
limit are all independently supplied.

No comparator may enter concept selection, pass thresholds, endpoint values,
or interpolant ranking. Mutable integration uses `np.trapezoid` or the canonical
helper; immutable legacy-name stops are version-only evidence.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

The primary route will inventory KI3's imports, five predicates, assertion,
endpoint definitions, interpolant family, continuity and codomain assumptions,
limit calculations, inverse uses, comparator isolation, and compatibility.
SymPy is the strongest oracle for exact limits, roots, derivatives, and explicit
counterfunctions.

The universal probe will construct continuous functions with the same two
limits that overshoot, undershoot, or repeat values. The illustrative-function
probe will determine exact ranges and inverses one function at a time. The
accepted-composition probe will replace source endpoint prose with exact
C-BPS-002/003, C-RDIFF-002, C-XOV-001, and P172 premises.

A fresh independent route will prove only the strongest quantifier-accurate
range statement without importing KI3 or the primary verifier. The exact Lean
theorem will be inspected and executed separately. Hash-identical accepted
evidence will be reused, with focused source-sensitive consumers only.
