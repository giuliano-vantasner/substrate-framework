# P118 CM4 Discriminating Derivative Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

This campaign must produce an exact independently checked response and
derivative ledger for the displayed positive-factor family, distinguish
continuous derivatives from integer-count forward differences, and terminally
adjudicate CM4. It must decide whether any distinct theorem remains after
C-RG-002, C-SPN-002, and C-CMP-001; documented duplication or rejected physical
prose alone does not replace the required exact ledger and source disposition.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

The campaign preserves exact positive real domains, a dimensionless exponent,
and explicit held-fixed conventions. A continuous N derivative and an integer
count comparison are distinct; the latter uses an exact forward difference.
The variable called A-squared is not assigned a physical dimension or loading
map until the source and C-RG-002 conventions are reconciled explicitly.

C-RG-002 may supply only its conditional inverse-quadratic capillary barrier
and declared constitutive ceiling. C-SPN-002 may supply only its normalized
finite-vector coefficients and non-rate ceiling. C-CMP-001 may supply only its
conditional product, dimension, and non-rate ceiling. Elementary exact real
calculus, finite differences, limits, inequalities, dimensional analysis, and
SymPy are allowed. CM4 becomes hash-pinned noncanonical evidence only after the
freeze gate.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Candidates are ranked first by accepted dependency closure and correct typing of
continuous versus discrete variables, then by mathematical novelty, dimensions,
held-fixed conventions, limits, sensitivity, parameter economy, identifiability,
reusable API value, semantic honesty, and complete consumer coverage. Numerical
agreement and the reported ten-check tally cannot select a concept.

The source formula and headline were exposed before P118, so blinding is
partial and recorded. The stronger novelty, discrete-difference, dimension,
alternative-monotone, zero-rate, arbitrary-prefactor, noisy-report, mutation,
dependency, predicate, and consumer gates are frozen before renewed execution,
whole-body inspection, or additional numeric output review.

## Proposed Claim Delta

No claim identifier is proposed at freeze. Candidate C predicts that the exact
source algebra is already contained in accepted claims. A new identifier will
be reserved only if Candidate D establishes a distinct positive theorem whose
assumptions and consumers are not an artificial generalization of CM4.

The mandatory source delta is a terminal CM4 disposition with each predicate,
dependency, cycle, and consumer reviewed. C-RG-002, C-SPN-002, and C-CMP-001 are
reviewed individually and never blanket-promoted through this campaign.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy exact calculus and finite differences fit the displayed elementary
response. The primary route will derive the continuous derivatives, integer
forward differences, log elasticities, limits, mixed sensitivities, dimensions,
scale transformations, and parameter-fitting families rather than compare
copied literals. Load-bearing mutations change exponent sign, N power, loading
power, prefactor, and gate inequalities. Exact flat, decreasing, nonmonotone,
alternative-monotone, zero-interaction, arbitrary-prefactor, sparse-report,
and noisy-report countermodels test specificity and physical semantics.

An independent verifier will not import any new P118 API. If the ledger exactly
duplicates accepted canonical APIs and campaign results, orchestration remains
inside P118 rather than creating a redundant module. If a novel reusable API is
selected, it must live under `src/substrate_framework/` with focused tests.
The source's numeric work is regression coverage only when an exact identity
already fixes the result.

Before source execution, compatibility preflight searches for `np.trapz`,
`np.trapezoid`, `trapezoid_integral`, and SciPy integration. Canonical sampled
integration would use `trapezoid_integral`; mutable scripts use
`np.trapezoid`; immutable source receives an alias-only replay if and only if
removed `np.trapz` is its sole abort. A version-only compatibility event is not
a scientific failure. P118 presently expects no quadrature obligation.

The primary and independent verifiers, focused existing tests, hash-pinned
direct and transitive consumers, governance validator, generated docs and
memory checks, one final `scripts/validate.sh`, and `git diff --check` form the
verification boundary. The full workflow is run once at a terminal promotion
or record-only adjudication boundary, not after every attempt.

## Attempts and Continuation

Every failed route is appended under P118 with its command, diagnosis,
scientific effect, and next attempt. Implementation and oracle defects are
repaired without weakening the theorem; a duplicate or ill-fitting candidate
is rejected in favor of the next preregistered route.

## Debt Ledger

The ledger tracks any new assumption, parameter, unit convention, continuous-
extension premise, report threshold, derivative estimator, uncertainty model,
residual, dependency gap, broken consumer, or narrative conflict. The ledger is
empty at freeze and must be empty for terminal adjudication.

## Review and Promotion Plan

Claim-level review will compare the exact ledger with each accepted dependency
before any registry edit. The source audit will classify all ten predicates and
the physical signature prose separately. Direct and indirect candidate
consumers will replay from pinned hashes, while cycles remain provenance only.

If a distinct claim survives, promotion requires a canonical module and tests,
immutable P118, a new release, generated docs and accepted memory, CM4's
qualified disposition, regenerated source queue, and complete validation. If
the result is fully governed already, P118 closes as an immutable no-release
campaign with CM4 qualified through exact accepted claims and the same
disposition, queue, documentation, memory, and validation honesty. A final gate
attempt begins in progress and is finalized only after clean exit; later record
edits receive targeted checks only.

## Done Gate

P118 closes only when the exact positive response ledger exists, novelty is
decided against accepted claims, every source predicate and consumer is
audited, the physical and report-gate ceilings are explicit, canonical state
agrees, validation is mutation-sensitive, and the debt ledger is empty. A
passing source tally, rejected signature, or documented duplication without
the positive ledger is not completion.
