## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-BRN-001 exactly governs two nonnegative common-dimension quantities and the
weighted specialization with positive baselines, positive dimensionless
weight, and positive integer population. It already contains GB4's advertised
fractions, the fixed-weight positive-real derivative, relative odds, endpoint
and common-scale laws, unequal-gate residual, and arbitrary-target family.

For fixed positive rho and weight, the comparison fraction decreases on the
positive-real extension and at every adjacent positive integer. The integer
forward difference is derived directly; calculus is not used as a substitute
for the discrete occupation statement.

If the weight depends on a subdivision count that itself depends on population,
the total derivative is controlled by `w+N*w'`, and the adjacent-integer sign
is controlled by the change in `N*w`. Weight positivity alone is insufficient.
At fixed n, all three named regimes are merely positive constants with respect
to N. Under the distinct coupling n=N, the exponential regime becomes
nonmonotone after `alpha*N=1`, while linear and positive-power regimes remain
increasing in `N*w`.

The source's enhancement expression can mean the weighted-to-comparison odds
relative to a population-one baseline only when both evaluations use the same
positive channel baselines. Its rho cancellation is then exact but does not
determine a physical gamma suppression. Rho, the weight scale, alpha, k, n, and
any n(N) law remain free.

GB1 is usable only through C-BRN-001. GB3, PN2, and PN3 provide no accepted
physical soft/gamma rates, weight law, nuclear transition, or exhaustive
channel set. A physical branching fraction needs a common initial state,
exhaustive channels, interactions, final-state measures or spectral densities,
kinetics, normalization, parameter provenance, and a measurement map.

No numerical integration is expected. Canonical sampled work, if unexpectedly
required, uses `trapezoid_integral`; mutable current scripts use
`np.trapezoid`. An immutable `np.trapz`-only abort would receive an alias-only
recorded replay and would not count as a scientific candidate failure.
