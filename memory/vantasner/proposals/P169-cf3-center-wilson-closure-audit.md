through C-LIE-002 and C-WIL-001 while complete center algebra, abstract

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

The fundamental representation convention is exactly C-LIE-001. A complete
center theorem must solve the full complex commutant and then impose unitarity
and determinant one; merely exhibiting three commuting matrices is
insufficient. Center phases and trialities are abstract representation algebra
unless an accepted physical sector map exists.

For positive `R,T,sigma,rho`, the area and perimeter expectations are separate
declared premises. C-WIL-001 may apply the declared extraction
`-lim(log W)/T`; it may not derive or select either expectation. The same
center algebra must remain compatible with both laws. C-FLX-001 is a separate
ideal fixed-area theorem and supplies no identity for CF3's declared `sigma`.
No physical quark, gluon, screening, QCD, confinement, or substrate premise is
allowed.

Compatibility is scientifically neutral. Mutable P169 code uses exact or
current APIs. After freeze, executable syntax is audited for direct, imported,
dynamic, and eager legacy NumPy access; an immutable version-only abort is
preserved and replayed behind an isolated alias backed by `np.trapezoid`, never
counted as a scientific candidate failure.

## Review and Promotion Plan

Every distinct CF3 predicate receives an individual verdict. Accepted
composition requires native or compatibility-neutral reproduction, exact
scope mapping, mutations, fresh independent derivation, source/dependency/
nonduplication audits, consumers, graph, and materialized adjudication. A new
claim additionally requires an importable API, tests, impact analysis,
release/registry update, generated documentation, and accepted-memory closure.
Otherwise P169 updates only CF3's qualification evidence and leaves v0.127.0
unchanged.

Validation and commit are separate processes. Focused scientific gates run
during development, followed by one terminal repository validation, one
separately required full test command, and `git diff --check`. Later
record-only edits trigger record-sensitive validation rather than another full
suite.
