## Proposed Claim Delta

Revision 0001 reserves C-SCT-001 after source-aware nonduplication. For a
massless scalar on the right half-line, exact incoming and reflected harmonics
and the passive condition `phi_t-zeta*phi_x=0` give normalized impedance
`z=zeta/c`, amplitude reflection `(z-1)/(z+1)`, reflected power `R`, and absorbed
fraction `1-R`. The bulk energy rate is nonpositive. Reciprocal impedances flip
the amplitude phase but leave power ratios unchanged. A separately assigned
perfectly reflected reference channel yields contrast `(1-R)/(1+R)` through
C-BRN-001; this is a deterministic transform, not an independent prediction.
The claim introduces no piston derivation, physical chirality, parity
violation, detector, current, charge, weak interaction, or substrate mechanism.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy exact plane-wave substitution, component flux algebra, and rational
function analysis are primary. The fresh route will solve boundary amplitudes
and normalized contrasts without a new canonical helper. No ODE, PDE, sampled
quadrature, FFT, or empirical fit is justified unless the source unexpectedly
contains evolved data needed by a supportable claim.

Mutations change wave direction, boundary normal, residual sign, impedance,
amplitude squaring, channel completeness, handedness label, detector
acceptance, or current coupling. Countermodels keep the same scalar reflection
ledger while changing physical carrier, detector map, charge, parity action,
or interaction to show which readings are not derived.

Mutable code must use exact algebra, `trapezoid_integral`, or `np.trapezoid`
and contain no executable legacy access. Accepted P149 graph evidence says W5
has no legacy reference. Any contrary immutable version-only abort would be
isolated through the current compatibility workflow and would not reject a
scientific candidate.
