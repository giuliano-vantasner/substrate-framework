## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

The exact scalar reference waveform is
`delta_mu(t)=mu_static+A*cos(Omega*t)+D*sin(Omega*t)`. Its third derivative has
no static term and its exact squared cycle average is
`Omega^6*(A^2+D^2)/2`. For one cosine of amplitude `B` at `Omega=2*omega_b`,
the result is `(2*omega_b)^6*B^2/2`; any different scalar, tensor, RMS,
peak-amplitude, one-sided-spectrum, or proportionality convention must be
stated and transformed explicitly.

A squared third derivative has dimensions `[delta_mu]^2/time^6`. A power law
requires a separately derived source type, coupling, unitful prefactor,
retarded or radiation-zone solution, angular pattern, tensor contraction,
normalization, and validity regime. Electric dipoles, magnetic dipoles,
quadrupoles, antennas, near fields, and detector readouts are competing models,
not labels that may be selected by the desired derivative order.

C-GW-001 may supply only exact conditional STF third-derivative calculus and
its explicit non-radiation ceiling; FS4 is duplicate evidence only for static
offset cancellation. C-COH-001 may supply only fixed-normalization directional
phasor intensity. C-SG-017 may supply only the exact dimensional breather
frequency `omega_b=omega*omega_0` under C-MED-003. C-CMP-001 and C-RES-001 may
supply only their exact conditional algebra and non-rate ceilings. P087/P088
may establish only that no accepted physical dV/dt seeding map was supplied.

Emitted total power, angular intensity, local focal intensity, collection
efficiency, transmission, and detector response remain distinct. DC, ramp,
sinusoidal, finite-window, nonlinear-response, time-varying-medium, and
zero-coupling inputs are separate cases. Elementary exact calculus, Fourier
endpoint identities, complex phasor sums, angular integration, dimensions,
limits, and SymPy are allowed. CM5 becomes hash-pinned noncanonical evidence
only after this freeze validates.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy exact differentiation and exact cycle integration fit the harmonic
obligation. The primary route will derive general cosine-sine scalar and STF
tensor formulas, static and zero-frequency limits, phase invariance, peak
versus RMS conventions, dimensions, and scale laws. Load-bearing mutations
change derivative order, frequency multiplier, amplitude convention, omitted
one-half, tensor trace, radiation prefactor, phase alignment, per-source
normalization, integration over angle, voltage waveform, endpoint closure,
bandwidth, heat coupling, and interaction strength.

Finite complex sums and exact angular examples fit the array obligation. The
route will compare aligned and disordered phases, fixed per-source and fixed
total normalization, directional intensity and angular total, finite aperture
and focal observation, and detector efficiency. Zero electromagnetic coupling,
arbitrary unitful prefactors, alternative electric-dipole and quadrupole laws,
equal-frequency independent heat channels, and DC/ramp/sinusoidal source models
test nonidentifiability and semantic specificity.

An independent verifier will rederive the formulas without importing any new
P119 API. If the exact positive content duplicates accepted canonical APIs,
orchestration remains inside P119 rather than creating a redundant module. A
new package API and focused tests are mandatory only if a distinct reusable
claim survives. Numerical samples may regress exact identities but are not an
independent oracle.

Compatibility preflight will search CM5 and its executed consumers for
`np.trapz`, `np.trapezoid`, `trapezoid_integral`, and SciPy integration.
Canonical sampled integration uses `trapezoid_integral`; mutable
current-environment scripts use `np.trapezoid`; an immutable source that aborts
only on removed `np.trapz` receives an alias-only recorded replay. Such an
abort is a version event, not a scientific failure. The exact P119 ledgers
require no numerical quadrature.

The primary and independent verifiers, focused accepted tests, hash-pinned
direct and transitive consumers, governance validator, generated queue and
memory checks, one final `scripts/validate.sh`, and `git diff --check` form the
verification boundary. The full workflow runs once at the terminal promotion
or record-only adjudication boundary, not after every preserved attempt.
