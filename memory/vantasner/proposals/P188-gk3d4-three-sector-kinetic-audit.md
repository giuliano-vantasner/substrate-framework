# P188 GK3D4 Three-Sector Kinetic Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P188 must determine whether GK3D4 derives one common four-dimensional induced
kinetic construction for U(1), SU(2), and SU(3), including a physical 3/8 weak-
angle relation. The positive deliverable is an exact provenance ledger that
either derives the product-sector kinetic matrix and every relation among its
entries from accepted dependencies, or proves that the complete surviving
object is an already accepted factorwise composition and terminally
adjudicates GK3D4 without creating a duplicate claim. Merely finding that the
physical headline is unsupported does not close the campaign.
