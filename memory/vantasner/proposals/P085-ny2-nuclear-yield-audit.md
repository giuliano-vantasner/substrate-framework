yield or engine replacement beyond C-SK-001's conditional energy ratio, or

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-SK-001 derives only a conditional ratio from two supplied mass premises and
explicitly excludes physical numerical prediction. P084 proves that evaluating
that ratio retains the empirical electron energy and supplies no correction,
multi-soliton binding, nuclear state, reaction yield, or engine implementation.
Dimensional equality between two energies permits `Q=kappa*U`; it does not
select the positive dimensionless coordinate `kappa`.

A binding-energy difference requires a declared mass functional, solved
configurations, state assignments, and corrections. A reaction requires a
separate physical channel map and convention. An engine replacement requires
an actual consumer dependency and replay. Exact P085 work uses no numerical
quadrature, `np.trapz`, or `np.trapezoid`; future sampled integration must use
the shared `trapezoid_integral` compatibility helper.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted closure of the scale, mass functional,
solutions, states, subtraction, reaction, units, and consumer; exact
coefficient, sign, multiplicity, inverse, limits, and mutation sensitivity;
separation of unit, coefficient, binding, measurement, engine literal, and
runtime behavior; parameter economy; and nonduplication. Numerical closeness
cannot select `kappa=1`. All reaction, engine, band, and coefficient literals
stay blinded until the contract hashes and immutable freeze validate.

## Proposed Claim Delta

No new claim is expected. C-DIM-002 already states that dimensions leave
dimensionless multiplicative coefficients unconstrained, C-DIM-003 preserves
free physical coordinates, and C-SK-001/P084 bound the energy ratio. A new
claim proceeds only if Candidate H identifies a distinct theorem, importable
API, and accepted consumer. No accepted claim is challenged or superseded.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy is the correct oracle for the exact coefficient map, inverse, sign,
multiplicity, arbitrary-target family, and limits. The primary route will pin
the source and contracts, reproduce its tally, audit every equation and
literal, trace accepted dependencies and the declared consumer, mutate
coefficient and state-map inputs, and open comparators only after the
structural verdict freezes. An independent route will rederive the generic
energy-coordinate and binding subtraction without importing P085 expressions.

Reusable code will be added only if nonduplication succeeds. A PDE or BVP
solver is required only if NY2 actually supplies a mass functional and
multi-soliton boundary problem; numerical bands cannot substitute for those
objects. Focused canonical/governance tests, generated queue and memory checks,
one integrated unchanged-boundary gate, and `git diff --check` close the task.

## Attempts and Continuation

Failed routes are append-only. Missing multi-soliton physics or an unimplemented
consumer is preserved as attempt evidence while the coefficient ledger,
source-oracle audit, terminal disposition, and corpus continuation proceed.

## Review and Promotion Plan

disposition will name durable evidence in `migration/dispositions.yaml` and
regenerate the queue. Without a surviving claim, release v0.74.0 and the
canonical package remain unchanged.

## Done Gate

P085 closes only when the positive coefficient/state/reaction/consumer
classification exists, every source predicate has an individual verdict, both
exact routes and mutations pass, affected consumers replay, campaign debt is
empty, and the parent migration can continue. Comparator agreement, a source
pass tally, or absence of a multi-soliton solution is not sufficient alone.
