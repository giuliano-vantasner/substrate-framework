## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

Use `E>0` for a declared relative barrier, `theta>0` for an independent
per-source scale in the same energy convention, `V` in `[0,1]` for the
declared coherence coordinate, `n>0` for a continuous population coordinate,
and `N` for a positive integer count only after an explicit integer map. The
ratio `x=E/theta` is positive and dimensionless. C-COH-001 already supplies
`Theta(n,V)=theta*n*(1+(n-1)*V)` and its unique positive continuous crossing;
C-RG-001 and C-RG-002 supply only the relative barrier.

The endpoint crossings are `n_inc=x` and `n_coh=sqrt(x)`. Alignment lowers the
endpoint threshold only for `x>1`, is neutral at one, and reverses for `x<1`.
A positive-integer threshold additionally requires strict monotonicity in the
population coordinate and a declared minimum-count convention; ceiling a
continuous root is not silently part of the source theorem.

A normalized classical breather energy, a physical classical breather energy,
`hbar` times an angular frequency, a thermal scale, and a population energy
remain distinct. Dimensional agreement is necessary but cannot identify them.

escape equation, material map, DBD breakdown criterion, nuclear reaction,
deposition law, or event observable may be imported by name.
