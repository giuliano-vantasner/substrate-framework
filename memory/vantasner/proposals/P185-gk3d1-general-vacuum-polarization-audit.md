## Corrected Formula Freeze

The source audit is complete at commit `669557a`. It retains the exact
integer-endpoint Clifford dimensions, conditional D=2 fermion coefficient,
fixed-four-component D=4 pole and mass slope, and subtracted logarithm route.
It rejects an imposed transverse tensor as a loop Ward derivation,
`2^floor(D/2)` as an analytic continuation, the phrase "regulator free", an
undeclared charged-Dirac sector, and any inference from the loop term to a
selected total kinetic normalization.

Before canonical code, P185 freezes the source tensor convention
`Pi_mn=(q^2*g_mn-q_m*q_n)*Pi2(q^2)` and the spacelike variable `Q=-q^2>0`.
For integration dimension `d`, independently declared integer spinor trace
`n_gamma`, charge magnitude `e`, mass square `M2>=0`, and
`Delta=M2+x*(1-x)*Q`, the conditional physical-dimension family is

`Pi2(-Q)=-2*n_gamma*e^2/(4*pi)^(d/2)*Gamma(2-d/2)
* integral_0^1 x*(1-x)*Delta^(d/2-2) dx`.

Here `e^2` has mass dimension `4-d`, so `Pi2` is dimensionless. The mixed
projector coefficient is `q^2*Pi2=-Q*Pi2` and has mass dimension two. No
single analytic `n_gamma(d)` is asserted. The D=2 endpoint declares
`d=2,n_gamma=2,M2=0` and gives `Pi2(-Q)=-e^2/(pi*Q)`, hence
`q^2*Pi2=e^2/pi` for `Q>0`; it is fermionic and does not repair or reproduce
the scalar massless limit in C-VAC-001.

For the regulated D=4 endpoint, P185 holds `n_gamma=4`, sets
`d=4-2*epsilon`, and multiplies the same master by `mu2^epsilon`. At zero
momentum its bare value is
`-e^2/(12*pi^2)*Gamma(epsilon)*(4*pi*mu2/M2)^epsilon`. Its Laurent residue is
`-e^2/(12*pi^2)`. Adding the declared MS-bar pole counterterm plus an
arbitrary finite local constant `c_fin` gives
`Pi2_MSbar(0)=e^2/(12*pi^2)*log(M2/mu2)+c_fin`. Thus the `log(M2)` slope is
`e^2/(12*pi^2)`, while `c_fin`, the bare coefficient, and the total matched
kinetic coefficient remain free.

For timelike `w=q^2/M2` strictly below the first branch point `w=4`, the
finite subtraction is
`Pi2(q^2)-Pi2(0)=e^2/(2*pi^2)*integral_0^1 x*(1-x)
*log(1-w*x*(1-x)) dx`. Its frozen series begins
`-e^2/(2*pi^2)*(w/30+w^2/280+w^3/1890+...)`, with radius four. Above the
threshold it requires the separately typed `-i0` boundary value and is not
returned by the real below-threshold API.

The Ward route is also frozen before code: with `A=S(p)`, `B=S(p+q)`, a
spectator vertex `G`, and the free inverse-propagator identity
`qslash=B^-1-A^-1`, exact trace cyclicity gives
`Tr[qslash*B*G*A]=Tr[G*A]-Tr[G*B]`. A shift-invariant regulator then cancels
the integrated difference. This derives the contraction from the loop
integrand; it does not infer it from a transverse ansatz.
