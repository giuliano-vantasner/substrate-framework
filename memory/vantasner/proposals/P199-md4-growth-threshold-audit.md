# P199 MD4 Growth Threshold Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P199 must deliver the strongest exact importable mechanism that can make
`n<S` a genuine local growth-drift statement with state space, generator,
boundary, time units, initial law, and rate scale explicit. It must distinguish
that process from C-CMB-003's static mass ratio and C-VOP-001's one-time number
measurement. Rejecting MD4's physical rescue narrative is evidence, not the
positive deliverable.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-CMB-003 is a mathematical distribution, not a time process. C-VOP-001 makes
it a Born law only for a declared state and number measurement, not for a
material event. MD1's count, MD2's variance, and every accepted medium or
lattice claim supply no occupation generator, participating set, or rate.
Detailed balance fixes adjacent rate ratios, not a unique generator or time
scale. P199 may introduce a separately declared nonnegative-integer Markov
chain, positive dimensionless `S`, and positive inverse-time `r`. Mutable
numeric probes use `numpy.trapezoid` or the shared helper.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection prioritizes typed state space and generator, exact boundary and time
units, normalization, positivity, detailed balance, PGF and moment closure,
mutation sensitivity, distinction among drift, mean, paths, and static weights,
dynamical nonuniqueness, novelty, and usefulness without physical overreach.
Numerical closeness selects nothing.

MD4's 34-check result was already exposed in P196 and reused later. No fresh
blinding is claimed. This contract freezes process candidates, imports,
criteria, mutations, and ceilings before renewed P199 execution, detailed
predicate audit, comparison-value inspection, or implementation.
