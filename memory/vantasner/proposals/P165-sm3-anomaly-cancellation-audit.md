# P165 SM3 Anomaly Cancellation Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P165 must reproduce and independently adjudicate SM3's local and global anomaly
claims for its declared left-handed carrier. The positive deliverable is a
reusable exact anomaly-coefficient ledger and complete solution-branch
classification, or an equally complete accepted composition, that terminally
qualifies SM3. Showing that one supplied table gives zero, or finding that its
uniqueness prose fails, does not finish the campaign.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-REP-003 types only a separately supplied finite multiplet table,
charge-conjugation convention, and normalization map. C-REP-001 proves the
homogeneous scaling ceiling. C-LIE-001 and C-REP-002 provide standard
fundamental trace normalizations but no quantum anomaly theorem or physical
matter sector. Local triangle anomalies, global SU2 parity, and the cubic SU3
representation anomaly remain distinct obligations.

Allowed inputs are those four claims, exact commutative algebra, and explicitly
approved external four-dimensional chiral-fermion anomaly criteria, Witten SU2
parity, and SU3 conjugate-representation cubic signs. SM3 and its dossier become
hash-pinned noncanonical evidence only after the freeze. Physical field labels,
generation completeness, Yukawa interactions, anomaly-theorem derivations,
renormalizability, unitarity, observed charges, and a Standard Model identity
are not silently imported.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted dependency closure, explicit external-theorem
provenance, complete solution-set coverage, separation of local polynomial and
global representation obligations, chirality and normalization consistency,
assumption economy, mutation sensitivity, API novelty, and downstream
compatibility. Familiar hypercharges and green zeroes cannot select the
concept. Prior source exposure is recorded and no fresh blinding is claimed.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy exact polynomial algebra fits the local anomaly coefficients, homogeneity,
factorization, ideals, substitutions, and branch classification. A canonical
route will use any accepted P165 API; a fresh independent route will eliminate
the five variables without importing it. A solution-completeness oracle must
cover both the nonzero-doublet branch and any zero-doublet vectorlike branch,
not just perturb the displayed point. Global SU2 parity and SU3 cubic balance
will be typed as imported representation criteria rather than consequences of
the Abelian polynomial system.

Mutations include one-row charge changes, uniform scaling, triplet-to-
antitriplet sign changes, a neutral singlet, removal of one supplied doublet,
row exchange, and the exact vectorlike branch. The source graph will record lexical sites,
runtime executions, and assertions separately. Compatibility preflight will
audit direct, imported, dynamic, and eager legacy NumPy access; mutable code
uses exact algebra or current APIs, while immutable version-only access would
receive an isolated alias backed by `np.trapezoid` without scientific demotion.
