# P149 W4 Missing-Energy Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P149 must deliver an exact importable classification of W4's incoming,
reflected, absorbed, boundary, and detector energy-momentum statements. The
positive object must distinguish a threshold identity from a constituent
partition and boundary flux ledger, and distinguish an unobserved channel from
a conserved missing four-vector or physical neutral particle. If every exact
object is already accepted, a complete qualified disposition rather than a
duplicate claim remains a positive framework result.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

of identities from dynamics and observability, accepted sine-Gordon

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy exact algebra is the primary oracle for threshold, stress-energy,
boundary-balance, two-body, and countermodel statements. Numerical execution
may reproduce a source sample or evaluate genuinely unresolved evolved boundary
data; it cannot strengthen an exact identity or select a candidate. A fresh
route will derive load-bearing flux and momentum signs without importing any
new campaign helper.

Mutations change the threshold complement, energy split, momentum sign, boost
factor, boundary normal, work term, constituent mass, charge label, or detector
acceptance. Countermodels hold total energy fixed while varying the absorbed
share, give the same invisible energy to boundary storage, radiation, or a
neutral outgoing state, and separate nonzero winding transfer from energy
visibility.

The compatibility preflight uses the shared AST auditor. Mutable code must use
exact algebra, `trapezoid_integral`, or `np.trapezoid` and contain no
executable legacy access. If W4's immutable source has removed `np.trapz`
access, its native diagnostic is preserved and an explicit alias-only binding
to `np.trapezoid` is used before scientific adjudication. A version-only
abort does not reject a candidate.
