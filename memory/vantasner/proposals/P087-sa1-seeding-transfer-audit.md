# P087 SA1 Seeding-Transfer Audit

## Question and Positive Deliverable

P087 must determine whether SA1 derives a dependency-closed physical transfer
from a deposition spectrum to seeded breathers and the engine's slew trigger,
or only exact breather temporal-Fourier facts plus a declared overlap
coordinate. The positive deliverable is an exact parity, Fourier,
susceptibility, drive-coupling, spectral-overlap, normalization, population,
consumer, and nonduplication classification, individual promotion of any
distinct theorem that survives, and a terminal SA1 disposition.

## Invariants, Conventions, and Allowed Imports

C-SG-001 supplies an exact field but no external drive or response function.
At fixed position, temporal parity and half-wave antisymmetry can constrain a
Fourier series; they cannot supply the interaction, input/output map,
retarded condition, causality, or normalization of a susceptibility. Zero DC
does not uniquely select differentiation with respect to voltage.

C-SG-002 supplies normalized breather energy, not an object-production
efficiency. A deposition overlap becomes absorbed energy or population only
under separate unit, positivity, coupling, efficiency, bandwidth, and counting
premises. Exact P087 work uses no numerical quadrature, `np.trapz`, or
`np.trapezoid`; sampled work must use the shared `trapezoid_integral` helper.

## Selection Criteria and Blinding

Selection is ordered by accepted closure of drive, interaction, observables,
causality, normalization, absorption, population counting, breakdown, slew
law, and consumer; exact parity, Fourier normalization and support, coefficient
signs, dimensions, limits, and mutation sensitivity; parameter economy; and
nonduplication. Source spectra, coefficient values, output, thresholds, and
consumer readings remain blinded until both contracts and the immutable freeze
validate. Numerical closeness or a plotted peak cannot select a mechanism.

## Proposed Claim Delta

No new claim is presumed. C-SG-001 already owns the exact field and period,
while C-SG-009/C-SG-010 show that harmonic statements attach to a declared
observable without acquiring response semantics. A new claim proceeds only if
Candidate H identifies distinct reusable temporal-Fourier or conditional-
overlap content, complete assumptions, mutation-sensitive verification, an
importable API, and a governed consumer. No accepted claim is challenged or
superseded.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

SymPy is the correct oracle for temporal parity, half-period transformation,
Fourier support, exact coefficient integrals or series, DC limits, overlap
scaling, dimensions, and counterfamilies. The primary route will pin and
reproduce SA1, audit every source equation and literal, trace all named
evidence and consumers, mutate parity, harmonic index, spectrum normalization,
coupling, energy denominator, and DC structure, and inspect numerical values
only after structural selection freezes. An independent route will rederive
the harmonic selection and overlap identifiability without importing P087
expressions.

Reusable package code and tests will be added only for a distinct theorem. A
numerical quadrature is regression evidence if exact symmetry already fixes a
coefficient to zero; any genuinely unresolved coefficient integral must state
precision, measure, convergence, error norm, and independent cross-check. A
physical response requires a driven dynamical equation rather than another
Fourier transform of the same field. Focused canonical and governance tests,
queue and memory checks, one integrated unchanged-boundary gate, and `git
diff --check` close the task.
